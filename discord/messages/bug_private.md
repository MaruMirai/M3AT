**How to submit a bug report**

Each report is a private thread that only you and I can see.

1. Click the **Threads** icon at the top of this channel (the speech bubble with a #), then **Create**.
2. Switch on **Private Thread**, give it a short title that says what goes wrong (for example "Pawns float above beds"), and create it.
3. In the thread, type **@Maru** so I'm added and get notified.
4. In the game, **select the pawn** the problem happens to (you can select several), open **Options → Mod options → M3AT** and press **Copy bug report** (under Problems). **Paste** it into the thread (Ctrl+V), or attach `bug_report.txt` from the M3AT folder inside RimWorld's config folder if it's too long.
5. Say what you expected to happen and what happened instead.
6. Add a **screenshot or short video** if you can. These help a lot.

The report includes your M3AT settings, your mod list, your graphics card, any M3AT errors in the log and what the selected pawns are doing. Your home folder's name is removed from it, and nothing is sent anywhere unless you post it yourself.

One thread per problem, please. Check the known issues in #welcome first.
