One post per problem, please. Give it a short title that says what goes wrong (for example "Pawns float above beds" or "Error when a baby is carried").

In your post, include:
1. **The bug report from the mod.** Select the pawn it's happening to, open the M3AT mod options and press **Copy bug report**, then paste it here. It has your settings, mod list, errors and what that pawn is doing. If it's too long for a message, attach the file instead: it's saved as `bug_report.txt` in the M3AT folder of RimWorld's config folder.
2. **What you expected to happen, and what happened instead.**
3. **A screenshot or short video**, if it's something you can see. These help a lot.

Check the known issues in #welcome first. Add tags to your post to help sort it.
