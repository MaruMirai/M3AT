**How to report a bug**

1. In the game, **select the pawn** the problem happens to. You can select several.
2. Open **Options → Mod options → M3AT** and press **Copy bug report** (under Problems).
3. Make a new post in this channel and **paste** it (Ctrl+V). It's also saved as `bug_report.txt` in the M3AT folder inside RimWorld's config folder, if you'd rather attach the file.
4. Say what you expected to happen and what happened instead.
5. Add a **screenshot or short video** if you can.

The report includes your M3AT settings, your mod list, your graphics card, any M3AT errors in the log and what the selected pawns are doing. Your home folder's name is removed from it, and nothing is sent anywhere unless you post it yourself.

Please keep this thread for questions about reporting. Make a new post for each bug.
