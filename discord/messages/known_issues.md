## Known issues

These are already known, so there's no need to report them. They'll be worked on over time.

- **Modded clothes** don't have their own models yet. M3AT puts together the closest match from its existing garments (by what it covers, its layer, armour and tech level), in the right colour.
- **Alien races and modded gene parts** will most likely just show as ordinary humans for now.
- **Hairstyles are built from your hair textures** the first time a pawn wears one. The pawn is bald for a moment while this happens. After that it's saved and loads straight away.
- **Statues** are still drawn by IsoRim in its own style.
- **Some animations are still basic** and will be improved.

Found something that isn't on this list? Make a post in #issues-and-bugs.
