# Apparel, stand-ins and worn gear

How worn apparel becomes garments on the skinned figure. Making garment meshes: `dev_notes/garment_scripts.md` and `dev_notes/clips.md` (the export). Hiding under garments, hats over hair, visors: `dev_notes/skinning.md`.

## Which garment is drawn

- Each garment is drawn for the worn apparel named in its `defs` (`GarmentOf`). Children's versions borrow the grown-up one's (`AdultOf`: Biotech's kid shirt, pants, tribalwear, parka and helmet (the simple helmet's), Odyssey's children's vacsuit, CE's kid backpack). Every armour and helmet variant has its own garment (prestige marine/recon/cataphract armour and helmets, grenadier, phoenix, locust, mechlord suit, mechanitor helmets, `VacsuitHelmet`).
- Otherwise it is a **stand-in** for apparel with no garment of its own (`Source/StandIns.cs`, `StandInsFor`, cached per def), drawn in IsoRim's apparel colour. The player's choice in the clothing tool comes first (below).
- Not drawn at all: headgear, masks and other head/face/neck apparel with no garment and no stand-in; apparel IsoRim has a worn gear model for (`GearMountOf`) is drawn as that model (Worn gear, below).
- The shoes (`Shoes`, `GARMENT_PROPS` `with_legwear` and `colour`) are also worn, in that colour, once worn apparel covers both torso and legs (one piece or two), going with the first apparel covering the legs (`WalkFigures.WornGarments`); left off once other footwear is drawn (`WornGarmentsNow`'s `footwear`), on a prosthetic leg (`dev_notes/body.md`), and with the `shoes` option off (`WalkFigures.Shoes`).

## Stand-in scoring (`StandIns.cs`; **`anim_scripts/stand_ins.py` is the same scoring over the defs on disk: change the two together**)

- Pool: every garment that may stand in (not a held one, the shoes or the baby romper). A garment in `NoStandIn` only stands in for apparel with all its name words (`namedOnly`), and such a match is the thing itself whatever the groups say (coverage at least `NamedCover`, plus `NamedBonus`). One over the skull (UpperHead, not a mask) only for apparel over it.
- A garment is described by the first of its `defs` the game has; its `layer`/`groups` lines add layers and groups (or describe it alone; a `layer` line may name several, space separated); its kind (tech level, stuff, armour, noble) from the def its `like` line names (`GARMENT_PROPS` `like`); its name words from its own name.
- Score: body part group overlap (`GroupWeight`; FullHead counted as its parts) × the layers' closeness (one in common, else `OtherLayerStep` a body layer apart, `OtherHeadLayer` between head layers, none across or for a belt, backpack or a mod's own layer) × armour or not (`NotArmour`), plus small amounts for vacsuit, noble wear, tech level (`SameTech`, `TechStep`; counts most), stuff, the garment's name words in the apparel's name or label (`NameWords`; `HeadNounBonus` more when the label's head noun (`HeadNouns`: last word, or the word before the first "of") says it is one), `NameAlike`/`NameNot` (e.g. hotpants are the short shorts, any exoskeleton CE's exoframe), and the plain garments (`Generic`). The kid romper scores only `ChildOnlyShare`; a narrower kind of a plain garment (`Narrow`: crop top, tank top, shorts, short shorts) only `NarrowShare` without its name words. Every two garments on different main groups are scored as a pair (`PairScore`: joined coverage at the farther layer, kinds averaged, less `PairCost`), so a one-piece suit is the collared shirt and trousers. Best single or pair from `StandInLeast`.
- `stands_for` lines (`export_frames.py`'s `STANDS_FOR`, `StandsFor`): apparel named there gets every garment naming it, whatever the scores, even on the Belt layer, for what the defs can't tell (Kiiro's bra and briefs; VWE Heavy Weapons' `VWE_Apparel_Exoframe`; GrimWorld 40,000's headgear by their sprites, gorgets, and hand-held shields as CE's melee or ballistic shield, held and carried as CE's are, `WalkFigures.HeldGarmentOf`).
- Never stood in: belt-layer, shield and energy-shield apparel (`NeverStoodIn`; the belt or a shield rules out only apparel on no body layer, as GrimWorld's Terminator and Custodes armour have them), jewellery named a ring, psyring, bracelet or anklet.
- Matched as what they are, before scoring (`AsWhatItIs`): webbing or chest rigs (`WebbingWords`) as CE's tactical vest; packs (worn only on a backpack layer, or with the belt (a jump pack), or named a backpack, not a cape or cloak, `NotPackWords`) as `PackLayer` over `PackGroup`, so any mod's pack takes CE's backpack (or its radio pack for a vox or comms pack), but a Spacer-or-later pack over neck and shoulders takes none (`ArmourPack`, power armour's own); apparel only on a shoulder layer (`GW_Shoulder`) as `ShoulderLayer` (only `Pauldrons`); apparel only on `EyeCover` as over the eyes alone. Bags, satchels, rucksacks (`BagWords`) are packs. CE's packs and vest have `layer`/`groups` lines so they stand in without CE.
- Words: the defName is matched as words (`WordsOf`); the top and ladies' hats also need "hat"; headgear labelled a hood, cap, hat, beret or bandana (`HatNouns`) is matched as a hat even if armour; one named a helmet or helm (`HelmetWords`) never as a hat.
- `Hints` (`HINTS` in `stand_ins.py`): words in an apparel's name or label that say it is a garment's kind without its name words (headsets, sun hats, tiaras, lab gowns, winter coats, hairbands, ribbons, maid caps, nun veils (the broadwrap), stockings, tights, leotards, swimsuits, messenger bags, skirts and dresses...), less any `HintNot` phrase, let the garment stand in even if `NoStandIn` or a mask, coverage at least `HintCover` (`HintCoverHead` for headgear, one garment), `HintBonus` ahead (less than `NamedBonus`). Made for the race mods' clothes.
- Open fronts (`Source/OpenFronts.cs`): the `Duster` and `LabCoat` (`OpenFronts`) stand in only for apparel whose front sprite is open down the middle (`FrontFits`): `OpenFrontShare` (read once per def from `wornGraphicPath` `_<body>_south`, `OpenBodies`, by `Api.ReadSpritePixels` at `OpenSize`) is how far up from the hem the clear gap at the middle column (`OpenHalfWidth`) reaches over the garment's height; open from `OpenFrontLeast` (0.45; closed outfits read at most 0.22). `stand_ins.py`: `OPEN_FRONTS`, `open_front_share`, sprites from `texture_index` or Assets.zip.
- `pads` lines (`GARMENT_PROPS` `pads`; `SkinnedFigure.HasPads`, `WalkFigures.ArmourPads`): the marine and cataphract armour and variants also draw `Pauldrons`, in the armour's colour unless a shoulder apparel has its own; `PauldronsBig` (its `Big` key) in its place in Space Marine or Custodes armour (`BulkOf`'s `Marine`, `marine_of` line, `SkinnedFigure.MarineVersionOf`, `WalkFigures.MarinePads`).
- `stand_ins.py` reads each Workshop mod's folders as the game loads them for 1.7 (`mod_folders`: its `LoadFolders.xml`, else its root, `Common` and newest version folder). `--loo` scores the game's own clothes as if they had none. `standins` autotest notes what every loaded apparel gets in the game.

## Skirt and dress lengths (`Source/HemLengths.cs`)

- Whichever skirt or dress the scoring picks (`SkinnedFigure.StandInsOn`, from `WornGarmentsNow`) is swapped for the garment of its kind (`HemKinds`: a name starting Skirt or Dress with a `hem_share` line, `Piece.hemShare`) whose hem is nearest how far the apparel's front sprite comes down the pawn's body sprite (`HemShareOn`: `BodyGraphic`, the race's under HAR, with `ApparelGraphicRecordGetter`'s art; 0 top, 1 feet; crotch ~0.62, knee ~0.81; a legless body by its path, `LeglessBodies`/`LeglessFeet`), less `HemShorter` (0.09), cached per pair of textures, unless the player chose in the clothing tool. The hem is the picture's lowest row, so drawn stockings read as long.
- A dress is sleeveless when its apparel doesn't cover Arms and its art stands out past the body's at the shoulders (`SleeveBand`) by less than `SleevelessBelow` (`SleeveOverhangOn`).
- Art with legwear drawn in (Milira's bodycon dress over stockings to the feet) can't show its hem by its outline: when the apparel's sprite follows the body's outline (each edge within `HugSlack` px) up from its bottom for more than `HugRun` of the body's height (`LegwearDrawnIn`; that dress 57 rows of 76, every other race dress 7 at most), the scoring's length is kept, the sleeves still by the art. Looked into and passed over: the hem where the art stands out past the body (read Wolfein's narrow nun habit as a mini skirt) and the colour across the legs (fragile).
- The body picture exists only once the game resolves the render tree, so an unread pawn's garments are worked out again on its next draws (`HemUnread`, `WornCache.hemTries`, at most `HemTriesMost`). The bug report gives the hem; the clothing tool's Automatic shows the length (`AutoStandInsOn`).
- A skirt goes over what is worn with it on the shirt layer or under (a shirt tucked in; `SkinnedFigure.PieceGoesOver` in `OverOf`), also between two garments drawn for one apparel; only an outer layer goes over it.
- `hemlengths` autotest (with `M3AT_ADD_MODS="erdelf.humanoidalienraces ancot.ancotlibrary ancot.kiirorace ancot.milirarace melondove.wolfeinrace solaris.ratkinracemod"`): notes each race skirt's and dress's hem and garment (`SkinnedFigure.HemDiagnosis`, `HidingReport`), checks and pictures them.

## Layers and underwear

- Each apparel layer counts as a body layer (`SkinnedFigure.BodyLayerOf`, cached): the game's as they are; head layers (`HeadLayers`: Overhead, OnHead, StrappedHead, EyeCover) always themselves; a mod's by name or label ("under" → `Underwear`, a word of `BodyLayerWords` → that body layer, e.g. Kiiro's `Kiiro_Pants` → under `OnSkin`), else if drawn under `OnSkin` `Underwear`, else itself. For stand-ins every underwear layer is one body layer `Underwear`, a step under `OnSkin` (`UnderLayer`; `stand_ins.py`'s `under`).
- Underwear (`SkinnedFigure.UnderSkin`: UnderWhere's `Underwear`/`UnderwearTop`, Kiiro's `Kiiro_Underwear`, the like) goes under everything by draw order (`GoesOver`), stays on in bed with `NoEquipment` (`KeptOnInBed`), is put on with `dress_over`/`dress_legs` (`DressKindsOf`, `OnTheSkin`), carries wounds' blood (`SkinnedWounds`), and doesn't count toward the shoes (`IsUnderwear`).
- Garments: `Underwear` (briefs), `Shorts`, `Tanktop`, `Bra`, `Loincloth` (own flap weights, `part_weights`), `Chestwrap` (bandeau), `CropTop`, `ShortShorts`, `LongJohns` (waffle-knit union suit, `paint_long_johns.py`). Layers, groups and `like` set in `export_frames.py`'s loop; `STANDS_FOR` names Kiiro's sets. They sit at the shirt layer's offset, well inside `GarmentInside` (0.04 cells).
- `underwear` autotest (UnderWhere; Kiiro's with Kiiro): each set alone and under clothes, checking each garment (`SameGarments`).

## Clothing tool (`Source/ClothingTool.cs`)

- The player picks the garments drawn for apparel with no garment of its own, per defName (`Settings.clothingChoices`: names joined by commas, `WalkFigures.NoneDrawn` ("-") for none; not touched by "Restore all defaults", cleared by its own button). `StandInsFor` reads it first (`WalkFigures.ChosenStandIns`; unknown names ignored); `AutoStandInsFor` is the scoring's own. A change clears `wornCaches` and re-bakes ground apparel (`ClothingChoicesChanged`).
- The "M3AT tools" toggle among the play settings (`gripToolButton`/`gripTool`) gives a humanlike wearing such apparel (`HasStandInApparel`) an Adjust clothing button (`ClothingToolWindow`): Change (automatic, none, or any of `ChoosableGarments`, labelled by `GarmentLabel`), Add another, Automatic. With `spriteClothes`, a Paint box per apparel whose garments take its art (`PaintsArt`); unticked, its defName goes in `Settings.plainClothes` (`WalkFigures.PaintedArt`). The bug report marks these "(player's choice)".
- `plainclothes` autotest: the first loaded apparel (another mod's first) whose stand-in takes its art, painted, plain and painted again (`ClothPaintedDraws`).

## Stand-ins painted with their art (`Source/SpriteClothes.cs`; `spriteClothes`, on; a prototype)

A garment drawn for apparel with no garment of its own (`PaintsArt`: has UVs and an image; not a hat riding one bone, head-layer apparel, held, a pack, the shoes, the baby romper, pajamas or a corpse's) gets an image of its own per garment, apparel material and body picture (`ClothImageOf`, at most `ClothBakesPerFrame` a frame, `ClothMost` a session; plain until made): each texel (placed at rest, `TexelsOf`) coloured from the apparel's four worn sprites as the game colours them (`ViewOf`: ink taken out, colours grown, `GrownArt`), weighted by facing and alpha. The sprites are laid on the chibi by the pawn's body sprite: rows by landmarks (`ClothShare`: neck top, shoulder line and waist from `BodyMarks` (`ClothWaist` for the waistless male body) onto the chibi's shoulders and the skirts' waist ring (`RigWaist`), then `HEM_ANCHORS`' crotch, knee, ankle), across by the body sprite's width onto the chibi's trunk and legs at that height (`ClothBody`: cut through the surface per band and averaged over neighbours). The garment's own image is multiplied back about its median as detail. `Draw`'s `cloth` takes it (`textureMarkAt`). AUTOTEST writes images to `<config>/M3AT/cloth_debug/` (`DumpCloth`); `raceclothes` pictures each row plain and painted.

## Other mods' garments and gene parts (`Source/ModGarments.cs`)

- Modder guides (shipped): `Docs/Apparel.md`, `Docs/GeneParts.md`, the Blender add-on `Docs/Tools/m3at_garment_kit.py`, `Docs/Examples/`.
- Every `.txt` in an `M3ATgarments` folder at another active mod's root, then in `<config>/M3AT/M3ATgarments`, is read after this mod's garments (`AddModGarments`, from `Load` before `pieces` is set; a loaded name is skipped; its defs win over ours).
- The file is the export's garment form, but body-dependent parts are fitted here as `write_garment` fits them (`FitToBody`): weights (`FitWeights`; `bone` line whole on that bone; own `w` lines only with `own_weights yes` and a `bones` line, `ReadBoneNames`), every body key (`FitKeys`: blended from the body, `rigid` whole, `hard` (default with `bone`, `hardLine`) by bone middles tied to body vertices, `BoneAnchors`, not Shrinkhead; then `Hung`, `PushedOut`, `Filled`; own key lines for `own_keys`), hidden body triangles (`BodyUnder`: the exporter's `hidden_under` with angle-weighted normals and edge middles; `hides no`; `hide_bones`), winding turned to the body's if most faces in (`Orient`). A `body` line equal to this skin.txt's hash keeps the file's own w, key and hide lines.
- `base <Name>`: a retexture of our garment Name (`Retextured`). `genes <defNames>`: a gene part (`geneParts`, `GenePartsOf`), never worn: `DrawDressed` adds its bits to the `HideUnder` mask and draws it after the fur (`HideGenePart`: triangles under worn garments left out, `GarmentTris`, and `Tucked`) in the skin's, hair's or own colour (`colour_from`, `GeneColourFrom`).
- Worn-garment masks are 256 bits (`Source/GarmentMask.cs`, `MaxHiding` 254).
- `anim_scripts/garmentfit/run.sh [--compare] [--write folder] <garment.txt> ...`: the fitting outside the game. `--compare` fits our exported garments from shape alone against the exporter's (weights the same, keys within ~0.01 cells, ~90% of hidden triangles); `--write` writes fitted files with this body's `body` line.

## Content packs' garments (`SkinnedFigure.GarmentOf` → `Wanted`)

- A pack's garment for apparel that has one of ours too used to win silently; ours is kept in `ownGarmentOf` and drawn when the pack's isn't wanted.
- `PackGarmentWanted`: not if its mod is off for a benchmark turn; else the player's per-item pick in `clothingChoices` (the pack garment's name: the pack's; anything else, or `M3atAutomatic` "*", which names no garment so `ChosenStandIns` falls back to the automatic match: M3AT's); else the pack's Clothing source and not `ContentSources.ArmourByDefault` (sharp armour at least `ArmourSharp` 0.8 of its default stuff, or a helmet word in its label or defName: the packs' helmets came out as blobs over the face and their power armour with bare arms).
- M3AT look and a pack's Items list show it (`PackItemRow`): the pack's garment, M3AT's (its own garment or the stand-ins), none, or any garment; Paint (opt-in, `Settings.paintedPackClothes`, `PaintedArtFor`; `PaintsArt` now also takes a pack's garment and has a def overload) paints the pack's garment with the apparel's sprites, for the colours the packs got wrong.

## Triangle budget (`Source/GarmentBudget.cs`; `fullDetailGarments`, off)

- Another mod's garment (`Piece.owner`, its packageId: from its `M3ATgarments` folder in `ReadModGarment`, before `FitToBody`; or a file another mod put on skin.txt's garment list, found by path in `Load`, `ModOfPath`, as the Miho pack does) over `ClothesBudget` (2500) or, worn on the head (`OnHead`: `OnHeadShare` of its vertices above `NeckHeight`), `HeadBudget` (1500) is cut down to it as it loads (`KeepWithinBudget`, `Cut`: `Decimate.Run` per kind of triangle, plain, glass and cut out, so each kind stays one; every vertex kept keeps its weights, keys, texture place, normal). Logged, listed in `Simplified` and the bug report's Add-ons section. `fullDetailGarments` (Mod compatibility; read as the chibi loads) keeps them, to `Ceiling` (30000) (`OverBudget`). AUTOTEST: `<config>/M3AT/full_detail_garments` turns it on.
- Why (Nov 2026, `addoncost`, 24 pawns in the Miho and Wolfein packs' outfits, 1600x900): our garments are 230 triangles at the median, 1494 at most; the Miho pack's helmets 17,000-20,000. As made, the two packs cost 11.0 ms a frame (Miho 8.7, Wolfein 3.2); simplified, 1.1 ms. Simplifying the packs' 130 over-budget garments took 2 s at load (140 ms at most each), at the first draw.
- The garment kit (`Docs/Tools/m3at_garment_kit.py`, `TRIANGLES_MOST`/`TRIANGLES_HEAD`) and `Docs/Apparel.md` give the same budgets.

## Backpacks

A garment of `walk.HOLSTER_PACKS` is drawn by `SkinnedFigure.Packed`, not skinned: its base mesh, no body keys, whole on the `pack` gear slot's bone (`PackRigOf`: its back face's middle, measured over its inner `PackFace`, and the slot's way out), pushed past clothes and body by `Clearance` over that face, scaled by `Posed.packScale` (`WalkFigures.PackScaleOf`: as big on every pawn; CE's kid backpack is the grown one's, `BorrowsPack`, at `KidPackSize`). `Wearing`, from `DrawDressed`, sets this up first. A pack hides nothing (`SkinnedFigure.NoHideMask`, with held shields; left out of `HiddenBy`, the fur's and `OverOf`), as its exported `hide` is at its rest place.

## Webbing let out (`Source/LetOut.cs`)

A garment for apparel on a let-out layer (`LetOutLayers`: CE's `Webbing`) or in `LetOutDefs` (the slave collar, scarf, gorgets) is made close on the skin, but the game puts it over armour standing up to ~0.11 cells off. Worn over other garments (`LetOutUnder`: those it `GoesOver`, not packs, by `PieceApparel`), its rest pose is pushed out past them (`GarmentShape`, cached in `Shape.letOut`, ~50 ms each): each place along the body's normal under it (`BodyWay`), averaged over `LetOutTurn` (`Averaged`, `Falloff`), to `LetOutClear` over the outermost surface on that line (`SurfaceTop`), sampled across triangles (`LetOutSamples`), held over `LetOutHold`, tapering at `LetOutSlope`, averaged over `LetOutBlend`, never below a place's need. `Pose` and the holsters' `Clearance`/`SideSurface` use that shape, and `UnderOf` tests garments under it against it (`LetOutOver`). The webbing of `NoClearanceDefs` is left out of `Clearance` (`UnclearMask`; `dev_notes/weapons.md`). `dmsvest` autotest (`M3AT_ADD_MODS="aoba.framework=<Fortified-Framework clone> aoba.deadmanswitch.core"`): CE's tactical vest over The Dead Man's Switch's apparel, with every let-out (`SkinnedFigure.LetOutNotes`).

## Worn gear (`Source/WornGears.cs`; `WalkFigures.WornGears`, on)

- IsoRim's packs, belts and waist tools (`WornGearDef`s, `GearDefOf`, `GearModelOf`, tinted as `WornGear.ModelFor`) are drawn by `DrawWornGear` after the holsters, not lying, crawling or swimming. Apparel with a garment of its own gets none.
- `SkinnedFigure.GearAt` carries each from IsoRim's figure: the model's reference point (`IsoRimWaist`, 0.30 up; a pack's back-plane middle; a hip tool's clip `GearBeltWidth` out) to its slot, scaled by the slot's `scale`; a belt a ring stretched to the slot's half width over `GearBeltWidth` and `depth` front to back.
- Three slots, one per IsoRim mount (`walk.GEAR`, anim.txt's `gear` lines, `ReadGear`/`GearOf`): `pack` on `spine.002`, pushed out by `Clearance` over the back plane (`PackSpots`) and wholly past a worn backpack (`GearPackPlace`), same size on every pawn; `belt`, by `BeltPushes` (out of each side, front and back, trunk and legs only, `WaistOf`, as the hanging arms were taken for the waist); `hip`, a tool's clip at the right side (the hips' bone, like the belt; the hip gear's held clips: `dev_notes/weapons.md`).
- Edited in the preview (`draw_gear`, `gr_<mount>_on`, `MaruGearGrip`: G moves, R sets `turn`; `write_gear` writes `GEAR`), then exported. **`anim_scripts/holster_push.py` copies this placing: change it with `WornGears.cs` and `Holsters.cs`.**

## Shields (`Source/Shields.cs`)

- A garment with the `held` line (`SkinnedFigure.IsHeld`: CE's melee and ballistic shields) is in the left fist while CE would draw it (`ShieldShows`: drafted, or a job/duty with `alwaysShowWeapon`, as `WeaponShows`). With a `held_at` line (`CarriedOnBack`) it is on the back otherwise, as placed in its .glb: whole on the pack slot's bone (`Shield`, `ShieldPlace`, `PlaceWhole`, CPU-posed with its visor, `ShieldVisor`), pushed past everything there (`ShieldPush`: `Clearance` keyed `ShieldKey`, packs by `PackOuter`, back weapons by `ShieldOverWeapons` (every weapon the pawn has, slots from a `DrawHolstered` pass that draws nothing), each `GearGap` clear). `DrawDressed` tells the figure after `Wearing` (`SkinnedFigure.Shielding`, bumping `posedAt`). Without `held_at` it is worn only while shown, on `hand.L`.
- Drawn by `shield_draw_<garment>` (`walk.shield_draw_at`: the left hand round to the shield's left edge, `walk.shield_rim`, by `SHIELD_GRAB`, then into the guard; `SHIELD_DRAW_SECONDS`, `SHIELD_DRAW_UPPER`), stepped by game time by `StepShield` (`Ease.shieldOut`, backwards to put away; at once unless on its feet, not carrying, swimming or in the air), laid on with `Top` (`LayShieldDraw`); the guard waits. `ShieldPlace` keeps it on the back until the grab, then onto the hand as the clip holds it (`held_grip`, `walk.shield_grip_rest`) over `SHIELD_SLIDE`, then turned to `held_at`, blended about `ShieldMiddle` (anim.txt's `shield_timing`; `held_rim` for the preview's `Shield: taking <garment> off the back`).
- Hides nothing and is in no clearance (`SkinnedFigure.HeldMask`, out of `Clearance`, `SideSurface`, `NeckFit` beside `PackMask`); left to IsoRim on the ground.
- Up (`ShieldUp`: shown, on its feet, not carrying, swimming or in the air), the `shield` guard is laid on with `Top` (`ShieldEase`) over the drafted stance, aim and blows, giving way to hip gear, reload, draw, cast, stagger and daze; hip gear stays in its slot. A two-handed weapon loses its support hand. A one-handed weapon (handgun, `melee_1h`, grenade) or bare hands take `drafted_shield_<defName>`, else `drafted_shield_<kind>` (`WalkFigures.ShieldClip`; `DRAFTED` entries `shield_pistol`/`shield_melee`/`shield_unarmed` with `'shield': True`; per weapon by `walk.shielded_entry` for `walk.shielded_names`, the preview's `Shield weapon` menu).
- `holster_push.py` mirrors the placing (`shielded`, `shield_push`, `shield_over`); the preview's Worn panel shows it on the back, or in the fist on `SHIELD_HELD` clips.

## Helmets when undrafted (`Source/HelmetStow.cs`; `helmetStow`: `Worn` default, `OnBack`, `Hidden`, `AlwaysHidden`)

- Drawing only. For a draftable colonist (`StowableHelmet`) in an armour helmet (`SkinnedFigure.IsHelmet`, ArmorHeadgear; Biotech's kid helmet as its grown one, `AdultDefOf`), `Ease.helmOut` (1 on, 0 off, the phase of `dress_head`) goes toward `WantsHelmet` (drafted) by `StepHelm`; below `HatLands` it is left out of what is worn (`WithoutStowedHelmet`, in `DressedIn`). `Hidden` (or no garment or clip) snaps. `AlwaysHidden` never draws it.
- `OnBack`: `dress_head` backwards to take off, forwards to put on, `HelmHurry` times as fast, with `Top` (`LayHelm`, `HelmEdgeIn`/`Out`), on its feet with nothing else in hand (else, or aiming or striking, at once); downed or dead it stays; just put on by Wear it starts on the head. On before the weapon is drawn (`HelmetFirst`), off once it is away.
- Off the head, `DrawStowedHelmet` (after the holsters, `DressModelOf`) puts it on the back (`SkinnedFigure.HelmetOnBack`) by kind (`FullHelmet`: covers `FullHead`, upright facing the side; else open, its opening to the back, top out, front up) at `walk.HELMET_BACK` (anim.txt's `helmet_back open|full`, `HelmPlace`: bone, middle at rest on the bare body, share of size, turn; preview's Helmet on the back box, `hb_<kind>_`, `MaruHelmetGrip`), scaled `HelmetBackScale`, pushed past clothes (`Clearance`), packs (`PackOuter`), a back shield (`ShieldOuter`) and back weapons (`BackWeaponsOuter`), each `GearGap` clear, then sunk `HelmSink` (0.025 model units), slid to the right hand over the clip's first `HatHeld` round the shoulder (`HelmSwingOut`/`Up`), then placed as the hat is (`HatPlace`). Not drawn lying or crawling.
- `helmetstow` autotest pictures it; `helmetback` every helmet loaded on the back.

## Dressing (`Source/Dressing.cs`)

- Over the Wear job's wait (its `MakeNewToils` toil: `EquipDelay` plus what it replaces, `JobDriver_Wear.duration`) and RemoveApparel's, `DressWorkOf` makes a `dress_<kind>` clip the pawn's work kind (`WorkOf`, whatever IsoRim's setting: in place of the idle, weapon put away first, facing the apparel), played by `DressPhase` from the toil's ticks.
- `DressKindsOf` (cached per def): Belt layer `belt`; torso and legs on `OnSkin`/`Middle` (a suit) `legs` then the torso's; torso `over` for `OnSkin` without `Shell`, else `coat`; legs `legs`; head, face or neck `head`; else `belt`. Taking off is the clips backwards in reverse; Wear first takes off what it replaces (`removeTicks`). Squeezed into a short wait; in a long one its `dress_loop` keeps its pace and the rest is slowed (`Stretched`).
- `DrawDressItem`: the garment as a model at rest in the pawn's shape (`SkinnedFigure.AtRest`, `DressModelOf`). A hat is held by its front brim in the right hand from `HatHeld`, opening to the face (`HatPitch`, `HatRoll`), let go at `HatRelease` and arced (`HatArc`) onto the head by `HatLands` (the arms can't reach the chibi's head top). ## Apparel on the ground (`Source/GroundClothes.cs`; `groundGarments`, off)

A ground model rule (`GroundRule`, `Api.AddGroundModelRule`; `WornLook`: when IsoRim would lay it as its figure's clothes) bakes apparel with a garment (own or stand-in, `GroundGarmentOf`) as that garment: the idle's first frame of the Male shape at the options' head shrink (child-only apparel on the base body at `ChildClothesAge`), laid as `GroundGear` lays clothes (`SkinnedFigure.Laid`: on its back, collar north, squashed `GroundFlatten`; head/neck garments stand on their rim, front south), fit to `GroundFoot`, turned as the sprite, made a model with `Api.CreateModel`, tinted where `Tinted`.
