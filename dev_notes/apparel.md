# Apparel, stand-ins and worn gear

How worn apparel becomes garments on the skinned figure. Making garment meshes: `dev_notes/garment_scripts.md` and `dev_notes/clips.md` (the export). Hiding under garments, hats over hair, visors: `dev_notes/skinning.md`.

## Which garment is drawn

- Each garment is drawn for the worn apparel named in its `defs` (`GarmentOf`). Children's versions borrow the grown-up one's (`AdultOf`: Biotech's kid shirt, pants, tribalwear, parka and helmet (the simple helmet's), Odyssey's children's vacsuit, CE's kid backpack). Every armour and helmet variant has its own garment (prestige marine/recon/cataphract armour and helmets, grenadier, phoenix, locust, mechlord suit, mechanitor helmets, `VacsuitHelmet`).
- Otherwise it is a **stand-in** for apparel with no garment of its own (`Source/StandIns.cs`, `StandInsFor`, cached per def), drawn in IsoRim's apparel colour. The player's choice in the clothing tool comes first (below).
- Not drawn at all: headgear, masks and other head/face/neck apparel with no garment and no stand-in; apparel IsoRim has a worn gear model for (`GearMountOf`) is drawn as that model (Worn gear, below).
- The shoes (`Shoes`, `GARMENT_PROPS` `with_legwear` and `colour`) are also worn, in that colour, once worn apparel covers both torso and legs (one piece or two), going with the first apparel covering the legs (`WalkFigures.WornGarments`); left off once other footwear is drawn (`WornGarmentsNow`'s `footwear`), on a prosthetic leg (`dev_notes/body.md`), and with the `shoes` option off (`WalkFigures.Shoes`).

## Stand-in scoring (`StandIns.cs`; **`anim_scripts/stand_ins.py` is the same scoring over the defs on disk: change the two together**)

- Pool: every garment that may stand in (not a held one, the shoes or the baby romper). A garment in `NoStandIn` only stands in for apparel with all its name words (`namedOnly`), and such a match is the thing itself whatever the groups say (coverage at least `NamedCover`, plus `NamedBonus`). One over the skull (UpperHead, not a mask) only for apparel over it.
- A garment is described by the first of its `defs` the game has; its `layer`/`groups` lines add layers and groups (or describe it alone; a `layer` line may name several, space separated); its kind (tech level, stuff, armour, noble) from the def its `like` line names (`GARMENT_PROPS` `like`); its name words from its own name.
- Score: body part group overlap (`GroupWeight`; FullHead counted as its parts) × the layers' closeness (one in common, else `OtherLayerStep` a body layer apart, `OtherHeadLayer` between head layers, none across or for a belt, backpack or a mod's own layer) × armour or not (`NotArmour`), plus small amounts for vacsuit, noble wear, tech level (`SameTech`, `TechStep`; counts most), stuff, the garment's name words in the apparel's name or label (`NameWords`; `HeadNounBonus` more when the label's head noun (`HeadNouns`: last word, or the word before the first "of") says it is one), `NameAlike`/`NameNot` (e.g. hotpants are the short shorts, any exoskeleton CE's exoframe), and the plain garments (`Generic`). The kid romper scores only `ChildOnlyShare`; a narrower kind of a plain garment (`Narrow`: crop top, tank top, shorts, short shorts) only `NarrowShare` without its name words. Every two garments on different main groups are scored as a pair (`PairScore`: joined coverage at the farther layer, kinds averaged, less `PairCost`), so a one-piece suit is the collared shirt and trousers. Best single or pair from `StandInLeast`.
- `stands_for` lines (`export_frames.py`'s `STANDS_FOR`, `StandsFor`): apparel named there gets every garment naming it, whatever the scores, even on the Belt layer, for what the defs can't tell (Kiiro's bra and briefs; VWE Heavy Weapons' `VWE_Apparel_Exoframe`; GrimWorld 40,000's headgear by their sprites, gorgets, and hand-held shields as CE's melee or ballistic shield, held and carried as CE's are, `WalkFigures.HeldGarmentOf`).
- Never stood in: belt-layer, shield and energy-shield apparel (`NeverStoodIn`; the belt or a shield rules out only apparel on no body layer, as GrimWorld's Terminator and Custodes armour have them), jewellery named a ring, psyring, bracelet or anklet.
- Matched as what they are, before scoring (`AsWhatItIs`): webbing or chest rigs (`WebbingWords`) as CE's tactical vest; packs (worn only on a backpack layer, or with the belt (a jump pack), or named a backpack, not a cape or cloak, `NotPackWords`) as `PackLayer` over `PackGroup`, so any mod's pack takes CE's backpack (or its radio pack for a vox or comms pack), but a Spacer-or-later pack over neck and shoulders takes none (`ArmourPack`, power armour's own); apparel only on a shoulder layer (`GW_Shoulder`) as `ShoulderLayer` (only `Pauldrons`); apparel only on `EyeCover` as over the eyes alone. Bags, satchels, rucksacks (`BagWords`) are packs. CE's packs and vest have `layer`/`groups` lines so they stand in without CE.
- Words: the defName is matched as words (`WordsOf`); the top and ladies' hats also need "hat"; headgear labelled a hood, cap, hat, beret or bandana (`HatNouns`) is matched as a hat even if armour; one named a helmet or helm (`HelmetWords`) never as a hat.
- `Hints` (`HINTS` in `stand_ins.py`): words in an apparel's name or label that say it is a garment's kind without its name words (headsets, sun hats, tiaras, lab gowns, winter coats, hairbands, ribbons, maid caps, nun veils (the broadwrap), stockings, tights, leotards, swimsuits, messenger bags, skirts and dresses...), less any `HintNot` phrase, let the garment stand in even if `NoStandIn` or a mask, coverage at least `HintCover` (`HintCoverHead` for headgear, one garment), `HintBonus` ahead (less than `NamedBonus`). Made for the race mods' clothes.
- Cane Aternitigno (War Dogs) pass (2026-11-26), general rules learned from its misses, checked over every installed mod's apparel with `stand_ins.py` (only the meant items moved): a label naming a helmet and no mask beats "mask" in the defName (`IsMaskNamed(def, text)`: its `CG_HeavyMask` is a visored "Heavy Infantry Helmet" and took the gas mask); headgear named a helmet, not armour by category, not a hat or mask (`Profile.helmet`) puts soft headgear behind as armour does (`SoftForHelmet`: not armour and no Helmet in the garment's name, so the vacsuit helmet still counts), as without CE its chemical protection helmet took the broadwrap (counting such headgear as armour outright was tried: Wolfein's space suit helmet lost the vacsuit helmet and its gas-mask helmet the gas mask). Hints added: cassock/soutane/habit → `DressLong`, fedora/trilby/homburg/panama hat → `BowlerHat`, peaked/visor/service/officer cap and kepi → `AuthorityCap`, side/garrison/overseas cap → `Beret`, sweater/jumper/pullover/hoodie/sweatshirt → `BasicShirt`, suspenders/braces → `BodyStrap` (and out of `Stockings`, which keeps the singular suspender). `NameNot` socks: cassock. Words are still found anywhere in a word: matching at word starts only fixed cassock but lost run-together names (GrimWorld's fieldcap, VoxBackpack, Wolfein's AIPTGoggle). Per item (`STANDS_FOR` in `export_frames.py` and the garment files): its psyker hat and mech visor the authority cap (the visor with the airwire headset), its suits the jacket and trousers, its HFA set the flak vest and flak pants, its space army armour (Italian label) power armour. `stand_ins.py` now also indexes a mod's own `Assets.zip` (the game reads it; Cane Aternitigno packs all its art so).
- Race mods pass (2026-11-26: NewRatkinPlus, Wolfein, Milira, Kiiro; after the general race pass of 2026-11-23), by contact sheets of all 417 of their apparel. General rules (checked over every installed mod, with and without CE: only the meant item moved each): jewellery now also a pendant, necklace, amulet or locket (`Jewellery`; Milira's psychic defense pendant, Middle over the torso, took the psyfocus vest); a "load carrier" is webbing (`WebbingWords`; Kiiro's took the royal vest). Tried: "dustcoat" as the duster's name word (`NameAlike`) moved nothing, as the lab coat is far ahead on coverage for Wolfein's two (both open, drawn as the lab coat, which is fair). The rest, 75 items, by `STANDS_FOR` (a commented block per mod in `export_frames.py`, the reasons item by item): mostly outfits the sprites show as dresses or skirts that the scoring made a shirt and trousers (Ratkin's coats and aprons, Wolfein's office and sailor uniforms), coats with no Shell layer (lab gowns on OnSkin), helmets with a gas mask or goggles on them (two garments), Milian armour (the unit's whole mech body) as power armour, and items on a mod's own layer (Wolfein's scarf, muzzle) that took nothing. Name "Dress" or "Skirt": the length and sleeves come from the art (`HemLengths`). Skirted outfits still need their names (or `stands_for`).
- Open fronts (`Source/OpenFronts.cs`): the `Duster` and `LabCoat` (`OpenFronts`) stand in only for apparel whose front sprite is open down the middle (`FrontFits`): `OpenFrontShare` (read once per def from `wornGraphicPath` `_<body>_south`, `OpenBodies`, by `Api.ReadSpritePixels` at `OpenSize`) is how far up from the hem the clear gap at the middle column (`OpenHalfWidth`) reaches over the garment's height; open from `OpenFrontLeast` (0.45; closed outfits read at most 0.22). `stand_ins.py`: `OPEN_FRONTS`, `open_front_share`, sprites from `texture_index` or Assets.zip.
- `pads` lines (`GARMENT_PROPS` `pads`; `SkinnedFigure.HasPads`, `WalkFigures.ArmourPads`): the marine and cataphract armour and variants also draw `Pauldrons`, in the armour's colour unless a shoulder apparel has its own; `PauldronsBig` (its `Big` key) in its place in Space Marine or Custodes armour (`BulkOf`'s `Marine`, `marine_of` line, `SkinnedFigure.MarineVersionOf`, `WalkFigures.MarinePads`).
- `stand_ins.py` reads each Workshop mod's folders as the game loads them for 1.7 (`mod_folders`: its `LoadFolders.xml`, else its root, `Common` and newest version folder). `--loo` scores the game's own clothes as if they had none. `standins` autotest notes what every loaded apparel gets in the game.

## Tuning another mod (the clothing matcher and gene fallbacks; done for Cane Aternitigno and the four race mods, 2026-11-26)

When asked to check or tune M3AT's support for another mod (not a content pack: M3AT's own matching):

1. Find it: `~/.local/share/Steam/steamapps/workshop/content/294100/<id>/` (`About/About.xml` for the name and packageId; `LoadFolders.xml`). Its art may be loose in `Textures/` or packed in its own `Assets.zip` (`python3 anim_scripts/game_sprite.py <zip> <part of path> <out>`). Whether it is enabled: `~/.config/unity3d/Ludeon Studios/RimWorld by Ludeon Studios/Config/ModsConfig.xml` (ask before changing it for a test). Note what it adds: a HAR race (then `dev_notes/body.md`, Other races), xenotypes and genes, apparel.
2. Clothing, the baseline: `python3 anim_scripts/stand_ins.py > before.txt` with no arguments (every expansion and mod: garments with no `layer`/`groups` lines take their profile from their own def, so passing one mod's defs drops Ideology's broadwrap and the like and misleads), then `python3 anim_scripts/stand_in_sheet.py <mod folder> before.txt sheet.png <defName prefix>` and look at every item's sprite against its match.
3. General rules first: for each miss, ask what rule would have caught it for any mod (a missing word in `Hints`, a `NameNot`/`HintNot` phrase, a wrong rule), and change `StandIns.cs` and `stand_ins.py` together. Then diff the whole output against the baseline (`LC_ALL=C`, `awk '{print $1, $2}' | sort`, `join`, keep rows whose match changed): every change must be one you meant, across all mods. Run once without CE too (leave out folders `2172612733` and `3495749827`, which define CE's garments: without CE, `CE_PlateHelmet` and the like aren't there to stand in).
4. Then per item, for what no general rule should decide (a label that says nothing, a non-English label, a sprite that is plainly something else): `STANDS_FOR` in `anim_scripts/export_frames.py` (a commented block per mod) **and** the `stands_for` line in each named `Models/chibi/garment_<Name>.txt` (by hand: don't re-export for it). A `stands_for` names the apparel whatever the scoring says; two garments naming one apparel draw both (the jacket and the trousers for a suit).
5. Genes: list its `GeneDef`s with `renderNodeProperties` (parent `GeneEarsBase`/`GeneTailBase`, or ear/tail in the path). Lay their sprites over the mod's own head sprite (same mesh, so scale each to one canvas) beside the vanilla gene's (`Data/Biotech/Assets.zip`, `HeadAttachments/`, `BodyAttachments/`) and our parts rendered offline (vertices and `f` lines of `ears*.txt`, `tail*.txt`, `race_*.txt` on `skin.txt`). Add each to `TunedGeneParts` (`Source/TunedGenes.cs`) as the vanilla gene whose part it is closest to. A shape ours lacks needs its own file (a copy in the .blend, as Ratkin's and Wolfein's ears are of the cat ears), not shape keys on ours (`dev_notes/body.md`).
6. Docs: a row in `Docs/Compatibility.csv` (**its line endings are CRLF: keep them** (an edit once left it LF; check with `grep -c $'\r'`); Untested until seen in the game; Clothes `Automatched (tuned)` where `stands_for` lines name its apparel, and its parts and face in the content menu's words: `Custom models`, `Autogenerated (WIP)`, `Mapped`, `Available`, `FA face required`), then `python3 anim_scripts/compat_table.py`. The content switcher shows Mapped (parts) and Automatched (tuned) (clothes) by itself (`dev_notes/options.md`), and lists a mod with no race once it has tuned genes or `stands_for` apparel.
7. Notes: the rules added and why in the stand-in scoring section above; the genes in `dev_notes/body.md`.
8. Check: `dotnet build Source/M3AT.csproj`; in the game only with the mod enabled (`raceclothes`, `race_<defName>`, `tail`, or a scenario for it).

Tried and not to try again: matching words only at a word's start (run-together names: fieldcap, VoxBackpack, AIPTGoggle); telling skirts from trousers by the sprite's outline (above); treating apparel named a helmet as armour outright (the vacsuit helmet and gas-mask helmets lost: `SoftForHelmet` instead).

## Skirt and dress lengths (`Source/HemLengths.cs`)

- Whichever skirt or dress the scoring picks (`SkinnedFigure.StandInsOn`, from `WornGarmentsNow`) is swapped for the garment of its kind (`HemKinds`: a name starting Skirt or Dress with a `hem_share` line, `Piece.hemShare`) whose hem is nearest how far the apparel's front sprite comes down the pawn's body sprite (`HemShareOn`: `BodyGraphic`, the race's under HAR, with `ApparelGraphicRecordGetter`'s art; 0 top, 1 feet; crotch ~0.62, knee ~0.81; a legless body by its path, `LeglessBodies`/`LeglessFeet`), less `HemShorter` (0.09), cached per pair of textures, unless the player chose in the clothing tool. The hem is the picture's lowest row, so drawn stockings read as long.
- A dress is sleeveless when its apparel doesn't cover Arms and its art stands out past the body's at the shoulders (`SleeveBand`) by less than `SleevelessBelow` (`SleeveOverhangOn`).
- Art with legwear drawn in (Milira's bodycon dress over stockings to the feet) can't show its hem by its outline: when the apparel's sprite follows the body's outline (each edge within `HugSlack` px) up from its bottom for more than `HugRun` of the body's height (`LegwearDrawnIn`; that dress 57 rows of 76, every other race dress 7 at most), the scoring's length is kept, the sleeves still by the art. Looked into and passed over: the hem where the art stands out past the body (read Wolfein's narrow nun habit as a mini skirt) and the colour across the legs (fragile).
- The body picture exists only once the game resolves the render tree, so an unread pawn's garments are worked out again on its next draws (`HemUnread`, `WornCache.hemTries`, at most `HemTriesMost`). The bug report gives the hem; the clothing tool's Automatic shows the length (`AutoStandInsOn`).
- A skirt goes over what is worn with it on the shirt layer or under (a shirt tucked in; `SkinnedFigure.PieceGoesOver` in `OverOf`), also between two garments drawn for one apparel; only an outer layer goes over it.
- `hemlengths` autotest (with `M3AT_ADD_MODS="erdelf.humanoidalienraces ancot.ancotlibrary ancot.kiirorace ancot.milirarace melondove.wolfeinrace solaris.ratkinracemod"`): notes each race skirt's and dress's hem and garment (`SkinnedFigure.HemDiagnosis`, `HidingReport`), checks and pictures them.

## Layers and underwear

- Each apparel layer counts as a body layer (`SkinnedFigure.BodyLayerOf`, cached): the game's as they are; head layers (`HeadLayers`: Overhead, OnHead, StrappedHead, EyeCover) always themselves; a mod's by name or label ("under" → `Underwear`, a word of `BodyLayerWords` → that body layer, e.g. Kiiro's `Kiiro_Pants` → under `OnSkin`), else if drawn under `OnSkin` `Underwear`, else itself. For stand-ins every underwear layer is one body layer `Underwear`, a step under `OnSkin` (`UnderLayer`; `stand_ins.py`'s `under`).
- Underwear (`SkinnedFigure.UnderSkin`: UnderWhere's `Underwear`/`UnderwearTop`, Kiiro's `Kiiro_Underwear`, the like) goes under everything by draw order (`GoesOver`), stays on in bed with `NoEquipment` (`KeptOnInBed`), is put on with `dress_over`/`dress_legs` (`DressKindsOf`, `OnTheSkin`), carries wounds' blood (`SkinnedWounds`), and doesn't count toward the shoes (`IsUnderwear`).
- Garments: `Underwear` (briefs), `Shorts`, `Tanktop`, `Bra`, `Loincloth` (own flap weights, `part_weights`), `Chestwrap` (bandeau), `CropTop`, `ShortShorts`, `LongJohns` (waffle-knit union suit, `paint_long_johns.py`). Layers, groups and `like` set in `export_frames.py`'s loop; `STANDS_FOR` names Kiiro's sets. They sit at the shirt layer's offset, well inside `GarmentInside` (0.04 cells).
- `underwear` autotest (UnderWhere; Kiiro's with Kiiro): each set alone and under clothes, checking each garment (`SameGarments`).

## Clothing tool (`Source/ClothingTool.cs`)

- The player picks the garments drawn for apparel with no garment of its own, per defName (`Settings.clothingChoices`: names joined by commas, `WalkFigures.NoneDrawn` ("-") for none; not touched by "Restore all defaults", cleared by its own button). `StandInsFor` reads it first (`WalkFigures.ChosenStandIns`; unknown names ignored); `AutoStandInsFor` is the scoring's own. A change clears `wornCaches` and re-bakes ground apparel (`ClothingChoicesChanged`).
- The "M3AT tools" toggle among the play settings (`gripToolButton`/`gripTool`) gives a humanlike wearing such apparel (`HasStandInApparel`) an Adjust clothing button (`ClothingToolWindow`): Change (automatic, none, or any of `ChoosableGarments`, labelled by `GarmentLabel`), Add another, Automatic. With `spriteClothes`, a Paint box per apparel whose garments take its art (`PaintsArt`); unticked, its defName goes in `Settings.plainClothes` (`WalkFigures.PaintedArt`). The bug report marks these "(player's choice)".
- `plainclothes` autotest: the first loaded apparel (another mod's first) whose stand-in takes its art, painted, plain and painted again (`ClothPaintedDraws`).

## Stand-ins painted with their art (`Source/SpriteClothes.cs`; `spriteClothes`, on; a prototype)

A garment drawn for apparel with no garment of its own (`PaintsArt`: has UVs and an image; not a hat riding one bone, head-layer apparel, held, a pack, the shoes, the baby romper, pajamas or a corpse's) gets an image of its own per garment, apparel material and body picture (`ClothImageOf`, at most `ClothBakesPerFrame` a frame, `ClothMost` a session; plain until made): each texel (placed at rest, `TexelsOf`) coloured from the apparel's four worn sprites as the game colours them (`ViewOf`: ink taken out, colours grown, `GrownArt`), weighted by facing and alpha. The sprites are laid on the chibi by the pawn's body sprite: rows by landmarks (`ClothShare`: neck top, shoulder line and waist from `BodyMarks` (`ClothWaist` for the waistless male body) onto the chibi's shoulders and the skirts' waist ring (`RigWaist`), then `HEM_ANCHORS`' crotch, knee, ankle), across by the body sprite's width onto the chibi's trunk and legs at that height (`ClothBody`: cut through the surface per band and averaged over neighbours). The garment's own image is not used (2026-11-26: multiplied back about its median as detail, it printed our seams, pockets and stitching over the apparel's art; blurred to its folds, and with none at all, compared in `raceclothes`, the art alone was chosen). `Draw`'s `cloth` takes it (`textureMarkAt`). What the game's head covers on the body picture (`HeadCovered`, from `WalkFigures.HeadCoverOf`: the head and body nodes' meshes and matrices through the render tree, as the race parts are, so body-type head offsets, children's heads and HAR are in it; the head node's picture, else its head type's) is taken out of the art and grown over, and the lowest covered row over the body's middle third is the view's `chin` (the south's, given to every view), pinned to `RigTop` in `ClothShare` (the shoulder line dropped as a landmark when it is above the chin less `ClothChinClear`; a chin past `ClothChinMost` 0.35 is ignored). 2026-11-26: Cane Aternitigno's (War Dogs) `CG_ShirtVest` has its neck hole and the back of its collar where the head covers the top quarter of the body (chin at 0.25-0.30 of the body on the vanilla bodies), which the old top-of-sprite pin put halfway down the chest. Images are keyed by the head's south picture too. `GrownArt` also takes out interior ink lines (a grey closing over `ClothLineReach`, pixels darker than `ClothInkDark` of it), not only ink at the edge: magnified onto the garment they were dark smears; mid-grey shading strokes and creases (the cargo pants') are kept. Edges smooth (2026-11-27, `EdgesOf`, `ColumnAt`, `ClothWidthAt`): the body sprite's left and right edges are read to a fraction of a pixel from the alpha either side and averaged over rows (`ClothEdgeSmooth`, 1.5 rows at 128), and both they and the chibi's band widths are read between rows and bands. Taken a whole pixel and a band at a time, every step in the sprite's outline moved that row of art sideways, so side shading and folds came out as saw-teeth on the garment (the War Dogs hoodie's sleeves and sides, compared in `cloth_debug`). The art's own pixels are still magnified about eight times (a 128 sprite gives a shirt about 125 texels a cell, the garment image about 750), smoothed by `Sample`'s Catmull-Rom. Tried 2026-11-27: each view upscaled to `ClothFine` (512) and smoothed along its edges (`Upscaled`: structure tensor at the sprite's pixels, two passes of line averaging `ClothFineReach` either way, by how clear the edge is). It smooths folds, seams and the knit's lines a little more but softens the art, and costs about 180 ms more per bake (bakes were already about 100 ms, 300+ for the suit jacket), so it is off: `SkinnedFigure.clothStyle` (0 smooth edges, the default; 1 the old whole-pixel edges; 2 also upscaled), switched by `RestyleCloth` (lets every image and view go). `spriteclothes` ends with close-ups of its last row in all three (`close_<def>_<yaw>_<before|edges|upscaled>`). Open: sleeves take the trunk's side colour, so a vest over a shirt gets the vest's sleeves where the head covers the shirt's sleeve stubs. AUTOTEST writes images to `<config>/M3AT/cloth_debug/` (`DumpCloth`, with each view's shoulder, waist and chin); `raceclothes` pictures each row plain and painted; `spriteclothes` the War Dogs tops on Thin, Female, Male and Hulk.

## Other mods' garments and gene parts (`Source/ModGarments.cs`)

- Modder guides (shipped): `Docs/Apparel.md`, `Docs/GeneParts.md`, the Blender add-on `Docs/Tools/m3at_garment_kit.py`, `Docs/Examples/`.
- Every `.txt` in an `M3ATgarments` folder at another active mod's root, then in `<config>/M3AT/M3ATgarments`, is read after this mod's garments (`AddModGarments`, from `Load` before `pieces` is set; a loaded name is skipped; its defs win over ours).
- The file is the export's garment form, but body-dependent parts are fitted here as `write_garment` fits them (`FitToBody`): weights (`FitWeights`; `bone` line whole on that bone; own `w` lines only with `own_weights yes` and a `bones` line, `ReadBoneNames`), every body key (`FitKeys`: blended from the body, `rigid` whole, `hard` (default with `bone`, `hardLine`) by bone middles tied to body vertices, `BoneAnchors`, not Shrinkhead; then `Hung`, `PushedOut`, `Filled`; own key lines for `own_keys`), hidden body triangles (`BodyUnder`: the exporter's `hidden_under` with angle-weighted normals and edge middles; `hides no`; `hide_bones`), winding turned to the body's if most faces in (`Orient`). A `body` line equal to this skin.txt's hash keeps the file's own w, key and hide lines; its second number, the body generation it was made on, older than skin.txt's `generation` gets a note in the load log (`dev_notes/garment_scripts.md`, The body's generation).
- `base <Name>`: a retexture of our garment Name (`Retextured`). `genes <defNames>`: a gene part (`geneParts`, `GenePartsOf`), never worn: `DrawDressed` adds its bits to the `HideUnder` mask and draws it after the fur (`HideGenePart`: triangles under worn garments left out, `GarmentTris`, and `Tucked`) in the skin's, hair's or own colour (`colour_from`, `GeneColourFrom`).
- Worn-garment masks are 256 bits (`Source/GarmentMask.cs`, `MaxHiding` 254).
- `anim_scripts/garmentfit/run.sh [--compare] [--write folder] <garment.txt> ...`: the fitting outside the game. `--compare` fits our exported garments from shape alone against the exporter's (weights the same, keys within ~0.01 cells, ~90% of hidden triangles); `--write` writes fitted files with this body's `body` line. `--outfit <name> ...` (names as in `garment_<name>.txt`, outer first) counts a person in those garments from the files as made, as `DrawnTriangles` counts a worn outfit in the game (a development count; the About poly counts message showed it until Nov 2026).

## Content packs' garments (`SkinnedFigure.GarmentOf` → `Wanted`)

- A pack's garment for apparel that has one of ours too used to win silently; ours is kept in `ownGarmentOf` and drawn when the pack's isn't wanted.
- `PackGarmentWanted`: not if its mod is off for a benchmark turn; else the player's per-item pick in `clothingChoices` (the pack garment's name: the pack's; anything else, or `M3atAutomatic` "*", which names no garment so `ChosenStandIns` falls back to the automatic match: M3AT's); else the pack's source for the garment's row: Armour for armour and helmets (`ContentSources.ClothingPartOf`, `ArmourByDefault`), Clothing for the rest; Armour is a part of its own, the pack's by default since 2026-11-25 like every part (it used to be a hidden rule inside Clothing, so choosing the pack's clothing never gave the pack's armour) (sharp armour at least `ArmourSharp` 0.8 of its default stuff, or a helmet word in its label or defName: the packs' helmets came out as blobs over the face and their power armour with bare arms).
