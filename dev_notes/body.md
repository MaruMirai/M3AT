# Body, size, races and genes

The pawn's size and shape on the skinned figure, other races, age, gene parts, missing and prosthetic limbs, wounds. Body keys are made in the rig (`dev_notes/garment_scripts.md` for the key-making scripts).

## Size (`WalkFigures.SizeOf`)

- Standard height: anim.txt's `game_scale` line (walk.py's `GAME_SCALE` × `FIGURE_HEIGHT`, 1.08 × 1.25 = 1.35 cells; 1.2 for an export without it; `WalkFigures.StandardHeight`, read with the weapon sizes by `ReadTypeFile`). `ScaleUp` (`GAME_SCALE`) is what the clips aimed at things in the world and the holds are made for, so **change the standard size in walk.py and export, not in the C#**.
- `pawnHeight`: a grown pawn's height in cells before gender and variety, absolute so the player's choice stands when the standard changes; -1 (default, not saved) is the standard. `Settings.HeightLeast`..`HeightMost` (1..1.7) by `HeightStep`. An old save's `pawnSize` (a share of the old 1.5-cell standard) is converted once (`OldStandardHeight`). `WalkFigures.PawnSize` (over the standard), on top of `ScaleUp`.
- By age, in place of IsoRim's life stage size (`Api.FigureSizeFor`): from `BirthSize` (0.25 of grown, before `ScaleUp`) along `Growth` (children's median heights) to 1 at the race's last life stage's `minAge` (`GrownAge`, 18 for humans); IsoRim's size only for a race with no stages.
- `genderSizes` (on, `WalkFigures.GenderSize`): a grown male `GenderGap` (0.1) bigger than a grown female (half each way), eased in over puberty from `GenderFrom` to `GenderFull` of `GrownAge` (11 to 16 of 18).
- `heightSpread` (0.02; 0.04 is real people's; `WalkFigures.HeightSize`): each pawn's own height, a normal spread cut at `HeightMost` (2.5) of it, from the pawn's id (`Mix`, murmur3's finalizer, as `Gen.HashCombineInt` gave ids in a row nearly one height), kept from birth.
- Old age `AgeShrink` (`AgeSize`), armour (below).
- Info card (`Source/SizeStat.cs`): a postfix on `Pawn.SpecialDisplayStats` adds "M3AT size" for pawns the chibi draws (`DrawnAsChibi`): `SizeOf` over a standard grown pawn at the options' height (`ScaleUp * PawnSize`) as a percentage, the standing height in cells, and each factor (keys `M3AT_Size*`).

## Shape (`ShapeFor`, `ShapeOf`)

- One shape per mix of keys (cached). Since varied builds make one for nearly every pawn, a shape builds only the body's rest pose at once and each garment's when first worn in it (`SkinnedFigure.ShapedPiece`).
- Body type: the gender's key (a child's on the base body, which with no key is sexless; no gender key for a baby). A race's own body type is drawn as the game's one its name says (`BodyKeyOf`: Milira_Female as Female, else the gender's); a mod's own body type keeps the plain shape.
- `variedBodies` (on) and `bodySpread` (0.3; `WalkFigures.VariedBuild`, `ShapeOf`'s `builds`): each pawn's build spread about its body type's on two axes, weight (Thin -1 .. Fat 1) and muscle (Hulk 0..1), a normal spread per axis from the id (`BuildSeed`), cut at `BuildMost`, turned back in off either end (never thinner than Thin; any type but Fat goes only `FatShare` (0.4) into Fat; muscle below none stays none), in `BuildSteps` (eighths), then cut to `WeightKeep` (0.8) and `MuscleKeep` (0.3) of the move, as the keys were made stronger since (re-measure those two if the keys change again).
- `bodyShapeStrength` (0..`WalkFigures.BodyShapeMost` 1.5, default 1, `BodyShapeStep`): the Thin, Fat and Hulk amounts (the body type's and the varied build's) × this; a pair key such as `Hulk_Female` goes with the body type's amount; gender, baby fat, pregnancy and gene keys whole. `ShapeOf` takes a body or build key up to `MostKeyAmount` (2).
- `headShrink` (0..1 of `Shrinkhead`, `Settings.DefaultShrink` 0; the slider shrinks to the right, labelled with the measured shrink, `HeadSize` from `ShrunkSize`, in `ShrinkStep`s as each amount makes its own shapes; `WalkFigures.HeadShrink`): a newborn takes `BabyHeadShrink` (0.25) of it, growing to all as `Babylimbs` goes (`HeadShrinkOf`).
- Babies (`DevelopmentalStage.Baby()`): the `BabyBody` key (`Fat`) from full at birth to none at `BabyYears` (3) (`BabyGrowth`, `ShapeOf`'s `bodyAmount`), no gender key, no hair. Every pawn: `Babylimbs` (short stubby limbs) from full at birth to none at `BabyLimbsYears` (10) (`BabyLimbsOf`, `BabyLimbsSteps`).
- Pregnancy: `Pregnant` by `Hediff_Pregnant.GestationProgress` (`PregnancyOf`, rounded up to `PregnantSteps`; full in labour).
- Gene keys (`GeneKeysOf`, `ShapeOf`'s `geneKeys`): body keys named for their gene, whole while active: `Ears_Pointed`, `Brow_Heavy`, `Jaw_Heavy`, `Head_Gaunt`, `Nose_Pig`, and the hands' `Hands_Pig`, `ElongatedFingers`, `WebbedPhalanges`; `No_Human_Ears` for a pawn with animal ears (`EarFileOf`) or race ears (`HasRaceEars`). `MeasureHead` leaves them out of the head's box, so head wear and ears still fit the skull.

## Armour size, soles and ceilings (`Source/ArmourSize.cs`)

- `ArmourSizeOf` in `SizeOf`; the larger of:
  - GrimWorld 40,000's Space Marines are plain Humans made Marines by their armour: its (`GW_`) Spacer armour over torso, arms and legs, not a scout's, Sister of Silence's or robe (`BulkOf`'s `Marine`; `marineBulk`, on, Mod compatibility, with the `armourBulk` slider, 1.25). `powerArmourBulk` (hidden, off, `ArmourSize.PowerArmourBulk`) would do the same for any such armour. A bulked wearer (`ArmourBulky`) gets at least `ArmourHulk` of the Hulk key (`ArmouredBuild`).
  - `armourDrawScale` (on): body armour a mod draws scaled up when worn (`DrawData.ScaleFor`: GrimWorld's Custodes 1.1, the Emperor's 1.2, Valdor's 1.4).
  - Whatever grows it, the head keeps its unarmoured size at the player's head size (`ArmouredHeadShrink`: the Shrinkhead amount solved from `SettingsMod.HeadSize`/`ShrinkFor`, `ShrunkSize`, rounded to `HeadShrinkRound`, up to `SkinnedFigure.MostShrink` (1.1), past the whole key, allowed only for this).
- Soles: a garment with `GARMENT_PROPS` `sole` (cataphract, marine, recon and variants) is measured at export for how far below the body's soles it reaches (its `sole` line, `SOLE` output line), and the wearer is lifted by the most of what it wears (`SkinnedFigure.SoleOf`, by last draw's `wornMask`) × its size, on its feet (`y` in placing, before the branches); none lying, crawling, swimming or in the air; less by `shownWork` knelt or sat on the floor (`ArmourSize.OnTheFloor`). The soles are made by `anim_scripts/armour_soles.py` (`dev_notes/garment_scripts.md`).
- Ceilings: a pawn under a roof drawn taller than the ceiling (`CeilingDrop`: its rest height with what it wears, `SkinnedFigure.TopOf` (body and garments but packs and held shields, cached), × size + soles, against `CeilingOver`: the underside of the floor above's slab with IsoRimZ's stacked floors (`Api.Floors` height less IsoRim's internal `Floors.SlabThickness`, 0.12), else `Api.WallHeight`, less `CeilingClear`) crouches (`Crouch`, up to `CrouchDepth`), then stoops (`Stoop`, up to `CeilingStoopMost`), eased (`Ease.ceilingDrop`, `CeilingPose`), on its feet only, and only while the floor above is drawn over it (`CeilingOver`'s `drawn`: IsoRimZ's upper floor viewed), so never without stacked floors (the `ceilingCrouch` option, which could also crouch it under any roof or never, is gone).

## Other races (`Source/OtherRaces.cs`; `otherRaces`, on)

- IsoRim draws every humanlike race but Human and CreepJoiner as a flat card unless an alien pack (`IsoRim.AlienModelDef`) makes it a figure, and only figures reach our drawer. An alien figure rule (`Api.AddAlienFigureRule`, `RaceRule`) answers `Human` for each race with no pack (`HasAlienPack`; the Kiiro pack still decides Kiiro); IsoRim's "Keep as paper" list still wins. Toggling adds or removes the rule as the window closes (`OtherRacesChanged`), and IsoRim asks every race again.
- `race_<defName>` autotest turns it on, takes head wear off, checks each pawn is a figure (`race_figure_`), notes race parts and writes pawn 0's parts' atlases (`part_<race>_<addon>.png`). Tried: NewRatkinPlus (`Ratkin`), Wolfein (`Wolfein_Race`), Milira (`Milira_Race`, a 1.6 mod that loads), Kiiro (`Kiiro_Race`, with its pack).
- Race hair: `dev_notes/hair.md`. Race faces: `dev_notes/faces.md`.

## Race parts (`Source/AddonParts.cs` gathers, `Source/AddonBuilder.cs` builds and draws; `raceParts`, on)

- Built in 3D from its pictures: every HAR body add-on node of the render tree (`Walk`: a node class named `BodyAddon`) but the hair ones (`HairBuilder.HairAddon`/`HairGraphic`; a small one the hair leaves out, `SmallTuft`, is built), a `CatLikeRaces` race's ears and tail (the chibi's own instead) and markings (`Marking`).
- Per facing (Rot4 order): the game's picture (`GetFinalizedMaterial`, read once per texture at `AddonTexSize`, `PixelsOf`), the map from its parent head/body node's frame to its uv (`UvMap`, from the mesh's corners, so HAR's offsets, sizes and mirroring are in it), and the race's head/body opaque box (`ReferenceBox`; the head type's art where FA blanks the head node), laid on the chibi's head/body box (`AddonPicture`, `AddonBoxes`: the head without `No_Human_Ears`).
- Solid: the outline in the picture showing most of it clear of the head/body (`ModelArea`: front on near ties, a side only if the front shows under `AddonSideShare`; a tail always a side), inflated as thick as it is far in from the outline (`BuildAddon`, `Distances`; ears, wings, fins and feathers no thicker than `AddonThin`, as two face-on ear pictures made a drum), depth row by row where the other pictures have it (`Middles`), surface nets (`SurfaceNets`, voxels at least `AddonCellLeast`), islands under `AddonIslandShare` dropped, cut to about `AddonTriangles` (tails `AddonTailTriangles`) while still welded (`Decimate.Run`), textured per triangle from the picture facing it most (`TexturedAddon`; an opaque atlas, `AddonAtlas`/`Bled`, the outline ink within `AddonInkReach` filled from inside), outlined along smooth normals (`SmoothHull` in `AddonShape`) in its inside colour × tint, weighted whole to the head or, by height, the hips or chest (`AddonChestFrom`), head parts kept in the head's box through the head shrink. Drawn as hair (`DrawAddon`, `Posed.addons`) in the add-on's material colour.
- Glowing add-ons (glow or mote shader: Milira's halo) are drawn as IsoRim's glow card of their material (`AddonGlowOf`, `AddonGlowAt`, `Api.DrawGlowCard`).
- Looked at every `AddonLook` ticks or when what's worn changes (`RacePartsOf`). A race with ears loses the chibi's own (`HasRaceEars`). Head parts off under what hides gene ears (`EarHiding`), tails under what hides the cat tail. A folded ear (Wolfein's `earR1`) comes out a flat plank. Meant as the fallback under bespoke models.
- Moving (`Source/RaceTails.cs`): a built tail (an add-on named a tail, on the body) or ear (named an ear, on the head) gets a chain of joints on its surface (`ChainOf`: from the vertex nearest the cat tail's root or the head's middle, by surface distance, each step's middle a joint; `ChainWeights` by nearest point; `ChainAddon`, from `BuildAddon`) and moves on the gene's springs: a tail as a `Tail` (`RaceTailBones` 9, `StepTailState`/`PoseTail`, moved so its root is the cat tail's root, as the picture put it on the thighs); an ear as an `Ears` on side 0 (`RaceEarBones` 3, `StepEarState`/`PoseEars`, `RaceEarFlat` of the mood's turns), closed with its ear gone.
- Race models (`raceModels`, on, Mod compatibility; `WalkFigures.RaceModels`): bespoke parts in place of the built ones, by the add-on they replace (`ModelFileFor`: ear or tail in its name), in the add-on's colour × its art's mean (`AddonMean`), the lining or tip (`inner` lines) toward the model's own colour: Ratkin's round mouse ears and thin tail, Wolfein's pointed ears and bushy pale-tipped tail, Milira's folded wings (AncotLibrary body-part nodes, found by wing body parts, `WingsOf`, coloured from the `left wing` node).
  - Files: `race_<race>_ears.txt` (gene ears' form), `race_<race>_tail.txt` and `race_milira_wing_L/R.txt` (the tail's form; a wing has `bone spine.003` (`Tail.bone`, hips by default) and the left `mirror -1`, so the pair shares one spring state, `DrawModelTail`'s `stateKey`).
  - First drafts: `python3 anim_scripts/make_race_parts.py "<mod>/Models/chibi" [part ...] [--force]` (numpy, from skin.txt's head and tail.txt's root; `RATKIN_EAR`, `WOLF_EAR`, `RATKIN_TAIL`, `WOLF_TAIL`, `MILIRA_WING`; leaves a file written from the .blend, header `(anim_scripts/export_cat.py)`, alone unless `--force`).
  - Editing: `anim_scripts/import_race_parts.py` (Run Script, or `blender -b Models/pawnHumanRig.blend --python anim_scripts/import_race_parts.py -- "<mod>/Models/chibi" [--over | out.blend] [part ...]`; parts already there left alone unless named) puts each into the rig as `<Race>_<Part>_Race` skinned to `<Race>_<Part>_Rig`, parented to its metarig bone, in the `RaceParts` collection (bones `Ear.L`, `Ear.L.001`, ... / `Tail`, `Tail.001`, ...; the `inner` group; properties `m3at_race_file`, `m3at_mirror`, `m3at_plain`). `export_cat.py`'s `write_race_parts` writes them back on a full export. Ratkin's and Wolfein's ears are copies of the cat ears (`COPIES`, `copy_cat_ears`, marked `m3at_from_cat`) reshaped by hand; after reshaping run `fit_floppy_ears.py` and `weight_floppy_ears.py` with `--ears <mesh> --rig <armature>`.
  - AUTOTEST: `<config>/M3AT/no_race_models` turns the models off.

- Content packs (`dev_notes/options.md`): ears or a tail the pawn gets from its genes or a pack (`EarFileOf`, `HasCatTail`, so a pack's patches or API count) leave out the race's ear and tail add-ons (`Gather`: `earsElsewhere`, `tailElsewhere`), else both drew; `RaceParts.version` has them gathered again when a pack's source changes.
- Skin (`Source/SkinTint.cs`): another race's skin colour times its body sprite's mean over the human body sprite's of the same body type (`RaceSkinTint`, opaque pixels lighter than `SkinInkBelow`, read at `SkinTintSize`, clamped `SkinTintLeast`..`SkinTintMost`, cached per sprite; an unreadable one tried every `SkinReadEvery` frames), as the game tints the sprite by the skin colour: Wolfein's grey skin colours over a pink sprite came out grey. A content pack's `SkinColour` wins.

## Gene parts

- Cat, pig and floppy ears (`Source/CatEars.cs`): `Ears_Cat`, `Ears_Pig`, `Ears_Floppy` (`EarFileOf`) get `ears.txt`, `ears_pig.txt` or `ears_floppy.txt` (the rig's `*_Ears_Gene`, written by `anim_scripts/export_cat.py`) on the head bone, fitted to the head (`FitOf`), the chibi's own ears off by `No_Human_Ears`. Pig's in the skin colour, others in the hair's; the `inner` group the skin's `EarPink` toward `EarLining`.
  - Motion: mood (`EarMood`, eased: `EarFlatBack`/`EarFlatDown` back and out when hit, enraged, downed or asleep; `EarPerkBack` up drafted; `up_L`/`up_R`/`down_L`/`down_R` shape keys too), a spring on each ear's joints driven by the root's motion in the head's frame (`StepEars`, `EarDrag`, `EarBob`), flicks (`Flick`, timed by `EarRoll`, a hash, no game random numbers). Other than three bones: each swing shared falling toward the tip (`SharesOf`).
  - Floppy ears (`Ears.flops`) move by curl (`StepFlop`, `PoseEars`): each joint's rest bend (`MeasureCurl`, `curlTurn`, plus `FlopLoose`, root `FlopRootLift`) straightened (`FlopPerk`, `FlopLift` going forward, `FlopBob`, `FlopSide` in a turn, a flick's `FlapCurl`) or tucked (`FlopTuck` hit, asleep, downed) within `FlopTightest` (off the hair) and `FlopStraightest`; swings back (`FlopDrag`, `FlopTuckBack`, about each joint's line to the tip, `axisSwing`) mostly to the hanging part (`FlopSharesOf`); each joint's spring `FlopSoften` softer outward.
  - Left off under what leaves the hair off or tucks it (`HidesEars`), by `earHiding` (`AnyHeadgear`, `Helmets` default (ArmorHeadgear, `SkinnedFigure.IsHelmet`), `Never`); a missing `Ear` closes that one.
  - Cat-like races (`HairBuilder.CatLikeRaces`: so far `Kiiro_Race`, races whose ears and tail were checked) with a HAR ear add-on (`CatLikeAddon`, `RaceHasAddon`: "ear" a word of its file name) get `ears.txt#race` (`ReadEars`' `RaceEars`): the cat ears shrunk `RaceEarScale`, a pale forward lining (`LiningFacing`, `RaceLining`, `RacePink`) and `RaceEarTufts` wedge tufts (`TuftLow`..`TuftHigh`, `TuftLong`, `TuftOut`).
- Ear linings (`Source/EarLining.cs`): the gene's cat ears, Kiiro's and Wolfein's take `Models/chibi/ear_fluffy.png`, Ratkin's `ear_mouse.png` (`LiningTextureOf`; pig and floppy keep the strand grain). `SplitLining` (in `ReadEars`): the forward-facing triangles (or the `inner` group's) are the inside, shared vertices copied so the two meet in a crisp rim (`e.inner`), each copy's outline pushed out the way both share (`LinedHull`), UVs along root to tip and across edge to edge (`LiningUvs`, `LiningHalf` either side of 0.25 outside and 0.75 inside, ears mirrored), so one painting fits any ear. **No pale inside since Nov 2026** (asked for): `LiningMost` 0 (was 0.95) leaves the cat ears' and Kiiro's inside in the hair's colour, Wolfein's `liningShare` is 0 (was 0.7), and `make_ear_textures.py`'s `PALE_INSIDE` off paints the inside half as the outside's fur; turn those back to bring the fluffy patch back. Ratkin's pink mouse lining is unchanged. Textures: `python3 anim_scripts/make_ear_textures.py "<mod>/Models/chibi" [preview.png]` (procedural, grey).
- Cat tail (`Source/CatTail.cs`): `Tail_Furry` or `Tail_Smooth` (`TailGenes`, `HasCatTail`; also cat-like races with a HAR tail add-on) get the rig's `Tail` (`tail.txt`; for furry `tail_furry.txt`, `Posed.furTail`, made at export by `anim_scripts/fur_tail.py`) in the hair colour, on the hips bone (`spine`, `TailBoneName`), moved with the hips joint for the shape (`TailFitOf`), its root moved onto the skin as posed (`OnSkin`, `RootWeights` of the nearest `TailRootSkin` body vertices).
  - Springs (`StepTail`, game time): each bone follows the one before (`TailChain`); the root swept by sideways motion of a probe in the hips' frame (`TailSideDrag`), lifted going forward (`TailForwardLift`), let down as the hips rise (`TailBob`), swaying at rest (`TailSwayAmp`, `TailSwayHz`, phase by id), set by state (`TailMood`); lifted `TailSitLift` seated.
  - Kept out of the ground on its feet (`tailFloor` 0); lying or getting up, above what it lies on (`OnFloor`, `TailFloorClear`); on the ground downed, dead or a ragdoll it droops (`TailDroop`) and swings out from the legs (`LaidOut`, `TailLieOut`), eased by `TailLayEase`.
  - Left off (still stepped) under body armour or a `hide_clothes` garment, not `Vacsuit`-tagged apparel (`SkinnedFigure.HidesTail`), with `armourHidesTail` (on). Not yet kept out of long coats.
- Strand grain (`Source/StrandGrain.cs`): cat and floppy ears, the cat tail, Furskin tufts and, with Furskin, the whole body (`GrainBody`: each vertex along the chain of bones with most of its weight; `DrawBody`'s `grained`, `BodyMaterial`, `PieceMeshes.grainMarkAt`) get the hair's strand streaks (`HairBuilder.Streaked`'s `StrandStreak`, `StreakLength`): UVs made as they are read (`StrandUvsSheet`, `StrandUvsTube` mirrored top and bottom, `StrandUvsTufts`), at the hair's density (`StrandScale`), on one tiling grey texture (`StrandTexture`), with the hair's material and light pass (`GrainedMaterial`, `GrainFur`).
- Fur coat (`Source/FurCoat.cs`): Furskin is drawn with a painted coat if the export folder has `fur.txt` and `fur.png`, from `anim_scripts/make_fur_texture.py` (`atlas` makes the body's atlas, `Models/fur/fur_atlas.npz`, **not remade lightly: a new one throws the painting out**; `painted` paints procedurally into `fur_painted.png`; `paintfile` writes `Models/fur/fur_paint.blend` for hand painting; `game` writes `fur.png` (lifted `GAME_LIFT`, the strand grain baked over) and `fur.txt` (each body triangle's corners matched to `skin.txt` by place)). `ReadCoat` copies the body split at the image's seams into a piece (`CoatPiece`, def `Gene_Furskin_Coat`, never worn) with the body's weights, keys and triangle order, so `DrawCoat` leaves out what the body does (`CoatTris`, `Tucked`) and paints prosthetics (`CoatPaintOf`); skinned by the body's bones, not the dress bones. Tufts take their colour from the body triangle under each root (`CoatTufts`), shaded root to tip (`TuftRootShade`, `TuftTipShade`, `Piece.shade`, `ShadedPaint`). **Run `game` again after painting or an export that changes the body** (`ReadCoat` warns and draws the plain body if `fur.txt` no longer matches); the tufts need only an export. Without the files, the strand grain alone. `furskin` autotest.
- Horns (`Source/Horns.cs`): `Headbone_MiniHorns`, `Headbone_CenterHorn` (`WalkFigures.HornsOf`) draw `horns_mini.txt`/`horns_center.txt` (`dev_notes/skinning.md`) on the head bone in IsoRim's horn colour (`HornColour`, `HornRootShade`). `HornShape` keeps each root's place in the head's box (`MeasureHead`) through the head shrink and scales by head width and, down to `HornNewborn`, `Babylimbs`. Off under what hides or tucks the hair (`HidesEars`) or with no head.
- Facial ridges: `FacialRidges` (`RidgesOf`, one of three designs by id) draws `Models/chibi/ridges_<a|b|c>.png` on the face patch in the skin's colour (`SkinnedFigure.DrawRidges`, `FaceLayers.RidgeMaterial`, queue 2997: over the face layers (at most 2996, `MaxLayers`), under the flat beard; with or without FA; off under what hides the face or with no head). `anim_scripts/make_ridges.py <chibi folder>` paints them (our own art).
- Pig nose: `Nose_Pig` key (a gene key, above) plus `Models/chibi/ridges_snout.png`, two nostrils drawn as the ridges are (`HasGeneKey`), on the upper half of the key's flat end (y 302..332 of the 512 px layout), made by `anim_scripts/generation/pigSnout/make_nostrils.py` (`out/nostrils.png`). The mouth moves down onto its lower half (`FaceLayers.Draw`'s `lowMouth`: `Patch.mouthLow` at `MouthRaisePig`, posed only once drawn, `lowUsed`). `anim_scripts/generation/pigSnout/face_test.py` renders it with FA's layers (`MOUTH_DOWN`, `ZOOM`, `TARGET_Z`).
- Baby romper (`Source/BabyRomper.cs`): a garment with `baby_romper` (`BabyRomper`, own `colour`; `SkinnedFigure.ForBaby`) is put on a baby with nothing over torso or legs once `RomperHours` (4) old, by age alone (`WornGarments`, `BabyRomperOf`), as a stand-in apparel made once and never spawned (`BabyRomperStandIn`: `Apparel_KidRomper`, else the basic shirt), which its layer and wounds go by.

- A content pack's gene part for an ear, tail or horn gene M3AT draws (`EarGenes`, `TailGenes`, `HornGenes`) is drawn in place of ours (`GenePartCovers`, in `DrawDressed`); with the player's GeneParts source M3AT's, the part is left out (`OffAddOn`).
## Age

- Old age (`Source/Aging.cs`; `agingLooks`, on): by biological age over `lifeExpectancy` (`AgeOf`: `AgingFrom` 0.55 to `AgingFull` 1, eased).
  - Wrinkles (`WrinklesOf`: nearest of `WrinkleLevels` (8), all in at `WrinklesFull`), drawn as the ridges are from `Models/chibi/ridges_age1..8.png`, painted by `anim_scripts/make_wrinkles.py <chibi folder> [preview.png]`: three stages as one amount (crow's feet and nose folds; forehead and eye bags; frown and mouth lines), each line fading in and thickening, then widening and darkening (`GROW`/`DEEPEN`), at `LEVELS` amounts; texels whose front-most patch triangle the `No_Human_Ears` key moves are cleared (`ear_mask`, `clear_of`), as the ears are on the patch.
  - Stoop (`StoopOf`: `StoopMost` degrees from `StoopFrom`, plus `BadBackStoop` (`BadBack`) and `FrailStoop` (`Frail`) at any age, at most `StoopMostOfAll`; `SkinnedFigure.Stoop`: the upper back bent over `TwistBones` in `Place`, the head tipped back up `StoopHeadUp`), less drafted (`StoopDrafted`), aiming (`StoopAiming`) and seated (`StoopSeated`), none lying, crawling, swimming, in the air, hunkering or getting up. Knees give `AgeKnees` of a crouch standing still (the larger of that and the cover crouch). `AgeShrink` of size.
  - Eyes: `Cataract` or `Blindness` on an eye (`CataractEyes`, not prosthetic): a dense milky cloud over FA's iris, drawn as a prosthetic eye is (below); every other own eye from `HazeFrom` gets age's faint haze (`HazeOf`, `HazeLevels`, `LimbDecal.weak`: the sheet's fifth row's cloud at that share, only on a measured iris); unmeasured, the eye row's third cell (`CataractColumn`, `make_prosthetic_decals.py`'s `eye_cataract`). In `DecalSign`.
  - Autotests: `aging` (a bald pawn at 25, 40, 52, 62, 85, checking greying, a dye at 85 shown as dyed and greyed again without it; at 70 with a bad back and cataracts; option off). `agingstrip` (`agingstrip_<hair defName>`; `agingstrip_Recruit` for bare temples): one dressed pawn at 20..85, full body and face, for a devlog (`previews/aging_devlog.png`, gridded with PIL). `eyetypes` pictures the haze at 55, 65, 72, 85.
- Greying hair (`Source/GreyingHair.cs`; `greyingHair`, on): drawing only. `HairColourOf` (hair, beard, scalp, ears, tail) fades toward a grey of the pawn's own (`GreyLeast`..`GreyMost` by id) along the game's grey-hair curve (`PawnHairColors.HasGreyHair`'s SmootherStep, 40 to 75 of 80: `GreyFrom`/`GreyFull` of life expectancy, shifted `GreyShift` by id). None with a `neverGrayHair` or `skinIsHairColor` gene, rotting or a shambler. Dyed hair is told by colour alone (the game keeps no mark): a styling station palette colour (`ColorDef` with `displayInStylingStationUI`) that isn't a natural hair colour (`colorType` Hair, a gene's `hairColorOverride`, `PawnHairColors`') stays as chosen (`Dyed`, `DyeColours`); any other greys.
- Skeletons (`Source/Skeletons.cs`; `skeletons`, on): a pawn the game draws as bones (`PawnRenderer.CurRotDrawMode` Dessicated: dried corpses, shamblers raised from one; a rotting shambler is drawn in the rotten skin colour, `SkinOf`) is drawn as the `Skeleton` garment (`SkinnedFigure.SkeletonPiece`, def `M3AT_Skeleton`, never worn) in place of the body, with what it still wears and no flesh (hair, beard, face, ears, tail, horns, fur, gene parts, wounds); `Handles` and both `placed.Bones` gates pass it only while `DrawsBones`. Made by `make_skeleton.py` (`dev_notes/garment_scripts.md`). `skeletons` autotest: dried corpses (naked, dressed, a child) and, with Anomaly, a dried and a rotting shambler, the dried one walking.

## Missing limbs

`CutLimbs` (after `Begin`, every frame, from the cached `GetMissingPartsCommonAncestors`, outside parts, side by `SideOf`) calls `SkinnedFigure.Cut` on the bone a part starts at: `Neck`/`Head` `spine.005` (no hair or face without `HasHead`), `Shoulder`/`Arm` `upper_arm`, `Hand` `hand`, `Leg` `thigh`, `Foot` `foot`, a thumb `thumb`, all four other fingers `hand.<side>.001`, all five toes `toe`. `End` squashes the cut bone and all below it to its joint in `p.bones` (what everything is skinned by), so the limb closes to a stump; `p.whole` keeps them for `BoneOf`/`JointOf` (weapons, tools, carried people). A prosthetic replaces the part, so that limb is whole.

## Prosthetics (`Source/Prosthetics.cs`; `prosthetics`, on; `archotechProsthetics`, on, off draws archotech as flesh)

- A limb with a `Hediff_AddedPart` on its arm (`Shoulder` or `Arm`), hand, leg or foot is drawn as that part, nothing modelled. `LimbsOf` (from `CutLimbs`) packs four bits for each of `SkinnedFigure.LimbBones`' eight slots: the look (`LookOf`, per HediffDef: Wood, Simple, Bionic or Archotech by name, else its `spawnThingOnRemoved`'s tech level, else `partEfficiency`) and `LimbPeg` for a name with `Peg`. `SkinnedFigure.Fit` → `Posed.limbs`; `DrawBody` passes `PaintOf` (cached per mix) as `paint`: vertices with `LimbShare` of their weight on the slot's bones in the look's `LimbPlate` colour, within `JointBand` of a joint `LimbJoint` (only a little darker, as vertex colour spreads), the outline too.
- Thinned bones (`Posed.thin`, `thinBy`, `thinAll`, `Thinned`, in `Place`): a peg's shin and all below to `PegThin`; a simple prosthetic's shin (`LegPylon`) or forearm (`ArmPylon`) alone, a pylon between a socket on the stump and a grey shell (`SimpleShell`, `SimplePylon`; a leg's shell by height, `ShellAboveAnkle`, as the shin bone carries the heel).
- Clothes over a peg or pylon keep their width: skinned by `Posed.dress` (cut, not thinned; `dressRows`; GPU `BlockOf(p, dressed)`; `PieceMeshes.dressed`; `Posed.pylons` re-poses them).
- No shoe on a prosthetic leg or foot (`Posed.bare`, a bit per side): the `withLegwear` garment's triangles on that leg left out (`Unshod`, after `Tucked`; `OnBareLeg`) and the body there drawn again (`HiddenBy`'s `bare`, `BodyTris`/`OutlineTris`).
- Decals on bionic and archotech limbs (`Wounds.LimbDecals`, `WalkFigures.DecalLimbs`/`DecalSign`, seeded by the part's load ID, `DecalSpan`, under the clothes): bionic plate seams, rivets, a vented hatch, a segmented cyan light strip and a joint plate with a lens (legs `LegDecalScale` bigger with extra panels; on the thigh kept `ThighDecalsBelow` down it, the decal's `below`, `SkinnedFigure.Spot`); archotech pale olive plates with yellow circuit traces and an inset panel. From `Models/prosthetic_decals.png` (`anim_scripts/make_prosthetic_decals.py`), stacked under the wound sheet as it loads (`Wounds.WithDecals`; `Rows` 14). No fur tufts (`HideFur`, `OnLimbs`) or wounds (`WalkFigures.OnProsthetic`, `AddedOn`) on them.
- Eyes (`WalkFigures.ProstheticEyes`: an added part on an `Eye`, with FA's face; `prostheticEyes`, on): drawn as an eye wound (`Wounds.ProstheticEyes`, `FaceLayers.DrawEyeWounds`, mode 7, `EyeWound.fitIris`): FA's own iris for that eye recoloured under the lids (`EyeWound.ramp`, `_Recolour`: the iris art's brightness, blurred by the cell's settings, through a colour ramp, plus a glow ring by distance from its middle in iris radii, within `OwnIris`; `make_prosthetic_decals.py`'s `RAMPS`, the sheet's fourth and fifth rows, `RampRow`/`BlindRow`: bionic steel to cyan, archotech olive to pale gold, cataract and haze clouds). The iris is measured by `Source/FaceGaze.cs` (`IrisOf`: alpha-weighted middle and a disc radius from its spread, by `Api.ReadSpritePixels`, on that eye's half, `HalfOf`). Unmeasured: the sheet's third row's art laid on the iris (`IRIS`, `IrisArtShare`, or `EyeArtSpan` where the frame puts it), cut to its alpha and moved with its gaze. Ears, jaws and implants aren't drawn.
- Autotests: `prosthetics` (each look on a bare pawn, eyes close up shaved, archotech off); `eyetypes` (every FA eye type per gender with a bionic eye and a cataract, or an archotech eye and blindness, front and looking right, checking both irises measured).

## Wounds (`Source/Wounds.cs`, `Source/SkinnedWounds.cs`; `visibleWounds`)

- Each `Hediff_Injury` on a part is drawn on the skin from `Models/wounds.png`: a 3 × 12 grid of 128 px cells, a row per kind (`Wounds.Cells`: stab, burn, bruise, cut, scratch, smeared blood, gunshot (two rows), running blood (two rows), then bandages: four bloodied, three clean), loaded once and freed from memory once on the GPU. Made from `Models/injuries2.png` (painted on white, at 256) by `anim_scripts/key_wounds.py Models/injuries2.png "M3AT: Maru's 3D Animated Toons/Models/wounds.png"` (colour to alpha; bandages, `BANDAGES`, by flood fill; gunshot spatter faded, `trim_spatter`; two blank gunshot cells made, `GUNSHOT_MADE`; `OUT_CELL`).
- Kind by the injury's def name (`KindOf`); place by its part, up the body to the first part with bones (`PlaceOf`, `room` for thin limbs; face features an `aim` and `scatter` by face texture place, `SkinnedFigure.FacePlace`, `EyeU`, `EyeV`, `NoseV`, `JawV`, an eye on the half FA draws it, `FaceLayers.EyesSwapped`; an ear at the head's widest, `SkinnedFigure.HeadMost`; skull and brain within `CrownScatter` of the top). Spot and turn from `loadID`, area-weighted on those bones' skin at rest (`SkinnedFigure.Spot`); size `Span` × √(severity / `SpanDamage`), clamped, in eighths (`Step`), so it shrinks as it heals.
- Bleeding: blood under it (`BloodSize`; running blood's top kept up the body). Tended: a bandage over everything (`BandageSize`; bloodied if `injuryProps.bleedRate` > 0). Scars: the sprite through the shader's scar filter (`SCAR_`: vertex alpha `ScarAlpha` < 1 carries the skin colour, the sprite's brightness alone kept, dark seams to pale skin); scarification the cut sprites at `ScarifiedStep`.
- On an eye (with FA's face; its bandage stays on the skin): `EyeWoundOf` gives a `FaceLayers.EyeWound` (`EyeWoundSpan`, `EyeWoundScatter`) drawn on the patch in mode 7 between the eyeball's and lid's layers, cut to that frame's iris and white, so it blinks with the eye.
- `SetWounds` lays all as one mesh: body triangles near the spot (its bone or a neighbour, facing within `WoundFacing`, not under a garment), UVs projected at rest, the cell's square in the second UV pair. A decal marked `onClothes` (not scar, scarification, bruise or bandage) is laid the same way on each `OnSkin` garment's triangles that follow the skin (`SkinOf`: corners and middle within `ClothOnSkin` of the body, facing within `ClothFacing`) and aren't under an outer garment (`OverOf`, `GarmentHidden`). Remade only when `SignOf` or the garment mask changes; `PoseWounds` moves it, `WoundLift` out. Drawn with `MarusFaceLayer` mode 6 (cut to the square by `vLocal`) at queue 2999.
- Hurt holds (`HurtOf`; `injuryAnims`): each place's fresh injuries (not scars; tended `HurtTended`; a freshly missing part its whole health) over the part's health (at least `HurtHealth`), the place up the body (`HurtPlaceOf`: an arm by manipulation tags, `Head`, `Neck`, the chest by `Ribcage`/`Sternum`/`Clavicle`/heart/lungs, else the belly; legs left to the limp; the side from the first left/right label). Checked every `LimpCheck` ticks; the worst place's `hurt_` clip laid on with `Over`: the arms fully once past `HurtFrom` (`HoldEase`), the hunch from `HurtFrom` to `HurtFull`; standing, moving or seated, not drafted, carrying or bending. It gives way to a tool.
