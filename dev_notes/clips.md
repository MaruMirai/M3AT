# Animation clips and the exporter

Steps 1–2 of the asset pipeline: `anim_scripts/walk.py` makes every clip on the Rigify metarig (`metarig`) in `Models/pawnHumanRig.blend`, and `anim_scripts/export_frames.py` writes them for the game. Distances in walk.py are in leg lengths unless said. The parameters of each clip are walk.py module globals named by a prefix (`SIT_`, `TOSS_`, ...); read walk.py for the detail. The game side of each clip: `dev_notes/motion.md`, `dev_notes/weapons.md`.

**When adding a new clip, enable it in the preview tool too** (`anim_scripts/preview.py`): an entry in `clip_items` (by `hasattr`/`getattr` on walk, so an older walk.py still loads), how it is posed (`loop_pose`'s `if what == ...`), its length in `loop_seconds` if on the clock, its section in `CLIP_SECTION` and category in `CATEGORY_OF`, and `OVERS` if the game lays it over the gait.

## Exporting

```
/home/t/Programs/blender-5.2.3-linux-x64/blender -b Models/pawnHumanRig.blend --python anim_scripts/export_frames.py -- "M3AT: Maru's 3D Animated Toons/Models/chibi" [--fit]
```
(`./export_model.sh` runs it with filtered output.) It writes:

- `anim.txt`: every clip as one rotation and head position per deforming bone per frame (`clip` lines with lengths in game seconds, `k` frames, `over` lines: each bone's share when laid over a gait, `walk.UPPER`, unlisted bones take their parent's, a free arm none), plus `holster`, `holster_*`, `weapon`, `weapon_hold`, `weapon_grip`, `weapon_type`, `type_scale`, `gear`, `tool`, `game_scale`, `aim_pitch`, timing and pair lines named below. Loops sampled at `IDLE_FPS`.
- `skin.txt`: the rest mesh, bone weights, body shape keys (`Male`, `Female`, `Thin`, `Fat`, `Hulk`, `Shrinkhead`, `Babylimbs`, `Pregnant`, `Armoured` (power armour's frame), gene keys...) as vertex moves, each joint's move (`db`, by tying each bone head to its nearest body vertices with weights that reproduce it, `anchor`), the face patch, the `scalp` and `hairline` lines, `nfix i x y z` lines (the body's `normal_fix` attribute; garments write theirs from the .glb's `_NORMAL_FIX`, per copy written; `export_frames.normal_fixes`). The body is `walk.BODY_PARTS` (`Pawn`, or `Body` and `Head` joined by `walk.join_body`, merging groups and keys by name, each part's vertices given a `walk.PART_GROUP` group). **The body must have no modifiers besides its Armature.**
- `garment_<name>.txt` and `.png` for every other mesh on the rig (below). Stale `garment_*` files are deleted (`GARMENT removed`).
- The old baked frames: `idle.obj` (`stand_pose`), `walk_00..23.obj`, `run1_00..run6_23.obj` (run blended in by sixths), `<folder>.mtl`, `clip.txt` (keys `frames`, `ground_per_cycle`, `run_levels`, `run_ground_per_cycle`, `crawl_`, `limp_`, `hop_`, `wade_`, `swim_ground_per_cycle`), and a `face_<frame>.obj` per frame.
- Also runs `make_horns.py`, `export_cat.py` (ears, tails, race parts), `fur_tail.py` and `hide_edges.py`.
- Checks: its own linear skinning against Blender's for every frame (`SKIN ... matches Blender within`); `--fit` (a couple of minutes, writes nothing) how far each garment sinks into the body in any frame (`FIT` lines, `check_fits`), and where: the deepest vertex's rest place (rig units) and the frame (since 2026-11-27; it found the shirts' back of the shoulder after the shoulder reshape).
- Files are in the game's model space: Blender's (x, y, z) as (-x, z, -y) for the .txt files (z already flipped), (-x, z, y) with reversed winding for the OBJs.

### Garments in the export

- Every mesh on the rig besides the body is a garment, as is each mesh in `Models/garments/*.glb` or a `.glb` named after the output folder (`import_garments`: put on the rig, a one-mesh file named after the file with a capital first letter, replacing one of that name in the .blend; imported with `disable_bone_shape`).
- Weights: the body's, blended from the point under each vertex. Own weights are kept only for `KEEP_WEIGHTS` (duster, robes, burka, lab coat, tribalwear, parka, cape, skirts, dresses, skeleton...). Of those, `BODY_CUT` (cut from the body's faces) get the body's weights again at each export for every vertex on the skin (within `BODY_CUT_BAND` of the usual offset, `body_cut_weights`, `BODY_CUT` lines), and the rest (a skirt, a hood) are moved by as much of the change as they carry of their nearest such vertex's weights. `GARMENT_PROPS` `part_weights`: a vertex keeps its own weights where it has any and takes the body's elsewhere. `solid_parts` names sets in `anim_scripts/solid_parts.json` (rest places): every vertex within `SOLID_REACH` of them weighted alike (`solid_weights`: the mean less `SOLID_NOT`, the arm, neck and head), so armour backpacks move as one piece (`marine_backpack`, `recon_backpack`, `cataphract_backpack`; matched by place). The `own_weights` custom property is no longer read.
- Seams (`SEAMS`, `{'Pants': 'Shoes'}`): a garment meeting another edge to edge takes, at each vertex within `SEAM_NEAR` of one of the other's at rest, the other's move in every key (its hand-made ones too); the other is written just before it (`write_order`), and the export prints a `SEAM` line. Each garment's fit is smoothed over its own neighbours (`filled()`, the push out of the skin), so rings that matched at rest drifted apart in Fat (0.013 cells) and Thin. `cuffseam` autotest.
- Keys: every body key fitted from the body surface under each vertex. A garment's own non-empty key wins, except `ALWAYS_FITTED` (Thin, Fat, Hulk; make scripts baked stale copies); `own_keys` keeps named ones. A fitted key on what hangs clear of the legs (`HANG_FROM`..`HANG_FULL` cells off over `HANG_BONES`, not the garment's arm weights `HANG_NOT`) is smoothed over the garment (`hung`, neighbours by place) and kept as far off the reshaped body as it stood (up to `HANG_KEEP`), as skirts folded in Fat. Then dents are filled (`filled`: a vertex whose move falls behind its neighbours' mean outward is lifted, never lowered). A vertex buried in the body at rest (more than `BURIED`, 0.005 cells, under the skin: the Parka's sleeve ends, started inside the trunk; inner rims of helmets) only follows the skin under it, never pushed out nor filled: the push used to put it out at its gap's distance in every key, even ones that don't touch it (Hands_Pig), so the Parka's sleeve ends came through its chest and turned over (holes, the skin showing in Hulk), and a Cultist mask vertex moved 0.14 cells in Female. `BURIED` lines list them per garment (2026-11-26). The `bridge` prop (`TribalA`) lifts the rest shape out of creases past `BRIDGE_SLACK` (`bridged`). `inside` (the skeleton): moved as the skin nearest, without the push back out past the reshaped skin. `fit babylimbs` with `hard`.
- Hiding: `hide` lines, the body triangles covered at rest (body vertices whose nearest garment point is within `HIDE_REACH` either side (a garment's `hide_reach` prop instead: the Parka's 0.1, as its quilting stands 0.04-0.07 off the trunk and most of the skin under it was drawn) and with the garment straight out along the skin's normal within `HIDE_LOOK`; `HIDE_RIDES_WITH` for one-bone garments). `hide_groups` (body vertex groups, comma separated) adds triangles with all corners in one at `HIDE_GROUP_WEIGHT`; `hide_body`/`hide_head` hide the `HIDE_BODY`/`HIDE_HEAD` part whole, and `hide_body` writes a `hide_clothes` line (or the `hide_clothes` property), so the game leaves off worn garments on a lower layer covering one of its groups (`SkinnedFigure.HidesClothes`). `hides` off writes none.
- `anim_scripts/hide_edges.py` (run by the export; by hand `blender -b --python anim_scripts/hide_edges.py -- <chibi folder> [garment ...] [--dry]`): for loose garments (a vertex some frame moves `DRIFT` (0.02 cells) or more from where the skin under it would carry it: own weights), re-tests the hidden triangles along the edge of what it hides in every 4th frame of every clip (points `INWARD` from each corner) and keeps drawn any some pose brings out, moving the edge in up to `ROUNDS` times; these become `opens` lines (read back as hidden). Fixed hiding (`hide_groups`, `hide_body`, `hide_head`) gets `hide_edges no`. Cached in `~/.cache/m3at-hide-edges/` by body, clips and file (all of them take ~7 minutes).
- `defs` (the apparel it is drawn for): `default_defs` makes `Apparel_<name>`; a name starting with one of `DEF_PREFIXES` (`CE_<name>` → `CE_Apparel_<name>`) or containing `Apparel_` as it is; or the `defs` property. `GARMENTS` (or `layer`/`groups` properties) gives the layer and body part groups for standing in, read only for a garment none of whose `defs` the game has. `STANDS_FOR` → `stands_for`, `like` → `like` lines (`dev_notes/apparel.md`).
- Texture: with a UV map and a Base Color image, `garment_<name>.png` (at most `TEXTURE_SIZE`, opaque unless `cutout`; identical images share a file, `write_texture`), a `t` line per vertex (split at UV seams; the game welds normals by place), an optional `tint` line.
- Other lines from `GARMENT_PROPS` or custom properties: `bone` (rides that bone whole), `hard`, `rigid`, `hide_hair` (`yes`, `no`, `tuck`), `hide_face`, `over_hair`, `sole` (measured, `SOLE` output), `pads`, `marine_of`, `held`/`held_at`/`held_grip`/`held_rim`/`standoff` (shields, `shield_held_at`, `SHIELD` output), `with_legwear`, `colour`, `baby_romper`, `hem_share` (skirts and dresses, `SKIRTS`, `DRESSES`, `HEM_ANCHORS`), `visor` (triangles on a material named `Visor`, `VISOR`, left out of the hide test), `unlined` (triangles on a material named `Unlined`, `UNLINED`, left out of the outline).
- Non-bone vertex groups (`Selecthead`...) are ignored.

## The clips

`walk.VARIANTS` gives a clip (and every clip named after it plus `_`) extra ways to be played, each a dict of walk.py numbers to pose with instead (`walk.varied`; a mocap clip's variant can name another clip json), written as `<clip>~2`, `<clip>~3`, ... with the clip's `over` line. So far: `sleep`, `flinch`, `bend`, `reel`, `land`, `jump`, `melee_slash` (backhand), `melee_stab` (low), `melee_fist` (hook), `knockdown`/`die` (the other `FALL_CLIPS`), `work_scythe` (the gather), `eat_sit` (knife and fork), `work_sow`, `lovin_L`/`R` (the caress), `talk`, `work_stomp` (the other foot). The preview's `Variant` slider poses them.

Keyed procedural clips use `walk.toss_key`/`loop_key`/`reel_key`/`dive_key`/`jump_keys` to join keys smoothly; `walk.keyed_at` keys a clip as `DRESS` is (hips, `pitch`, `lean`, `twist`, `look`, `tilt`, ankles, and palms placed from a landmark of the bent pose with facing, fingers and curl).

### Moving

- Walk/run: legs by 2-bone IK against a planted-foot path, converted to FK. `WALK` holds the defaults, `RUN` the overrides, `set_gait(g)` blends.
- `idle` (`walk.stand_at`, `IDLE_SECONDS`): breathing and weight shifts.
- `sit` (`sit_at`, `SIT_SECONDS`), `lie` (`lie_at`, on the back but made upright; the game lays lying clips down), `crawl` (`crawl_at`, upright tipped back `CRAWL_TIP`, the game's 75° lay-down; one loop of a hands-and-knees mocap crawl, `anim_scripts/crawl_clip.json`, else the old `belly_crawl_at`).
- `limp_L`/`R` (`limp_at`), `hop_L`/`R` (`hop_at`: that leg gone and held up, `HOP_STANCE`), `wade` (`wade_at`, `WADE_`), `swim` (`swim_at`, upright tipped `SWIM_TIP`: Meshy's `Swim_Forward`, `anim_scripts/swim_clip.json` from `import_pluck.py -- <glb> Swim_Forward --as swim --afloat`; else procedural `SWIM_FRAMES`), `tread` (`tread_at`, `TREAD_SECONDS`). All by ground covered except tread.
- `jump` (`jump_at`, `JUMP_FRAMES`, take-off to landing, keyed at take-off, top and landing: `JUMP_LEAN`/`_LOOK`/`_POINT`, `JUMP_OFF_`/`TOP_`/`LAND_`), `land` (`land_pose`, one crouch, `LAND_SECONDS`).
- `bend` (`bend_pose`, squat to the ground, whole), `bend_walk` (`bend_walk_pose`, folded at the waist, `BEND_WALK_UPPER`), `BEND_SECONDS`.
- `pool_climb` (`climb_at`, `CLIMB`, `CLIMB_WALL`, `CLIMB_FLOOR`), `pool_vault` (`vault_at`, `VAULT`, `VAULT_REACH`).

### Carrying and holding

## Motion capture (`anim_scripts/mocap/`; the steps in its `README.md`)

- `./record_mocap.sh [folder]` records from SteamVR (7 Vive trackers: head, chest, hips, elbows or knees (outside of the shins just below the knee) and feet, plus 2 wands; no headset) with `record.py`, one CSV per take into `animDLs/mocap/<date>/`. Start and stop: both wands together over the head, or with the wands taped to a tool, both held still over it for `GESTURE_STILL_HOLD` (1.5 s) (SteamVR gives a background app no button input). Spoken prompts and lost-tracker call-outs by `spd-say`. Own venv (`anim_scripts/mocap/.venv`, openvr, numpy). Trackers are told apart from each take's rest pose (`take.assign`: height and side, forward the way the wands point together, as the hips tracker may be worn at the front).
- `import_take.py` (`blender -b Models/pawnHumanRig.blend --python anim_scripts/mocap/import_take.py -- <take.csv> [from to] [--as name] [--loop shortest longest] [--once] [--tool --calibrate from to --hands right left] [--forward-tool] [--swap] [--wrist share]`) writes `<name>_clip.json` beside walk.py in `import_pluck.py`'s format: the stillest second near the start as `walk.stand_pose`, each tracker's turn since laid on its bone's standing turn (waist and neck halfway), the limbs by IK to the hips, ankles (leg lengths) and wrists (arm lengths from the shoulder) as the performer's, the knee across the ankle hinge, the elbow toward its tracker (with knee trackers, the knee toward a joint on its tracker and the elbow `ELBOW_WAY`); a lost tracker carried with its `ANCHOR`, a lost foot filled straight across (planted); the floor from the feet at rest (`FOOT_TRACKER_HEIGHT`), not SteamVR's; loops centred on the feet and closed.   - `--tool`: wands taped along a two-handed tool's handle (`tool_head`, `tool_grip`; forward from the hips tracker to the chest's). `--calibrate`: a stretch held upright with the point forward names the wands and the point (else from the downswing, `TOOL_EDGE_SPEED`); `--hands right left` puts each fist that far down the handle from its wand. The Line menu converts a hold to the `hand` line; the grip handle (`MaruWeaponGrip`) sets `at`, aim and roll. Aim clips: handles set the weapon's `AIMED` entry from the frame shown (`walk.aim_last`): G on the weapon hand `fist`, on the other `support`/`under`, on a bow's string hand the stage's `draw`/`nock`/`loose`; R `other_turn` (in the weapon's frame, `walk.aim_frame`; "Free the hand's turn" drops it); `AIM_NUMBERS` sliders; `Reset sliders` (`MARU_OT_aim_numbers_reset`). Strike clips play by Time from the guard (`STRIKE_SECONDS`), no key handles yet. `Slab holds`: `dev_notes/weapons.md`. `Shield weapon` menu.
- Holsters: a preview weapon in each slot (`hs_<slot>_weapon`, `draw_holsters`, `holster_frame`, placed as in the game), sliders and a handle (`MaruHolsterGrip`, `holster_moved`: G `at`, R `aim`/`roll`), `Mirror from <slot>` (`MARU_OT_holster_mirror`, `mirrored_place`, spun `HOLSTER_MIRROR_SPIN`); `hs_<slot>_filled`; picking a `pack` slot puts on what makes it used (`slot_picked`). Weapon types (`wt_<weapon>`, `ts_`/`th_`; `write_table` rewrites the three tables).
- Worn panel (`draw_worn`, `worn_now`): the export's garments (`wn_<garment>`, skinned in Python from `garment_<name>.txt` in the rig's key shape, a backpack as `Packed` has it, in place of the rig's own meshes, put back for a save) and the gear that's on; holsters, backpacks and gear placed by `anim_scripts/holster_push.py` (**a numpy copy of `Holsters.cs` and `WornGears.cs`'s placing: change it with them**), on the exported files, so it needs a new export to see a changed garment. Gear (`draw_gear`), helmets on the back (`hb_<kind>_`, `MaruHelmetGrip`), shields on the back or in the fist (`SHIELD_HELD`).
- Working section: `walk.tool_fist` sliders; on a work clip with a tool, `Adjust the fist on the handle` (ticked: the hands held as posed, `state['fist0']`, the tool moved in the right fist, `fist_held`, by the `MaruToolFist` handle, `place_tool_fist`/`tool_fist_moved`: G where the handle runs through the fist, R its slant).
- Expansions (`EXPANSION_API`): an `anim_scripts/preview_expansion.py` in any repository beside this one adds a category of its own clips, posed by that module with whoever it poses (`draw_others`: a partner at the anchor, gendered sizes, on a double bed's mattress), and its `NUMBERS_FILE` as sliders that Write puts back and Reload re-reads.
