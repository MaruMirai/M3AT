# Weapons

The held weapon, holsters, aiming and fighting on the skinned figure. The clips (`drafted_`, `aim_`, `melee_`, `draw_`, holster slots, weapon types) are made by `walk.py`: `dev_notes/clips.md`.

## Drafted stance

- The stance is taken whenever the game would show the weapon (`WeaponShows`: aiming or `CarriesOpenly`, the game's `CarryWeaponOpenly` less the duties and lord jobs that show it for a peaceful outsider (visitor, trader's guard) unless an enemy is near; `ShieldShows` the same). With bare hands, only while an awake, unfogged enemy is within `ThreatRange` (`ThreatNear`, every `ThreatCheck` ticks). Eased by `DraftEase`; laid over any gait by `Over`.
- Clip by the equipped weapon: `drafted_<defName>` (`WalkFigures.WeaponName`; a `_Unique` or `Bladelink` weapon takes the plain one's), else its kind's (`WalkFigures.KindOf`, per ThingDef: melee; a bow by name but not a crossbow; pistol/revolver by name or tag; a thrown grenade by tag; CE's `CE_OneHandedWeapon` tag (`OneHandedTag`); a gun not `RangedHeavy` drawn shorter than `PistolLongest` (0.7 cells along the barrel, `ArtLength`, a unique as its plain one) a pistol; any other gun a rifle; nothing equipped unarmed), then `drafted_rifle`, then an old export's `drafted`; crossfaded when the weapon changes.
- Slab stances: a weapon drawn as its sprite's slab (`WalkFigures.AsSlab`: models off or failed, none, or one that doesn't fit its sprite) takes `drafted_<name>_slab`, `aim_<name>_slab` (and `fire_`, `nock_`) and `weapon <name>_slab` (`SlabName`, `HoldOf(name, slab)`): its own, else its kind's, else the model's. These are walk.py's `SLAB_DRAFTED`/`SLAB_WEAPONS`/`SLAB_AIMED` (the holds from before they were redone for IsoRim's models), only where they differ, laid over the tables by `walk.slab_numbers()` at export (`SLAB` lines). Strikes and draws still start and end at the model's guard. The preview's `Slab holds` box edits them (`slab_tables`, `slab_mode`, `slab_split`, `write_slab`; ticking it drops unwritten edits).

## Drawing the weapon

- As IsoRim draws a held one: its 3D model if IsoRim has one and "Weapons as 3D models" is on (`WalkFigures.DrawModel`), else IsoRim's solid slab of the sprite (`Api.DrawHeldThing`, its picture scaled to the slab's placing).
- Model fitting (`Source/WeaponFit.cs`): IsoRim's `WeaponModels.Fit` lays the model into the sprite's slab so its outline covers the sprite's (so sprite holds still fit), plus the sprite's level axis tried beside IsoRim's narrowest-width one (which tipped pistols up) and a bigger `FrontBonus` (a minigun fitted backwards). Sized by the type scale on all three axes; the slab's thickness scaled as the sprite. The model is `Api.ReadThingModel(..., Held)`, its triangles read once from `Api.ModelParts`.
- The game's own weapons (Core or a DLC, a `Graphic_Single`) are fitted to the game's own art at their texPath (`OwnArt`: DLC content, then Core, passing over another mod's picture at the same path), as the preview places them. Grenades by their own art (`WalkFigures.FitArt`, `GrenadeArt`) and scaled to their ground size first (`WeaponFit.AtGroundSize`).
- Outline: held and holstered weapons share the figure's one outline (`SkinnedFigure.DrawJoined`, `Source/OutlineOrder.cs`: IsoRim's meshes from `Api.ModelParts`, the solids plus a `MarusStencilSet` pass setting bit 64 in the figure's slot, the shell on `MarusOutline` and its light pass, the slot's clear over both). With the silhouette or outline options off, and for a flung weapon, `Api.DrawModel` with its shell pushed `Api.OutlinePush`. Screen-space: `DrawJoinedScreen` (`dev_notes/options.md`).
- Placing: from the hand joints (`SkinnedFigure.JointOf`) by the hold (`walk.WEAPONS`; the fist `walk.HAND_GRIP` from the wrist, `WalkFigures.FistOf`). On the `hand` line (every kind now) it rides the holding hand bone (`SkinnedFigure.BoneOf`): aim, drop and roll at the hand's rest turn, the fist along the hand (`weapon_grip` lines, `GripOf`), so a hand's `<side>_turn` aims it and `at` moves the grip in the hand's frame; then `WEAPON_LIFT`/`_OUT`/`_FORWARD` along the figure. Written as `weapon`/`weapon_hold` lines (`SkinnedFigure.HoldOf`; old exports `WalkFigures.oldHolds`). The sprite's `equippedAngleOffset` is undone first. The preview's Line menu converts a hold; its grip handle (`MaruWeaponGrip`) sets `at`, aim and roll.
- Aiming, the figure faces the aim (the placing's) and `Api.ReportMuzzle` gets the barrel end (the model's tip; a slab's by `WeaponFit.SpriteBarrelEnd`), so shots leave the barrel as drawn. It follows the hands, not the target's height.

## Weapon types and sizes (`Source/WeaponTypes.cs`)

- `TypeOf` (per ThingDef): the export's `weapon_type` by `WeaponName`; else `grenade` for anything thrown (`WalkFigures.Thrown`: the vanilla verb's throw log, or CE's `CE_AI_Grenade`/`CE_Pila` tags); else a melee weapon `melee_1h` if CE tags it one-handed (`OneHandedByTag`) or under `HeavyMelee` kg, else `melee_2h`; else its `KindOf`'s type.
- `ScaleOf` (held and holstered, `DrawSlab`'s scale): the type's size in the options (`Settings.weaponScales`), else the export's `type_scale` (against the ground size, 1 for every type), divided by that weapon's `GroundRatio` (its model's fit to its sprite over IsoRim's `ModelDef` scale; 1 without a model or thrown), else `WeaponScale`. A slab is also times `slabScale` (`dev_notes/options.md`). The options' defaults come from `TypeDefaults`. It is also times the pawn height option's share of the standard (`WeaponSizeOf`, `dev_notes/options.md`).
- A grenade the export doesn't name (`WalkFigures.Grenade`: thrown and in `Grenades` or CE-tagged) takes `Weapon_GrenadeFrag`'s name, so its stance, hold, type and throw; `WeaponRule` answers true for a grenade bound only by category (`Api.HowBound`'s `category:`), so CE's grenades get IsoRim's model.
- `WeaponRule` (`Api.AddWeaponModelRule`) and `retexturedWeaponModels`, `RestoredModels`: `dev_notes/options.md`.

## Holsters (`Source/Holsters.cs`; `holsters` option, on, and only with IsoRim's held weapons on)

- `DrawHolstered`, after the dressed figure and the load, not lying, crawling or swimming: the equipped weapon unless in the hands, then each inventory `Primary`, each in the first free slot (`holster` lines, in order) of its size (`HolsterSizeOf`, its type's holster); none if no slot is free. `TakeSlot` keeps the equipped weapon's slot while it is in the hands.
- `DrawSlab` (shared with `DrawWeapon`) from `SkinnedFigure.HolsterAt`: the slot's rest place moved with its bone's head for the shape, pushed along `out` by `Clearance` (the outermost body or worn garment surface on that line, `Outer`: a triangle it passes through or a vertex within `HolsterReach`, `HolsterDepth` in or out, less the bare base body's; a big slot also along the weapon, `BigHalfLength` either way, counting only what comes up past its stand-off; cached in `Shape.holsterPush`).
  - Left out of `Clearance`: `HOLSTER_PACKS` garments, shields, and the webbing of `NoClearanceDefs` (bandolier, CE's tactical vest, scarf; `UnclearMask`). A back slot lies on a pack by `HolsterClear`, `PackOuter`.
  - Pack sides (`HolsterFits`, `OnPackSides`: with both a `HOLSTER_PACKS` garment and an equipment pack): `back`/`back_2` give way to `pack_R`/`pack_L`, placed by `SkinnedFigure.PackSide` against the pack as drawn (`IsoRimPackSide`, `SideSurface`) plus `HalfDepth`. Belt (`OnBeltSlots`, IsoRim's `belt` mount drawn and the left arm there): `grenade_L`/`grenade_R` give way to `grenade_arm`/`grenade_arm_2`.
  - With one pack, a big slot on its bone and way out (`LiesOnPack`) is raised to its middle (`PackLift`, `IsoRimPackMiddle`, `OverPackMiddle`). A big slot is pushed past the weapon in an earlier big slot on the same bone and way (`OverOthers`, `HolsterOver`, by `HalfDepth`: the model's `WeaponFit.HalfDepth`, else the slab's). Then moved by `p.whole` of its bone.
- A slot is posed for its type's placeholder (`walk.HOLSTER_WEAPON`, `holster_weapon` lines, `SkinnedFigure.HolsterWeapon`). A grenade of another name with a `hand`-line hold (`HolsterAsHeld`) is laid where the fist holding the placeholder would hold it (`WalkFigures.AsHeld`, `SlotPlace`; also a draw's start; the preview's `as_held`).
- Melee slots: `holster_melee` lines (a blade at `walk.MELEE_SLOT_OFFSET`; other offsets turned by the difference, `WalkFigures.SlotLine`).
- **`anim_scripts/holster_push.py` is a numpy copy of this placing for the preview: change it with `Holsters.cs` and `WornGears.cs`.**
- Hands kept out of holstered weapons (`Source/HandsClear.cs`, `HandsClear`, after `Chain`): off (it looked restless); `M3AT_HANDCLEAR` turns it on. Each hand's wrist, knuckles and fingertips (`HandRadius` discs) against each weapon holstered last draw (`drawnHolsters`, `Ease.holstered`, placed again by `SlotPlace`/`SlabMatrix`): its art's solid pixels (`ArtOf`, `ArtCells`) across its face and `HalfDepth` out; moved the shortest way out (`PointOut`), the arm bent by `Reach`, eased (`Ease.clearR`/`clearL`, `ClearRate`), never while drawing. `handholster` autotest.

## Drawing and holstering (`Holsters.cs`)

- When the game starts or stops showing the weapon (`Ease.handHeld`, `WeaponShows`), `StepDraw` plays its `DrawClipOf` (the first usable slot of its size, `OnPackSides`, and its `TypeOf`) forward or back by game time, picking up where it got to if reversed; not with a load, in a blow, in the air, swimming or off its feet. Sped up only if it wouldn't end `DrawAhead` before the shot (`ShotIn`); a shot already fired ends it.
- Laid on with `Top` (`DrawWeight`, `DrawIn`, `DrawOut`); meanwhile `Ease.drafted` follows its progress, so the other arm comes up as the weapon arrives. `DrawWeapon` moves the weapon from slot to hand by `DrawnShare` (`HolsterAt`, by `draw_slide` up to `DrawGrab`), covering lengths, coats, packs and children the clip wasn't made for.
- A weapon swapped, or out of the inventory to reload, is not drawn from anywhere.
- Anything else wanting the hands (`EaseOf`'s `handsWanted`: work, eating, talking, cowering, leading, a helmet going on; work it will reach within the holster time too, `WorkOf`'s `coming`, `HolsterBefore`; not while aiming, striking, reloading, for a wound or the cold) holsters it first and draws again after, the rest easing in once it's away (`putAway`; not a cower). Already at the work (`wantedNow`), the holster is over within `HolsterRush`.

## Aiming

- While the placing aims (`FigurePlacement.Aiming`; not carrying), `AimName` (per ThingDef: `aim_<WeaponName>`, else `aim_<KindOf>`, else none: melee, grenades) is eased in by `AimEase` (`LayAim`): whole in place of the drafted stance standing still, `Over` from the waist up moving or seated.
- Frame by IsoRim's pitch (`FigureWeapons.PitchOf`, private, by reflection; from `AimHeight` × size), never wrapping.
- Kick (`KickOf`: from the verb's `LastShotTick` over `recoilRelaxation`, at least `KickTicks`, as strong as `recoilPower` from `KickLeast`) blends in `fire_`.
- Bows: drawn from `nock_` to `aim_` over the warm-up (`DrawOf`: `Stance_Warmup.startedTick`, `ticksLeft`; a warm-up within `DrawHoldGap` of full draw with no shot since, CE's second one, holds at full draw, `Ease.bowFullTick`/`bowHeldFrom`), loosed into `fire_` over `LooseTicks`, nocked again between shots.
- Standing still, the facing is hurried past the tracker's (`TurnedFor`, `Ease.turnYaw`) only if it wouldn't get there before the shot (`ShotIn`, `ReadyIn`), or by `TurnLate` if already firing (`dev_notes/motion.md`, Turning).
- Muzzle flash (`Source/MuzzleFlash.cs`; `muzzleFlash`, on): `DrawMuzzleFlash`, from `DrawWeapon` while aiming, draws a tongue of fire along the barrel (turned to face the camera) and a star at its end for `FlashSeconds` of real time after each shot (a new `LastShotTick` within `FlashLateTicks`), with the `ShotFlash` fleck's glow material at `FlashQueue`; only for a verb with `muzzleFlashScale` (sized by its square root against a rifle's). The barrel end is the model's `tip`, else `Api.TryGetMuzzle` (Api 44).

## Striking

- `StrikeOf`, while the stance is busy with a melee verb or a thrown weapon's (`Thrown`). Melee by maneuver (`Slash` slash, `Stab`/`Poke` stab, else smash; the weapon's own clip first); a fist by the tool's body part group side; a ranged weapon by its kind, own clip first (`melee_jab_<kind>` for `Poke`, else `melee_butt_<kind>`).
- A blow at a target already down or lying (`LowBlows`, a Harmony prefix on `Verb.TryCastNextBurstShot`, so CE's too, looked at before it lands) takes the clip's `melee_down_` version.
- The game picks the blow as it lands, so it is played from `LastShotTick`: to `StrikeHit` (`walk.STRIKE_AT`) over `WindTicks`, back over `RecoverTicks`. A throw (`throw_R`; `throw_pack` for one held in the left hand, `PackTake` = `PACK_TAKE['at']`) is cocked over the warm-up and followed through over `ThrowRecover`; thrown weapons aren't aimed.
- Laid on like the aim (whole standing still, `Over` moving) by `Swing`, none at the guard, so each weapon's drafted stance shows between blows. Takes the drafted stance (bare hands in a social fight, `WalkFigures.SocialFighting`) and faces the target (`strikeFace`).

## Hip gear (`Source/HipGear.cs`, with worn gear on)

Psychic lances, orbital targeters, the tornado generator (IsoRim's `hip` mount; the clips hold it from the right shoulder slot). Taken in the left hand only while used (`HipVerb`: the stance busy with a verb whose `DirectOwner` is that apparel's `CompApparelVerbOwner`), on its feet with nothing else in hand (`hipCan`; else back to the slot). `StepHip` moves `Ease.hipOut` through `hip_draw` by game time and eases `hipAim` (`HipAimEase`); `LayHip` lays `hip_draw`, `hip_hold`, `hip_aim` by IsoRim's pitch and `hip_fire` from `LastShotTick` (`HipKick`, `HipKickTicks`) with `Top`, giving way to reload, cast, stagger and daze. `DrawWornGear` draws it `HipShare` of the way from its slot (`GearAt`) to the hand (`SkinnedFigure.HipHeldAt`, the `tool hip` line), blended by `Between`. Faces the target (`hipFace`); the weapon is neither aimed nor brought out (IsoRim's `AimOf` takes any busy stance). Held, it takes the left arm off a two-handed weapon.

## Held arms clear of the trunk (`Source/ArmsClear.cs`)

- `ArmsOut` first pushes a stance's held hands forward by how far body and garments stand out past the bare chest.
- `SkinnedFigure.ArmsClear` then keeps the held arms out of a bigger trunk (Hulk, Fat, strength over 1, thick clothes): `ArmRoom` (per shape and garment mask) is how far out the trunk reaches per band (`ArmRoomStep`) and way (`ArmRoomTurns`) round the chest bone's upright line at rest (trunk skin; the shoulders' only front and back, `ShoulderSides`), against the bare base body's (`baseRoom`). An arm point sunk more than `ArmLeast` further than on the base body moves out: the elbow swung about the shoulder-to-wrist line (`Reach`'s `pole`), then both wrists together, only forward along the chest (`WristsForwardOnly`; the old `WristsByUpperArm` slid weapons up in armour), for what is still in (`WristCarries`), `ArmRounds` times, at most `ElbowOutMost`/`WristOutMost` a round. Nothing moves on the base, Male or Female body.
- `armourgrip` autotest: bare Male, Hulk and Fat (strength 1.5) and the Male in power, cataphract and recon armour, drafted with an assault rifle, with and without (`WalkFigures.ArmsOutOn`, `ArmsClearOn`, `WristsByUpperArm`, `WristsForwardOnly`, AUTOTEST only). `hulkarms`: Male, Hulk man and Hulk woman with a rifle, pistol and knife at strengths 1 and 1.5 (`<config>/M3AT/no_armsclear` turns it off).

## Grips found in the art (`Source/GripFinder.cs`; `autoGrips`, on)

- A gun or melee weapon with no hold of its own (another mod's; held by `rifle` or `melee`) is slid along its art (`WalkFigures.GripSlide`, in `DrawWeapon`, in the art's own axes, `SpriteAxes`; not in the holster) so the grip `GripFinder.Find` finds in its alpha (128 px, per texture) comes to the fist where the grip found on the kind's own sprite (`GripReference`: the assault rifle, the knife, at `OwnArt`) is under that hold (`FistOnSprite`).
- A gun is levelled by its `equippedAngleOffset`; the underside is the highest cut leaving the most parts hanging; the grip the rearmost part not the stock (followed up its slant), else the stock's thinnest column. A melee weapon (head right): the narrowest stretch from the left end, the fist in its middle, near the guard two-handed, 40% along a long shaft. Pistols, bows and anything unclear keep the kind's hold.
- The other hand along a gun (`GripFinder.FindFore`, `detect.py`'s `fore`; used by `WalkFigures.ForeShift`, above): a fore grip if a narrow, deep part hangs below the body past half its length (`ForePast`, `ForeWide`, `ForeDeep`: a vertical grip, a launcher's front handle), else `ForeShare` (0.65) of the gun's visible length from its back. Shape rules (the handguard up to where the underside steps up into the barrel, a share of the way from the grip to where the body thins) scored worse than the plain share, as the assault rifle's thin barrel threw them. `fore_score.py` scores it against `offtruth.json` (each vanilla two-handed gun's other fist and holding fist on its art in its own drafted stance, the `weapons_` notes' "grip tool's drafted-stance fists", parsed from a run): along the gun, a median 6.3 px of 128 and worst 14 off, against 11.4 and 32 for the assault rifle's distance from the holding fist. Only the triple rocket launcher shows a fore grip among the game's guns.
- **`anim_scripts/grip_detect/detect.py` is the Python it was made from: change the two together.** `truth.py` (in Blender with the rig, `-- truth.json`) puts each vanilla weapon's slab hold on its sprite; `score.py` scores against them (median 0.079 cells with the kind's hold, 0.041 slid); `modsheet.py <mod folder> <out.png>` draws a mod's weapons (`moddefs.py`) with the fist before and after. `weapons_` autotest notes each slide (`AutoGripSlide`).

## Grip tool (`Source/GripTool.cs`)

- The player sets where the fist holds any weapon on its art and its turn, per defName (`Settings.gripOverrides`: x, y on the art, 0..1, y up, x < 0 leaves the place; z degrees; not touched by "Restore all defaults", cleared by its own button). `DrawWeapon` turns the weapon about the fist (`GripOverrideOf`, in the art's plane, + tips the right end toward the top), then `GripSlide` puts the fist on the place, before the auto grip and the weapon's own hold, for slab and model alike.
- The other hand too (`Settings.offHandGrips`; Main hand / Other hand): `GripOffHand`, between `Chain` and `Finish` beside `GripCarried`, places the weapon as `DrawWeapon` will (`PlaceWeapon`, `WeaponPlaced`), finds that point of its art and bends the arm to it (`SkinnedFigure.Reach` keeping the hand's turn, then `Reached`), weighted by how far the weapon is in the hands, let go while reloading, a shield or hip gear is up, casting, striking, staggered, dazed or diving; not for a `hands`-line hold or with a load.
- With no point set, the other hand is still kept on the weapon (`HoldSupport`, `Source/SupportHand.cs`). The stances were made on the bare base body, so on a shape with moved joints each arm carried its own hand out, and the weapon rides the holding hand: on a Hulk the left hand came off the rifle along the barrel and over its top (0.07 model units off at Hulk 1.0, 0.11 at 1.5). `SkinnedFigure.SupportOf` poses the stance's first frame on the base body once per clip and side, keeps the other wrist and turn in the holding hand's frame, and puts them back there on any shape (fingers turned with the hand). It only does this when the base pose has that fist within `SupportOnLine` (0.08) of the weapon's line, so a free hand keeps its swing. Where the other arm can't reach (a Hulk 1.5's shoulders are 0.17 out, so it needs 0.33 against an arm of 0.29), the holding hand and the weapon are brought toward that shoulder by what it lacks, so the arm is stretched to at most `SupportReach` (0.97) of its length. Checked with `./autotest.sh armourgrip --stills`, which also has a `none` run (ArmsOut and ArmsClear off) and notes `HandGapOf`, the closed fist against the skinned hand in the hand's frame. That was the same on every body to within 5 mm, so the holding hand was never the problem.
- Kept on the same part of the weapon at any size (`SupportShift`, in `HoldSupport` before `SkinnedFigure.HoldSupport`): the stance was set on one weapon (`DesignWeapon`: the stance's own, `drafted_<defName>`, else its kind's `KindLook`, preview.py's `KIND_LOOK`) at its type's size in the export (`DesignDrawSize`; a slab at `DefaultSlabScale`). The other fist's distance along the weapon from the holding fist is scaled by the drawn length over that weapon's (clamped to `SupportScaleLeast`..`SupportScaleMost`); how far beside or under the line it is stays. So a type size or slab share changed in the options, or weapons taking the pawn's size, no longer leave the hand at the old distance (at rifle size ×1.4 the hand was drawn at u 0.619 on the assault rifle's art and 0.700 on the sniper's, against 0.624 and 0.686 at ×1; left alone it would have been at 0.542 and 0.586). A gun held by its kind's stance (another mod's) instead has the hand moved along it to its own fore grip as the stance has it at the kind's gun's (`ForeShift`, `GripFinder.FindFore`, `autoGrips`), along the weapon only: the hand's height against the line is the stance's. `TurnFromNow` gives the fist where the stance has it before the arm is bent there. AUTOTEST: the `weapons_` notes give `AutoSupportShift` (which way, and the fist from and to on the art); `bigweapons_<names>` runs them at every type's size ×1.4 (`WithTypeSizes`).
- UI: the "M3AT tools" play settings toggle (`PlaySettingsPostfix` on `PlaySettings.DoPlaySettingsGlobalControls`; `gripToolButton` shows it, `gripTool` its state; icon `Textures/UI/M3AT_GripTool.png`) gives a colony pawn with a weapon an Adjust grip button (`GizmosPostfix` on `Pawn.GetGizmos`) opening `GripToolWindow`: the art, click or drag to set, the yellow mark the fist as last drawn (`GripSeen`, `FistOnArt`), a turn slider, saved on mouse up; Restore default drops both entries.
- AUTOTEST: `M3AT_GRIP_TEST=u,v,turn` and `M3AT_OFFHAND_TEST=u,v` hold every weapon so for a run, checked by the `weapons_` notes (`AutoOffFist`).

## Cover, hunkering, reloading

- Taking cover (`CoverOf`): standing still in a job CE crouches a pawn in (`CrouchJob`: `JobDefExtensionCE.isCrouchJob` by reflection; without CE `Wait_Combat` and `AttackStatic`; never `FleeAndCower`, `Stabilize`, `TendSelf`), with a ranged weapon and an awake, unfogged enemy in sight (`EnemyInSight`: `GenSight.LineOfSightToThing` within range, every `CoverCheck` ticks, kept `CoverHold` ticks), the hips of whatever standing pose it has are let down and back over its planted feet, the knees bent by IK (`SkinnedFigure.Crouch`, `LetDown`, in `Chain` after the balance; `CrouchDrop`/`CrouchBack`/`CrouchSplay` = walk.py's `COVER_` numbers; the preview's `walk.crouch_legs`), eased by `CoverDown`/`CoverUp`; not moving, carrying or at work. The hips keep their turn, so the aim points as standing (the exported `cover` clip isn't played). Depth: all the way in the open, else just enough for the drafted hands (`HeadOf` the stance's first frame) to clear the tallest low cover within 8 cells by `CoverClear` (`CoverTop`, `Api.SurfaceHeight`), not CE's share of the body, as the chibi is drawn taller beside cover than CE counts a person.
- No dive from explosives: the game's only reaction (vanilla and CE) is a sprint of ~9 cells (`mindState.knownExploder`, `JobGiver_FleePotentialExplosion`), so the figure only runs. Under CE suppression may make a pawn hunker, the only drop drawn.
- Hunkering: during CE's `HunkerDown` (`Hunkering`, by defName) the figure is drawn on the standing path though CE sets a lying posture: it dives flat (`dive_<hand>`, `DuckOf` moving `Ease.duck` by game time, back up at `DropRise`; `DuckDown`/`DuckUp` on an export without the dive), then `duck_<hand>` from `DropLoop`, whole in place of the rest (`land`), let down onto its lowest point from `DropRest`, facing `CompSuppressable.SuppressorLoc` (`SuppressorOf`). `DrawWeapon` lays the weapon along the ground (`WeaponLaidFrom`), still in its hand (`HandOf`). Downed or killed there it goes limp from that pose (`limpPose`). `ExtinguishSelf` (`Rolling`) takes the same path with `roll` as its loop (`Ease.duckLoop`). Not yet checked in game.
- Reloading: during CE's `ReloadWeapon` (`Reloaded`, by defName) the weapon reloaded (equipped or inventory) is the one in the hands (`HeldWeapon`, which `DraftedClip`, `WeaponShows`, `DrawWeapon` and `DrawHolstered` go by), `WeaponShows` holds, and `reload` is laid on with `Top` (`ReloadEase`), once through each of CE's timed wait toils (`ReloadOf`: `ticksLeftThisToil` against the most seen; per round for round-at-a-time weapons).
