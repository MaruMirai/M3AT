# Garment make scripts

Scripts that make or mend garments, body keys, ears and other meshes in Blender (or numpy). Unless said, `<blend>` is `Models/pawnHumanRig.blend` (or a copy), outputs go to `Models/garments/`, and a garment is then exported by `export_frames.py` (`dev_notes/clips.md`). "Run Script" means the Text Editor's Run Script in the open session. Blender: `/home/t/Programs/blender-5.2.3-linux-x64/blender`.

**The .glb files in `Models/garments/` are often hand-edited after a `make_*.py` script wrote them, and many are not in git, so re-running a script over one loses those edits for good.** Before re-running a make script whose output exists, copy that .glb aside (e.g. `Models/unused/<name>_before.glb`) or ask. A .glb newer than the script's last run has likely been edited. Don't take a script's output as the source of truth. Known hand edits: the glasses, gorget, loincloth, exoframe, shields, skirts' masters, the phoenix launcher, and the armours' soles.

Scripts that build on another garment as it is (re-run after the source changes): `make_sunglasses.py` (glasses), `make_radio_pack.py` (CE backpack), `make_formal_shirt.py` (collared shirt), `make_locust.py`/`make_mechlord.py` (recon armour), `make_grenadier.py` (marine armour; `--phoenix` the cataphract), `make_mech_helmets.py` (recon helmet), `make_prestige_helmets.py` and `prestige_paint.py`/`swap_garment_image.py` (the plain armours and helmets), `make_underwear.py`'s tank top (crop top, shirt), `make_baby_romper.py` (kid romper), `RobeRoyal` (robe), `paint_cloth.py` (after `make_from_groups.py`/`make_underwear.py`/`make_skirts.sh` remake). Since the soles were stretched (`armour_soles.py`), scripts starting from those armours start from the stretched ones.

## From the body's own faces

- `anim_scripts/make_from_groups.py` (`blender -b <blend> --python anim_scripts/make_from_groups.py -- <out folder> [garment ...] [--blend out.blend]`): a garment from each of the body's dashed vertex groups (`-Pants`; `-CollarShirt -Jacket -FlakJacket` all three from the same faces): the group's faces pushed out along the normals by the apparel layer (`LAYER_OFFSET`; `OWN_OFFSET` for one set apart), the body's UVs (`unwrap_pawn.py`'s atlas) kept, its weights and keys rebuilt from the keyed body, `defs` from `GARMENTS`. One with its own script (`OWN_SCRIPT`: TribalA) or its own mesh in the .blend is left alone. Each goes to `<out folder>/<Name>.glb` skinned to the rig.
  - `BELOW`: what a group leaves bare below it: `Shoes` (below `-Pants`' cuffs, `OWN_OFFSET` 0.15; worn with legwear, `dev_notes/apparel.md`).
  - `CUT` (bmesh on a body copy: `body_copy`, `bisect`, `onto_skin`; new vertices take their edges' weights, keys, UVs): `Socks`, `Boots` (the shoes' top ring extruded `BOOT_UP`, pushed `BOOT_GAP` past `Pants.glb`), `Sandals` (sole rim and straps, `SANDAL_`; `hides` off), `Gloves` (to a plane through the next ring, `GLOVE_NEAR`). The shoes are the plain footwear stand-in (`Generic`); boots, sandals and socks `NoStandIn` by `NameAlike`. `footwear` autotest (`M3AT_ADD_MODS="Techmago.BootsnStuff"`).
  - `WHOLE`: every face of the `Body` part: the `Vacsuit`, at the Shell layer's offset.
- `anim_scripts/make_shirt.py` (`blender -b <blend> --python anim_scripts/make_shirt.py -- <out.glb> [out.blend]`): a plain T-shirt from the rest body cut by planes (`HEM`, `NECK_BACK`/`NECK_DIP`, `SLEEVE`, `ARM_REACH`), smoothed and pushed `OFFSET` out, `THICKNESS` > 0 solidifies. Output in `Models/bases/` (a base to model from; the game has no T-shirt).
- `anim_scripts/make_pants.py` (same arguments): plain trousers (`WAIST`, `RISE_BACK`, `LENGTH`), named `Pants`, kept in `Models/bases/` (it would replace the drawn `Pants`).
- `anim_scripts/make_vacsuit.py` (same arguments): an earlier vacsuit (`Models/unused/VacsuitPushed.glb`), cut only at the neck (`COLLAR_BACK`, `COLLAR_DIP`), `OFFSET` easing to `HAND_OFFSET`/`FOOT_OFFSET` from `CUFF_FROM`, gloves and boots unsmoothed (`GLOVE_SHARE`).
- `anim_scripts/make_underwear.py` (`blender -b Models/pawnHumanRig.blend --python anim_scripts/make_underwear.py -- <out folder> [garment ...] [--blend out.blend] [--plain]`): UnderWhere's sets (`Malistaticy.UnderWhere`, Workshop 1870010563), kept to the body's faces and atlas where it can (planes cut by edge loops within `ON_PLANE`, `fine`/`exact` for narrow straps, `lean` dissolving and welding within `WELD`, pushed out along smooth normals at `OFFSET`, `clear_corners`; numbers per garment in `PANTIES`, `BOXERS`, ...). Most were replaced by the briefs, bandeau, crop top, shorts and short shorts cut from the trousers and shirt; still exported from it: `LongJohns` (`LONG_JOHNS_GROUPS`, whole), the plain `Bra`, and from textured sources (`SOURCES`, `source`, `made_from`): `Tanktop` (`CropTop.glb` with `BasicShirt.glb` below its hem joined on, `crop_lengthened`, `SOURCE_WELD`, `ON_EDGE`) and `Loincloth` (`Underwear.glb` with flaps, `waist_line`, `FLAP_PATCH`, `FLAP_THIGHS`, `part_weights`). Also `Leotard` (`LEOTARD`), `Stockings` (`STOCKINGS`) and `Tights` (`TIGHTS`) at `OFFSETS`' 0.006 leg lengths (`NoStandIn` with `Hints`; Kiiro's by `STANDS_FOR`). **The loincloth was edited by hand since; don't re-run it over that**: `anim_scripts/reweight_loincloth.py` (`blender -b Models/pawnHumanRig.blend --python anim_scripts/reweight_loincloth.py -- <edited.glb> <out.glb>`) weights an edit saved without the rig (on `Underwear.glb`'s surface within `ON_BRIEFS` the briefs, the rest each flap, hips to thighs). Unweighted save: `Models/unused/Loincloth_edit_unweighted.glb`.
- `anim_scripts/paint_long_johns.py` (`blender -b Models/pawnHumanRig.blend --python anim_scripts/paint_long_johns.py -- <in.glb> <out.glb> [image.png]`): a waffle-knit union suit painted by rest place (`RIBS`, `WAFFLE_ALONG`, ribbed cuffs and neck from the open edge loops of a copy welded by place, a henley placket `PLACKET_LONG` (long, as the chin hides its top), a drop seat `SEAT`), near white.
- `anim_scripts/mirror_cut.py` (`blender -b Models/pawnHumanRig.blend --python anim_scripts/mirror_cut.py -- <out.blend> [mesh]`, or Run Script, unsaved): copies a loop or knife cut on one side of the `Body` to the other (each mirrored edge split at the same share, places, keys (flipped in x), weights (.L for .R), UVs and seams interpolated; a new vertex is in a dashed group only if both edge ends are, as `make_from_groups.py` takes any weight above 0 as in).
- `anim_scripts/unwrap_pawn.py` (`blender -b Models/pawnHumanRig.blend --python anim_scripts/unwrap_pawn.py -- <report folder> [--save]`, or Run Script, unsaved): unwraps `Body` and `Head` onto one atlas with no overlap: seams from the bones (front and back, each ear whole on the front by place (`EAR_OUT`, `EAR_BACK`, `EAR_UP`), limbs cut along their hidden side, hands and feet two pieces each), the right the left's mirror, front and back panels exactly symmetric, the head at `HEAD_DENSITY`. Writes back only UVs and seams. Run again after a remesh. `skin.txt` has no body UVs yet.

## Body and head keys

- `anim_scripts/make_nose_pig.py` (Run Script, or `blender -b <blend> --python anim_scripts/make_nose_pig.py -- <out.blend>`): the head's `Nose_Pig` key: the nose drawn straight forward onto a flat end (`END_Y`), only forward, so the face patch's front projection still lines up.
- `anim_scripts/make_no_human_ears.py` (Run Script, or `... -- <out.blend>`, `--over` to save over the opened .blend): the head's `No_Human_Ears` key: each ear's edge loop on the skull (`RING`) eased `RING_EASE`, what it shuts off laid across it at its neighbours' mean, a low dome (`DOME`).
- `anim_scripts/make_fat.py` (`./make_fat.sh <in.blend> <out.blend> <offset> [hands] [steps] [smooth]`): the body's `Fat` key, inflated in steps smoothing each step's move from the base shape (so detail rides along), the hands taking `HANDS` by their weight on `HAND_BONES`, on every `walk.BODY_PARTS` part but the `Head`; never saved over its input.
- `anim_scripts/widen_hulk_arms.py` (`blender -b Models/pawnHumanRig.blend --python anim_scripts/widen_hulk_arms.py -- <out.blend> [--key Hulk] [--out leg lengths] [--hand share] [--smooth passes] [--measure]`): fits the arms to a key that widens the trunk: `--out` moves arm vertices out by their arm weight (the shoulder's at half), `--hand` keeps that share of each hand's growth about its wrist, `--smooth` eases the forearm's move; each adds, so run once. Run on the Hulk 2026-11-23/24 (`--out 0.1`, then `--hand 0.65 --smooth 8`). `--measure` prints the arm-to-chest gap per height.
- `anim_scripts/mirror_keys.py` (Run Script adds "Mirror Keys from -x/+x" to the Shape Keys menu, or `blender -b <in.blend> --python anim_scripts/mirror_keys.py -- +x|-x <out.blend>`): mends keys after Symmetrize (which carries moves across without flipping x).
- `anim_scripts/make_hairline.py`, `hairline_to_mesh.py`: `dev_notes/hair.md`.

## Hats and head wear (`anim_scripts/make_hats.py`)

`blender -b <blend> --python anim_scripts/make_hats.py -- <out folder> [hat ...] [--blend out.blend]`. Each entry of `HATS` is made from the head's own shape, lengths in head radii, low-poly (`SEGMENTS` round, about 180–240 triangles; a `Shape` may set its own count), one surface, each file named for its apparel (a name starting with two capitals keeps its first letter). Plain hats: a snug shell cast out from the head's middle (`snug`) or a crown of rings (`crown`, `profile`) on an oval band (`oval`) with brims (`flare`), no UVs. A hat with a `palette` (`Shape.palette`) gets UVs and a small flat-colour image, each ring and part naming its colour. `GARMENT_PROPS` gives hats `bone` (`walk.HEAD`), `hard` and `hide_hair tuck`; the hood only `hide_hair` (its cowl takes the body's weights). The old textured high-poly hats are in `Models/garmentsOldHighPoly/`.

- Hats: simple and flak helmets, tuque, cowboy hat, bowler, hood, tribal headdress, tinfoil hat, beret (`BERET_`), top hat, ladies' hat; crowns: coronet (`circlet`, `CORONET_OFF` over the hair, keeps it), crown, stellic crown (`STELLIC_`, `STELLIC_CREST`).
- Royalty: eltex helmet (`PsyfocusHelmet`) and skullcap (`ELTEX_HELMET_EDGE`, `ELTEX_CAP_EDGE`, `ELTEX_SEGMENTS`, `ELTEX_PLATE`; keep the face), gunlink (`Gunlink`, `GUN_`; replaces IsoRim's `person_hat_gunlink`).
- Ideology: authority cap (`AUTHORITY_`), flophat (`FLOP_`), headwrap (`HEADWRAP_`, `hide_hair` true), shadecone (`SHADECONE_TILT`, on a headband the hair tucks under), slicecap (`SLICECAP_OUTLINE`, `over_hair`; three variants one garment), tailcap (`TAILCAP_`, `TAIL_`), torture crown (`TORTURE_OFF`, `TORTURE_`), visage mask (`VISAGE_`), broadwrap (`WRAP_`: rings of columns, the face opening drawn onto an oval, each ring below it hanging from the one above (`WRAP_HANG`), down to a hem over the shoulders (`body_reach`, `WRAP_FLARE`); the body's weights; `hide_hair` true), slave collar (`slave_collar`, `neck_skin`, `COLLAR_`: built round the neck's axis, no `bone`, so the body's weights and keys; replaces IsoRim's `person_hat_collar`).
- Biotech: airwire headset (`AIR_`), array headset (`ARRAY_`), integrator headset (`INTEGRATOR_`, `bar`), all keeping hair and face and replacing IsoRim's models (`person_hat_airwire_headset` and the like); gas mask (`GAS_`, `GAS_CANISTER`, lenses on the `Visor` material, `GLASS`).
- Anomaly: cultist mask (`CULT_`, `skull_face`, `slanted`, `hang_teeth`, `TEETH_FROM`, `TEETH_LONG`; four styles one garment), ceremonial hood (`CeremonialCultistMask`: the broadwrap's `wrap_cloth` peaked (`HOOD_PEAK_UP`, `_BACK`), belled (`HOOD_BELL`), open down the front (`open_down`), a dark mask inside (`WHISPER_`)).
- Masks (`hide_hair` and `hide_face` off, riding the head bone): `WarMask`, `WarVeil`, `ClothMask` (`cut_out` sheets by azimuth and elevation, eye holes `EYE_AZ`/`EYE_EL`, `strap`; the cloth mask over the most skin within `CLOTH_OVER`, `over_skin`), `Blindfold` (`BLIND_`, `blind_skin`, `KNOT_`, `TAIL_`; replaces IsoRim's `person_hat_blindfold`). Hard face sheets: `face_sheet` (`cut_out` `centred`), `thick_sheet`, `lathe`.
- Eyewear: `Glasses`/`Sunglasses` (`glasses_frame`, `GLASSES_`, `GLASSES_SIDES`; `NoStandIn` with `NameAlike`) — **Maru's hand edit of the glasses is the shape of record**: `anim_scripts/make_sunglasses.py` (`blender -b --python anim_scripts/make_sunglasses.py -- Models/garments/glasses.glb Models/garments/sunglasses.glb`) makes the sunglasses from it; don't remake either from `make_hats.py`. `Goggles` (`GOGGLE_`, `GOGGLE_SIDES`, lenses on `Visor`; `NoStandIn`). CE's night vision (`CE_SimpleNightVision`, `CE_AdvancedNightVision`: `NV_`, `nv_harness`, `NV_STRAP_OFF`, `NV_CLEAR`).
- Over the hair (`GARMENT_PROPS` `over_hair`, hiding nothing; `NoStandIn` with `Hints`): `Hairband` (`HAIRBAND_TILT`, `HAIRBAND_OFF`), `Ribbon` (`RIBBON_`), `MaidHeaddress` (`MAID_`).
- Neck and chest (the body's weights; let out, `LetOutDefs`; no clearance, `NoClearanceDefs`): `Scarf` (`scarf`, `SCARF_`, `chest_skin`; `tint`; `NoStandIn` by `NameAlike`, on any layer), `Gorget` (`gorget`, `GORGET_`; **edited by hand since: don't re-run over `gorget.glb`**; the Belt layer).
- `BurkaHood` (`burka_hood`, in `PARTS`, made only when named, into `Models/bases/`): the broadwrap closed but for an eye slit (`BURKA_`, `BURKA_OVER`).
- `anim_scripts/decimate_garment.py` (`blender -b --python anim_scripts/decimate_garment.py -- <in.glb> <out.glb> <triangles>`): takes a textured high-poly garment to the same style (welded, decimated, UVs and images dropped; the `PlateArmorHelmet`, 250).
- `anim_scripts/split_visor.py` takes a helmet's `Visor` group off for a Meshy retexture and joins it back; `anim_scripts/smooth_visor.py` (`blender -b --python anim_scripts/smooth_visor.py -- <in.glb> <out.glb> [spacing]`) rebuilds the `Visor` faces as an even mesh on a fitted ellipsoid inside their edge ring (run on the recon and power armour helmets at 1.0; lumpy originals in `Models/unused/`).

## Coats, robes, skirts and dresses

- `anim_scripts/make_duster.py` (`blender -b <blend> --python anim_scripts/make_duster.py -- <out.glb> [out.blend] [--robe|--burka|--labcoat|--skirt [length]|--dress [length]] [--low]`): the `Duster`: the `-Jacket` group at the Shell offset, cut open down the front (`OPEN_WIDTH` at `OPEN_LOW`; cut vertices kept as places on their edges, so keys move them), a long skirt hung from its bottom edge (`HEM`, `EASE`, `FLARE`, the hem kept at its share down the leg in each key), a back vent from `VENT_TOP`, weights to each thigh then the shin below `KNEE` (`FRONT_`/`BACK_`). It reads the .glb back and stops if any vertex lost its weights (it unhides the rig first, as a hidden rig exports no skin).

## Skeleton (`anim_scripts/make_skeleton.py`)

`blender -b Models/pawnHumanRig.blend --python anim_scripts/make_skeleton.py -- Models/garments/Skeleton.glb [out.blend] [--preview out.png] [--jaw]` (Workbench previews from four sides, the skull from three, and the image). A cartoon skeleton in the chibi's proportions, near the body's size (~1230 triangles, 720 vertices; the skinning cost goes by vertices, so geometry where the shape shows from every side and painting where a part is thin), every vertex whole on one bone:
- The skull: the head's own mesh (with `No_Human_Ears`) shrunk `SKULL_SHRINK`, dented (`DENTS`), eye sockets and a nose hole cut by Blender's exact boolean (`cut`, `use_self`, `use_hole_tolerant`) as dark cones (`HOLES`, `hole_cutter`); one piece with teeth painted, unless `--jaw` cuts the jaw off (`JAW_`, opened `JAW_OPEN`). Painted by azimuth and elevation (`paint_skull`).
- Spine rods (`paint_spine`), long bones as 5-sided shafts with knobs (`SHAFT`, `KNOB`, `SHAFT_MOST`/`KNOB_MOST`, `KNOB_FAR`), clavicles.
- Cut out by alpha on the `Unlined` material (left out of the outline): the ribcage band (`RIB_IN`, `paint_ribs`), pelvis band (`paint_pelvis`), hand cards (`paint_hand`), foot cards (`paint_foot`), outlines painted (`bone_art`). One image `IMG` in `PANELS`.
- `GARMENT_PROPS`: `hard`, `fit` `babylimbs`, untinted, `cutout`, hides nothing, `KEEP_WEIGHTS`, `inside`.

## Textures through Meshy (`anim_scripts/place_textured.py`)

`strip`, `place`, `view`; the batch and each garment's prompt in `anim_scripts/generation/textures/prompts.py`, work and originals in `generationAssets/textures/<file>/`. The mesh alone is uploaded (`strip --keep-uvs` with `meshy.py multitexture --keep-uvs` paints the garment's own UVs); what returns is put on the garment's vertices (twins told apart by triangle), a `KEEP_WEIGHTS` garment's weights and own keys carried over, the image made grey with its median near white (`neutral`), as the game multiplies it by the apparel's colour. Make scripts write untextured meshes, so re-running one over a textured `.glb` loses its texture: run this again after (10 credits, or none from the kept `2_textured.glb` if the triangles are unchanged). Older high-poly pipeline notes: the `garment-pipeline` memory.
