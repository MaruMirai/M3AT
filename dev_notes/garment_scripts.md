# Garment make scripts

Scripts that make or mend garments, body keys, ears and other meshes in Blender (or numpy). Unless said, `<blend>` is `Models/pawnHumanRig.blend` (or a copy), outputs go to `Models/garments/`, and a garment is then exported by `export_frames.py` (`dev_notes/clips.md`). "Run Script" means the Text Editor's Run Script in the open session. Blender: `/home/t/Programs/blender-5.2.3-linux-x64/blender`.

**The .glb files in `Models/garments/` are often hand-edited after a `make_*.py` script wrote them, and many are not in git, so re-running a script over one loses those edits for good.** Before re-running a make script whose output exists, copy that .glb aside (e.g. `Models/unused/<name>_before.glb`) or ask. A .glb newer than the script's last run has likely been edited. Don't take a script's output as the source of truth. Known hand edits: the glasses, gorget, loincloth, exoframe, shields, skirts' masters, the phoenix launcher, and the armours' soles.

Scripts that build on another garment as it is (re-run after the source changes): `make_sunglasses.py` (glasses), `make_radio_pack.py` (CE backpack), `make_formal_shirt.py` (collared shirt), `make_locust.py`/`make_mechlord.py` (recon armour), `make_grenadier.py` (marine armour; `--phoenix` the cataphract), `make_mech_helmets.py` (recon helmet), `make_prestige_helmets.py` and `prestige_paint.py`/`swap_garment_image.py` (the plain armours and helmets), `make_underwear.py`'s tank top (crop top, shirt), `make_baby_romper.py` (kid romper), `RobeRoyal` (robe), `paint_cloth.py` (after `make_from_groups.py`/`make_underwear.py`/`make_skirts.sh` remake). Since the soles were stretched (`armour_soles.py`), scripts starting from those armours start from the stretched ones.

## The body's generation (other mods' garments)

`export_frames.BODY_GENERATION` is written to skin.txt as `generation n`. **Raise it by hand when the body's surface or rest pose moves enough that a garment made on the last one could clip or float** (a reshape like the 2026-11 shoulders and shins, or a cut that moves skin outward). Re-weighting, new edges or vertices slid along the skin don't need it. The hash can't do this job, because it changes with every export. The garment kit (`Docs/Tools/m3at_garment_kit.py`, `body_line`, kit 4) writes `body <skin.txt hash> <generation>` into every garment it exports. `ModGarments` adds a note to that garment's load log line when the generation is older than skin.txt's (`Piece.bodyGeneration`, `SkinnedFigure.bodyGeneration`). Our own garments have no body line, and the exporter refits them. Generation 1 is the body as of the belly cut (2026-11-27). Modders are told what may change and what is kept in `Docs/Apparel.md`, "When M3AT's body changes". Changing what is listed there as kept (bone names and how they connect, the A-pose and scale, the body shapes' names, the file format) breaks other mods' garments, so treat it as an API change.

## Which garments are the body's faces (to modify when the body changes)

These garments' triangles are the `Body`'s own triangles pushed out, so they hold the body's topology. When the body's mesh changes (a cut, a re-triangulation, vertices moved, merged or added), each needs its triangles over the change recut or remade, or at least moving with the new shape (`follow_body.py`, which keeps their mesh but not the new edges). `anim_scripts/body_faced.py` (`blender -b Models/pawnHumanRig.blend --python anim_scripts/body_faced.py -- [garments folder] [--all]`) lists them from the files: per garment its triangles, how many are body faces, and its usual distance off the body (rig units); re-run it after adding a garment. ## From the body's own faces

- `anim_scripts/make_from_groups.py` (`blender -b <blend> --python anim_scripts/make_from_groups.py -- <out folder> [garment ...] [--blend out.blend]`): a garment from each of the body's dashed vertex groups (`-Pants`; `-CollarShirt -Jacket -FlakJacket` all three from the same faces): the group's faces pushed out along the normals by the apparel layer (`LAYER_OFFSET`; `OWN_OFFSET` for one set apart), the body's UVs (`unwrap_pawn.py`'s atlas) kept, its weights and keys rebuilt from the keyed body, `defs` from `GARMENTS`. One with its own script (`OWN_SCRIPT`: TribalA) or its own mesh in the .blend is left alone. Each goes to `<out folder>/<Name>.glb` skinned to the rig. The `Sandals` are left out unless named with `--over` (the drawn ones, 106 triangles, aren't this script's, 698).

After: wire edges the deleted faces left removed, the far side's seam flags set from the source's, and it stops unless every vertex and face has its mirror. - `anim_scripts/recut_from_parent.py` (`blender -b --python anim_scripts/recut_from_parent.py -- <parent.glb> <child.glb> <out.glb> [--check] [--name N]`; no rig needed): re-cuts a garment cut by hand from another out of the parent as it is now, so it holds the parent's (the new body's) triangles again. The child's hems (its open edge loops, welded by place) are laid on the parent (each hem vertex at the parent's nearest point among triangles not facing away, `FACING`; onto a parent vertex or edge within `SNAP`), each hem segment traced over the parent along the plane through its two ends holding their mean normal (a crossing found once per parent edge, so neighbours share it: no cracks), each parent triangle the hems touch split by `delaunay_2d_cdt` in its own plane with the hem pieces as constraints (the triangulator is single precision and returns flat triangles along an edge holding several points: dropped by their area in double, from the points' shares), the pieces grouped by flood fill across non-hem edges and a group kept if most of its area lies on the child (middle within `ON` of its surface and not past one of its hems). Every new vertex is blended from its parent triangle's corners (normal, texture place, morph targets), so the child reads the parent's image as the parent does; the output is the parent's .glb (material, image, zeroed keys) with the child's node name. It keeps the outline only: anything of the child's own off the parent's surface inside its hems goes (the tops' chests stood 0.02-0.03 off the old shirt from being recut apart from it, the Bra's centre 0.1 in front), so it suits a child that is one surface of its parent (check: one manifold piece, its hems the only open edges, no inward triangles). Rings cast from the bones in planes across them (`ring`), joined by quads (`bridge`, `chain`): torso 14 round (rings at the leg seam, belly, waist, underbust, bust, armpit, mid-armhole, shoulder line); each arm plugs into an 8-corner hole two columns wide across the armpit, mid and shoulder rings, the armhole cast in an upright plane at x 2.2 (cast across the arm it went through the torso); legs 9 round from the leg seam plus a crotch vertex `tc` (the inner thigh's column; one crotch edge cut off the pubic mound and the buttocks' underside); 3 loops at each elbow and knee, wrist, ankle; the hand a mitten (wrist, two palm rings, knuckle, mid-finger at the `.002` joint, fingertip ring, tip) with the thumb from a 6-corner hole across wrist-palm-palm (thumb joint ring, tip ring, tip); the foot a boot (ankle, then three rings round the foot's outline spaced by length along it, the top one tilted low at the toe, then a sole fan: cast by angle, a long foot got one vertex on its toe; a tube along the foot with the leg in its top crumpled at the ankle); the neck 8 round with the shoulder line fanned in, the cap's ring cast in the cap's own tilted plane. `DROP` leaves loops out: by default the thumb's root ring and the upper arm's middle ring, found by an ablation (each loop left out in turn: those two, and the elbow's upper loop, cost nothing in shape; the mid-shin, knee and thigh loops cost the most; the elbow's upper loop was kept for the bend).
- `relax.py`: tangential smoothing over the target with ring vertices held in their planes and the foot outline and tips locked (`LOCK`; relaxing slid the toe's vertices back to the ankle), mirrored halves averaged every step; then each quad's diagonal chosen by which one's middle lies nearer the surface (decided on the left, mirrored: one fixed split put one half of the crotch's quads in and one out); then fitted, each vertex moved along its normal by its faces' mean signed distance so faces straddle the surface rather than cutting in.
- `build_body.py` (Blender, from the rig): the new Body with the old one's bone weights (interpolated at each vertex's nearest point of the old surface, normalised, under 0.01 dropped), every shape key's moves the same way, the dashed and other groups (in where half or more), the old Armature modifier's settings; each quad split first-to-third corner (`run.sh` turns them so). Then `unwrap_pawn.py --save` for the atlas.
- Checks: `metric.py` (two-way distance to the target by region, old body and new), `view.py`, `errmap.py`/`errface.py` (where the new one cuts in or stands out), `pose_test.py` + `posecmp3.py` (both bodies in ten poses, framed on the joint; **the rig file is saved with the Body in Edit Mode, where its Armature modifier doesn't run: switch to Object Mode first**), `key_test.py` (each key at 1).

How it came out (mean distance to the target, the old body's in brackets; the target was fitted to the old body's vertices, which favours it): torso 0.032 (0.033), arms 0.027 (0.028), hands 0.022 (0.021), legs 0.033 (0.023), feet 0.034 (0.021); everything within 0.34. Posed: elbows, knees (sitting, kneeling), the fist and the wrist fold cleaner than the old body's; the armpit creases on raising the arm and the wrist tugs the thumb's root on both (weights carried over, not remade). All 11 keys carry over and look like the old ones. A full `export_frames.py` of it into a scratch folder ran (garments fitted from it, hides worked out over its 940 triangles; `SKIN` skinning matches Blender within 0.00000 cells; 51 garments `BURIED` by a few vertices at rest, most of them 1-26: the garments still have the old body's shape; no baseline run of the current rig to compare with). Not tried in the game. The neck's back rises a little more steeply into the head than the target's (the shoulder line's fan; raising the neck ring or tilting it made it worse).

## Skeleton (`anim_scripts/make_skeleton.py`)

`blender -b Models/pawnHumanRig.blend --python anim_scripts/make_skeleton.py -- Models/garments/Skeleton.glb [out.blend] [--preview out.png] [--jaw]` (Workbench previews from four sides, the skull from three, and the image). A cartoon skeleton in the chibi's proportions, near the body's size (~1230 triangles, 720 vertices; the skinning cost goes by vertices, so geometry where the shape shows from every side and painting where a part is thin), every vertex whole on one bone:
