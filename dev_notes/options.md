# Mod options and player tools

## The options (`Source/Settings.cs`: `SettingsMod`, `Settings`)

- Every piece of text is a `M3AT_` key in `Languages/English/Keyed/M3AT_Settings.xml` (with `{0}` for a value), and `Languages/ChineseSimplified (简体中文)/` has the Simplified Chinese (its `DefInjected` the freecam keys' names): **a new or changed option wants its key in both.** A weapon type is named by `M3AT_WeaponType_<type>`, else its own name (`TypeName`).
- Layout: sections (`SettingsMod.Sections`, one method each) in two columns; the freecam's always heads the second, and the folded Outlines, Weapon sizes, Advanced and Mod compatibility sections (`FoldHeader`, `outlinesOpen`, `weaponSizesOpen`, `advancedOpen`, `compatibilityOpen`, not saved; Weapon sizes not shown until the weapon types have loaded) always end it (`PinnedTop`, `PinnedBottom`); the rest is split so the taller column is shortest, by each section's last drawn height (`Columns`), worked out as the window opens or changes width.
- "Restore all defaults" (Problems, `RestoreDefaults`) copies a new `Settings`' fields over every option but `freecamOn`/`freecam3D`. The player's tunings (grip, clothing, face) are not touched and have their own clear buttons.
- `SettingsMod.WriteSettings` runs as the window closes: hair detail taken up, ground weapons/clothes re-baked, other races' rule added or removed, as below.

Each option, by area (the detail is in the notes named):

- Size and shape (`dev_notes/body.md`): `pawnHeight`, `headShrink`, `genderSizes`, `heightSpread`, `variedBodies` and `bodySpread`, `bodyShapeStrength`; Mod compatibility: `marineBulk` with `armourBulk`, `armourDrawScale`, `powerArmourBulk` (hidden, off).
- Looks (`dev_notes/body.md`): `agingLooks`, `greyingHair`, `skeletons`, `prosthetics`, `archotechProsthetics`, `prostheticEyes`, `visibleWounds`, `injuryAnims` (limp and hurt holds; flinches and hopping stay), `earHiding`, `armourHidesTail`; Mod compatibility: `otherRaces`, `raceParts`, `raceModels`.
- Clothes (`dev_notes/apparel.md`): `shoes`, `spriteClothes`, `groundGarments`, `helmetStow`, `sleepWear`/`bedClothes` and `bedBlankets` (`dev_notes/motion.md`), `vatClothes`.
- Hair and faces (`dev_notes/hair.md`, `dev_notes/faces.md`): `beardMode`, `hairDetail` (Performance; 0.05..1, `DefaultHairDetail` 0.3; `SkinnedFigure.TakeHairDetail` re-cuts each style when next asked for), `talkingMouths`, `gazeStrength`; Advanced: `sharpFaceArt`, `raceMouthInk`, `mouthSizeFit`, the face tuning window.
- Weapons (`dev_notes/weapons.md`): `holsters`, `wornGear`, `autoGrips`, `muzzleFlash`, `retexturedWeaponModels` (below), `weaponScales` (saved as `weaponGroundScales`, only types moved off the export's), `slabScale` (below), `weaponSizesAtStandard` (below), the grip tool toggle (`gripToolButton`, `gripTool`, "M3AT tools", also the clothing tool's).
- Motion (`dev_notes/motion.md`): `ragdolls`, `flingBodies`, `flingWeapons`, `flingPower` (0.25..5 of the standard distance, `FlingPowerStep`; `WalkFigures.FlingPower` is its square root × `FlingDistance`, as distance goes as speed squared; applied to a body's fling, `FlingSpeedOf`, and a blow's push, `Blown`; `dollHit` measured without it), `Hygiene.ClimbSwimmers` (a constant, off).
- Drawing (`dev_notes/skinning.md`): `hitFlash`; Outlines (its own folded section, `OutlinesSection`; the checkbox is "Draw outlines"): `outlines` (off: `DrawMeshes` passes only non-outline parts; IsoRim's models keep theirs), `outlineMode` and its widths (below), `clothesOcclusion`; Performance: `fewerPoses`; Advanced: `gpuSkinning` (saved as `gpuSkinningOn`; in Advanced so it isn't turned off by accident).
- Freecam and first person (below).

### Weapon model options

- `retexturedWeaponModels` (on, `WalkFigures.RetexturedWeaponModels`): IsoRim (Api 50) draws a weapon's sprite rather than its model when another mod supplies its art (CE does for every vanilla gun and grenade). `WeaponRule` (`Api.AddWeaponModelRule`) keeps the model for the game's own weapons (Core and DLCs, `IsOfficialMod`, bound by their own name, a model rule or their picture, `Api.HowBound`); another mod's is left to IsoRim; with the option off the rule says nothing.
- `Source/RestoredWeaponModels.cs` (`RestoredModels`, registered from `SettingsMod`'s constructor): an older IsoRim CE pack (its GitHub zip) removes the vanilla weapons' ModelDefs, so an IsoRim model rule (`Api.AddModelRule`, Api 42) gives each official weapon no ModelDef names a copy of IsoRim's own (`M3AT_Restored_<thing>`, IsoRim's pack as its `modContentPack`), drawn as a sprite while the option is off (`RestoredModels.Has`). Does nothing with the Workshop pack (since 2026-11-18).
- Changing it re-bakes the maps' weapons as the window closes (`RebakeGroundWeapons`).
- `slabScale` (0.3..1.2, `DefaultSlabScale` 0.7, under the weapon sizes): a weapon drawn as its slab (not thrown) is drawn that share of its type's size (`WalkFigures.SlabScale` in `ScaleOf`), as a model fitted to the sprite is shrunk by its `GroundRatio` (1.3..1.8) while the slab is the sprite's length. The game's guns match at 0.59..0.74 (median 0.71; 0.66 on CE's art; `slabsizes` autotest).
- Weapons follow `pawnHeight` (always): the type sizes are at the standard height, so they needn't be changed with it. Every place that turned a weapon's cells into the figure's model units by dividing by the pawn's size (`SlabMatrix`, the holsters' depths and slides, `HandsClear`, the grip slides and `ArtDrawSize`) divides by `WalkFigures.WeaponSizeOf(size)`, the pawn's size over `PawnSize`. A pawn's own size (age, race, genes, armour) still leaves its weapon as it is. Options saved before this (no `weaponSizesAtStandard`) had sizes at the saved height: `SizesToHeight` divides the saved ones by `PawnSize` once, on first use (`TypeSize`, the weapon sizes section), not in `ExposeData`, which runs as the mods are built, before `WalkFigures` can read `anim.txt`. Types left at the default aren't pinned, so they start following the height. Autotest `weaponpawnheight`.

### Outlines

- `outlineMode` (`OutlineMode`, `SkinnedFigure.Mode`, `Silhouette`: true but for `Lines`): `Silhouette` (default; shell outline only round the figure), `Lines` (over the body too, as IsoRim's), `ScreenSpace`. An old save's `silhouetteOutlines` off reads as `Lines`.
- Only `ScreenSpace` reads `pawnOutlineWidth`/`weaponOutlineWidth` (0.03 and 0.015 cells; the shell's width is baked into cached hulls, `OutlineWidth`), `screenSelfOutlines` (off) and `selfOutlineStep` (0.04..0.3 cells, 0.08; a line over the pawn's own body where one place is that much nearer than its neighbour, `_EdgeTune.z`), and the detail sliders: `outlineReach` ("Close-up width limit", the widest line in px at 1080 rows, 4..32), and in `SettingsMod.OutlineDetail` `outlineProbeDirs` (4..16), `outlineProbeRings` (1..4), `outlineRings` (2..16), with Fast (default, 6.2.6 at 8 px, `DefaultOutline*`) and Detailed (8.2.10 at 16, `DetailedOutline*`) buttons (`Settings.SetDetailedOutlines`; an old save's `highDetailOutlines` reads once as Detailed).
- Screen-space outline (`Source/ScreenOutlines.cs`), found on screen because the shell has no rim at an open one-surface part's edge (the pauldrons) and the silhouette hides the line round a held weapon over the body:
  - Each part's solid mesh is drawn again (`SetIdPart`, `m.idAt`; `IdMaterial` per slot, per image for textured parts) at queue 2455 with `MarusOutlineId`/`MarusOutlineIdTex` (`MarusSmooth.frag.glsl`'s `_OutlineId`): a blend multiplying the picture by (1, 1, 1, code), code = 2 + 2 × figure slot + 1 for a weapon, over 255, written once a pixel by stencil bit 64 (IsoRim's occlusion and shadow passes leave alpha 1).
  - Then the back faces of a box round each figure (`DrawFigureCover`: its joints, `p.whole` × `boneHead`, `CoverMargin` roomier, once a frame and slot, `Posed.coverFrame`) and each held weapon (`DrawJoinedScreen` in place of `DrawJoined`'s shell and stencil; left out inside its figure's box, `slotMin`/`slotMax`) with `MarusEdge` (`MarusEdge.vert/.frag.glsl`, queue 2456). It reads that picture (its own scene grab) and IsoRim's scene depth copy (`_IsoSceneDepth`, IsoRim's internal `DepthGrab`, `DepthGrab.Request` once a frame, `RequestDepth`; copied at IsoRim's `IsoRimAO` grab, which our part pass's defs name so the engine makes it with occlusion off: grab points are shared by name, `SceneGrabRegistry`).
  - A pixel is the line where a pixel of another part nearer the camera (by `EdgeNearer`) is within that part's width in pixels (rings out to `EdgeMostPixels` at 1080 rows, at most 10 rings of up to 32 points; inside one figure the thinner kind's, at least a pixel), searched only where a quick look (`EdgeProbeDirs` points on `EdgeProbeRings` rings; `EdgeRings`; the shader's `_EdgeShape`) finds a nearer part, or with self-outlines the same part nearer by half its step (`EdgeNearerOnly`, `_EdgeShape.w`; a background pixel never has a line). The full search everywhere cost a screen-filling pawn 10 → 28 ms; `EdgeFullSearch` (`_EdgeTune.w`) forces it for comparison.
  - The line is those pixels' mean colour × `LineShade`, written off the figure at their depth (`gl_FragDepth`, so plant cards drawn later stay behind) and over a part at that part's own depth (so later face layers aren't hidden). Other pixels are written back with alpha 1 and their depth, and bit 64 cleared.
  - Drawn as the silhouette instead in IsoRim's photos (`Api.CaptureView` makes no scene grabs; `InPhoto`, IsoRim's internal `Photos.Drawing` by reflection), so autotest videos never show it (use `--stills`), and when the depth can't be had (`DepthGrab.Broken`) or a shader is missing (`ScreenOutlinesAvailable`, shown in the options). IsoRim's own outlined things (slabs, tools, worn gear, flung weapons) keep theirs.
  - Costs: Fast ~2.7 ms a frame over the silhouette for a screen-filling pawn, Detailed 6.1; at the usual distance ~0.5 ms.
- Autotests: `perf` compares by `--counts` suffixes (`s` screen-space, `t` self-outlines, `x` full search, `i` part codes only, `b` a weapon's box even inside its figure's, `a` any part starting the full search, `@d.r.R` an edge shape; e.g. `--counts 99,99s,99st,99s@6.2.6`), with `--paused` (one frame for every condition), uncapped (`EngineWindow.TargetFrameRateOverride` 0) unless `--capped`, `--distance cells`, `--focus k` (following the colonist with the most others within 4 cells, `perf_view`). `--edgeshapes` sets `Edge*` and `EdgeShapeForced`. `outlines --stills [--edgeshapes 8.2.10,6.2.6]`: a rifleman, power armour and a pistol, drafted, each mode at two widths, checking the edge pass only in `ScreenSpace` (`ScreenCovers`).

## Bug reports (`Source/BugReport.cs`)

"Copy bug report" (Problems; also dev action M3AT > Copy bug report) copies to the clipboard and `<config>/M3AT/bug_report.txt`: the DLL's build time, RimWorld version, IsoRim `Api.Version`, `WalkFigures.StartState`, GPU skinning, OS, GPU, CPU, related mods (`Related`), the selected pawns (at most `MostPawns`; `WalkFigures.PawnReport`: job, driver, toil, lord job, posture, stance, load, age, body type, size, key mix, hair, beard, genes, missing parts, each apparel with layers and garments, weapons by `WeaponName`/`KindOf`/`TypeOf`/`AsSlab`, drafted/aim/carry clips, every `Ease` field not at rest), every `Settings` field by reflection (a new option needs nothing added), log lines mentioning `M3AT` or with `M3AT.` in the trace (newest `MostLogLines`, traces cut to `MostTraceLines`), and the load order; home folder as `~`. Nothing is sent.

## Benchmark (`Source/Benchmark.cs`, `BenchmarkWindow`)

"Benchmark M3AT impact" (Problems; on a map in the 3D view; closes the options) opens a window that, at the player's speed with the camera held (`preventCameraMotion`), alternates the chibi and IsoRim's figures (`WalkFigures.PerfMode` 0, 1, also used by the `perf` autotest), optionally no person figures (2) and the chibi with `fewerPoses` flipped. First turns aren't measured (the chibi's waits on hair, `HairBuilding`, at most `HairWaitMost`), then 2/3/5 rounds of 5/10/15 s, order turned each round, `SettleSeconds` after each switch. Shows mean FPS, 1% low, frame time, the chibi's cost against the noise (twice the combined standard error), the people's cost and per chibi in view (`PerfAsked`); warns of few people, a likely frame cap (`NearCap`) or a speed change. "Copy results" also writes `<config>/M3AT/benchmark.txt`. Stopped by leaving the map, game or 3D view; everything put back.

- Add-ons (`Source/AddOns.cs`): other mods that change what the chibi draws, by packageId: their garments (`Piece.owner`), their Harmony patches on our assembly's methods (by the patch method's assembly) and expansion posers. "Measure add-ons separately" (on when any are found, `tryAddOns`) adds a turn without all of them (`M3AT_BenchNoAddOns`: M3AT alone against IsoRim, the add-ons against the chibi) and, for more than one, one without each (`M3AT_BenchWithout`). `AddOns.SetOff` for a turn: `GarmentOf` and the stand-in pool skip its garments (`OffAddOn`; `standInsOf` and `standInProfiles` dropped on `AddOns.Version`, `AddOnsChanged` clears `wornCaches`), its patches are taken off (`Unpatch` by owner) and put back from their `Patch` (priority, before, after) after, its posers aren't asked (`ExpansionApi.Ask`). Patches on the game or IsoRim stay, so that share isn't counted. Ground apparel keeps what it was baked with. The switch's hitch falls in the turn's skipped frames.

## Content packs (`Source/ContentSources.cs`, `Source/ContentWindow.cs`, `Source/AddOns.cs`, `Source/ExpansionPacks.cs`)

Mods that change what the chibi draws (race packs: Miho, Wolfein), found by `AddOns.Find`: garments and gene parts (`Piece.owner`), patches on our assembly's methods (owning mod by assembly, `ModOf`), posers, ExpansionApi 2 registrations. Each touches parts (`ContentPart`: EarsTail, Face, Hair, HeadwearFit, BodyShape, SkinColour, Clothing, GeneParts, Poses, Other; `Shared` for plumbing). A patch's part is the patched method's (`ContentSources.PartOf`: a table of our method names, every `FaceLayers` method the Face's; `DrawDressed`, `ReadMesh`, `Load` Shared). **A method renamed or added for a part goes in that table in the same change.**
- Choice per pack and part (`Settings.contentChoices`, `"<packageId>|<part>"` → `pack`/`m3at`, only non-defaults kept; an option, so Restore all defaults clears it). `UsesPack` (cached per `AddOns.Version`). Defaults (`Default`): the pack's, but the Face M3AT's where Facial Animation has faces for the pack's races (`FacialAnimationHas`: its `EyeTypeDef`/`HeadTypeDef` `raceName`); races from IsoRim's `AlienModelDef` (its mod, or `modPackageId` a prefix of the id: Miho's names its old id) and API registrations (`RacesOf`).
- Enforced: `AddOns.Gate` takes off a patch whose mod is off for a benchmark turn (`SetOff`) or whose part is M3AT's (Shared ones only when every part is M3AT's), puts back the rest as they were; runs at `ContentSources.Started` (from `Ready()`, after the packs patched in their static constructors) and on every choice (`Apply`), so it takes effect at once. Garments: `GarmentOf` → `Wanted` (apparel/body.md); gene parts: `GenePartsOf` skips `OffAddOn`. API content asked only when `UsesPack` (`ExpansionApi.Ask`).
- First sight of a pack (`Settings.contentSeen`): one log line and message (`M3AT_ContentNew`).
- UI, one `ContentView` three ways: the Content tab of the options (`SettingsMod.DoSettingsWindowContents` → `TabDrawer`, Options = `DrawOptions`, today's page), the bottom-bar M3AT button (`Defs/MainButtonDefs/M3AT_MainButtons.xml`, `ContentTabWindow`, `ContentButtonWorker` shows it while packs are active and `contentButton`), and M3AT look on a pawn (the clothing tool's window, `ClothingToolWindow`, behind the M3AT tools toggle, scoped by `ContentView.Covers`). A row per part, Pack / M3AT by float menu, hints (`Hint`), Reset this pack; Clothing's Items opens `PackClothesWindow` (`ClothingToolWindow.PackItemRow`).
- Bug report: the Add-ons section gives each pack's parts and sources.
- Docs for modders: `Docs/RacePacks.md` (ExpansionApi 2: `RegisterPack` → `RaceEars`, `RaceTail`, `Garments(folder, textureFolder)`, `Face`, `Hair`, `SkinColour`; asked at `EarFileOf` (`PackEars`), `HasCatTail`/`TailOf` (`Posed.packTail`, `packTails`), `FaceLayers.Draw`, `HairBuilder.HairOf`, `SkinOf`, `ModGarments.PackGarmentFiles`). Not yet: body keys, hat rims.
- `contentsources` autotest: `dev_notes/autotest.md`.

## Expansion API (`Source/ExpansionApi.cs`, `Source/ExpansionClips.cs`)

`ExpansionApi.Version` 1 (a property, so an expansion reads the running M3AT's). Another mod can pose the chibi:
- `ExpansionApi.AddClips(path)`: a clip file in `anim.txt`'s form against the chibi's bones (its `bone` lines must match in order, else a warning; only `clip`, `k` and `over` lines; existing names skipped; read once the chibi loads, `ExpansionApi.Loaded`, or at once after).
- `ExpansionApi.AddPoser(poser)`: a `Poser(Pawn, PoseRequest)` asked in `DrawSkinned` for every figure once IsoRim's placing is read (after the gibbet, before M3AT's posture rules), given `placedAt`, `placedYaw`, `situation`, `size`. Returning true with at least one `Layer(clip, phase, weight)` draws it by `DrawPosedByApi` (`Begin`, `CutLimbs`, the layers, `End` with its `whole`, at its `at` and `yaw`, let down onto `restOn` if set, then `DrawDressed` wearing its `dress` choice of `SleepWear`, `apiDress`). A poser that throws is dropped.
- The preview tool's expansions: `dev_notes/clips.md`. The RJW expansion lives in a repo beside this one.

## Freecam (`Source/Freecam.cs`; keys in `Defs/KeyBindings.xml`, category M3AT)

- `M3AT_Freecam` (F6) flies a free eye in IsoRim's 3D view. Needs IsoRim Api 68 (`MinimumApi`; off with a warning before) and uses no IsoRim internals: the eye is a camera pose (`Api.PushViewPose`, pushed on `Enter`, disposed on `Leave`; `Posing` is the only code naming the camera Api's types), so IsoRim's rays, picking, view rect, culling, fog and sky follow; focus distance the ground at the screen's middle within `NearDistance`..`FarDistance` (`FocusDistance`); looking up to `LeastPitch`. `Frame` (on `Api.BeforeViewUpdate`) runs keys, flying and looking.
- Moving: the game's `MapDolly_` keys along the ground the way it faces; `M3AT_FreecamUp`/`Down` (Right Shift, Right Ctrl); eased (`Accel`) at `freecamSpeed` cells per real second, `BoostTimes` that with `M3AT_FreecamBoost` (Left Shift). `freecamScrollFlies` (on): the wheel flies (`ZoomPrefix` on `CameraDriver.CameraDriverOnGUI` takes the scroll event; `ScrollStep` × `freecamScrollSpeed` (0.5) s a notch, boosted, eased by `ScrollEase`, `scrollClimb`). Options: sensitivity, invert, speed, `freecamFov`.
- Looking: the mouse turns it except in pointer mode or while a window wants the pointer (`Look`, `PointerWanted`, `WindowEventsPrefix`/`Postfix` on `WindowStack.HandleEventsHighPriority`). The cursor is locked (`Cursor.lockState`, `PointerConstraint.Locked`) and put back in the screen's middle every frame (`Warp`: the input bridge's `_position`, found down the window's handler chain, `FindBridgeOnHandler`: `WindowBase._handler`, each decorator's `Inner`, to `RimWorldWaylith.WindowInputBridge`; freed, also the window's `WarpPointer`; plus `InputSystem.QueueMouseMove`; engines before 1.7.5013 (GLFW) by `CursorPos` and `Bootstrap._inputBridge._lastCursorPos`, tried first), movement read as the offset, so clicks land under the cross (`GuiPostfix`).
- Pointer: freed while a float menu, dialog or main tab wants it or the game is unfocused, then warped for `CentreFrames` frames back to where looking began (`freecamPointerBack`, off; `KeepLookedFrom`, `lookedFrom`; a menu opened under the cross moved with it, `MoveMenusBack`; a dot drawn there while looking, `freecamPointerDot`, on, `DrawLookedFrom`), else to the middle. `M3AT_FreecamPointer` (unbound; every key near WASD is taken) or `freecamButton` (middle; 3, 4 side buttons; -1 none) flips `pointerMode`. Right clicks are the game's alone and never change it. In pointer mode, or with a main tab up, left clicks go to the map (`KeepTabsOpen`). `freecamClickOffLooks` (off): a left click leaving nothing selected goes back to looking (`leftPending`). Relocking drops a box selection begun.
- Orders menus: `freecamMenuFreesPointer` (on) frees the pointer in the middle while up; off, a menu opened while looking is pinned to the place under the cross (`pinned`, `Pin`, `MovePinned`, `PinnedNearest`, moved as the pose is given), picked by turning onto options, closed by turning away. `freecamSnapArchitect` (off): the pointer jumps `SnapBeside` right of the architect menu as it opens (`SnapToArchitect`), back if nothing is picked (`snapBack`).
- IsoRim: its camera keys and look drag wait (`BlockPlayerCamera`; its roof key `R3D_ToggleRoofs` passed on by `Freecam.Roofs` through `Api.ShowRoofs`); its settings never written; on leaving, `Api.SetPlayerView` turns its camera to the freecam's yaw over the ground at the screen's middle. Api 68's `View3DActive`, `ControlsLocked`, `LeaveFirstPerson`, `KeyHeld`, `PerspectiveTanHalf`. First person coming on (`Api.FirstPersonOn`, Api 71; before it `R3D_FirstPerson` and `FirstPersonStarts`) sets the freecam aside; Resume brings it back if it didn't start. Complete walls requested every frame (`Api.RequestFullWalls`, `SyncWalls`) unless `freecamCutaway` (off).
- The game's panning is held off (`DollyPostfix`); its camera is kept a little ahead of the eye (`KeepGameCamera`).
- State: F6 sets `Settings.freecamOn` (written at once). The 3D view going off, first person, another map or a gravship cutscene only set it aside; `Resume` puts it back (a game loaded with it wanted turns the 3D view on first if it was on, `freecam3D`). Each map's last eye, yaw and pitch are saved (`FreecamPlaces`, a `GameComponent`) for coming back on by itself; F6 starts from IsoRim's camera (`Enter`'s `resuming`).
- Camera jumps (`CameraJumper.TryJumpInternal`: colonist bar double click, letters, alerts; `TrySelectInternal` on a map change) are noted (`JumpPrefix`, `SelectPrefix`) and taken once on that map (`TakeJump`): keep yaw and ground distance (`JumpNearest`..`JumpFarthest`), pitched down at least `JumpLeastPitch`, looking at `JumpAbove` over the cell (an upper floor's cell with IsoRimZ at its height: `OnItsFloor`, by `Api.Floors`), gliding there (up to `JumpLongest`; cut with smooth jumps off or across maps), stopped by flying or the wheel.

## First-person eye (`Source/FirstPersonEye.cs`; IsoRim Api 77, `Api.AddFirstPersonEye`; `firstPersonEye`, on)

IsoRim's first person looks out from the chibi's eyes (`SkinnedFigure.EyeOf`: the face patch's middle at FA's eye line, `EyeLineV`, in the pawn's shape, by the head bone). IsoRim doesn't draw the pawn whose head the camera is in, so each draw notes the eyes (`NoteEyes`, from `DrawSkinned`) and their height per `Situation` (standing, seated, lying, crawling, swimming): drawn this frame or the last, as drawn (a photo, `ViewPose.FirstPersonOf`); else IsoRim's eye across the ground at the height last drawn for what it does now; else IsoRim's eye. `fpeye` autotest: a grown pawn, a child and a seated one.
