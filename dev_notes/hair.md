# Hair and beards

The mod ships no hair. Every hairstyle (and beard) is built in the game from whichever hair sprites the player has loaded (`Source/HairBuilder.cs`), so no other mod's art is copied into this one. **Raise `HairBuilder.Version` (beards: `BeardVersion`) when the build changes**, so cached hair is rebuilt. Re-check after changing the body or its keys (the cache is keyed by `skin.txt`'s hash).

`archive/hair.py` (`./archive/export_hair.sh`) is the older Blender version, for reference only and out of step. Its output is made from another mod's art, so it must not go in this mod (`Models/chibi/hair/` is gitignored and not read).

## Where styles come from

`SkinnedFigure.Hair` looks in order: `M3AThair/` at the root of any other active mod (hand-made or `hair.py` output), then the cache `<config>/M3AT/hair/<skin.txt hash>/` (other hashes' folders are deleted once a session in the background, `PruneCaches`). Anything else is built on a background thread the first time a pawn wears it; the pawn is bald until then. Built files start with `# source <hash>` of the sprites, so changed art is rebuilt; `.none` marks a style with nothing at alpha 0.5 (Shaved). `<style>` is the HairDef's texPath less `Things/Pawn/Humanlike/Hairs/` (so `Royalty/Anne` is in a subfolder).

Output: `<style>.txt` (rest pose, game model space, `n` normals, `t` texture places, body keys fitted from the surface under each vertex, no weights: it rides the head bone), `<style>.png` (south, east, north, west (clear without its own west sprite) and the crown panel side by side, `Panels`, `CrownPanel`), and `<style>.shell.txt`: the closed hull before see-through triangles were dropped (vertices on no hair laid `CLEAR` off the head, listed as `skin`), with keys and the body triangles it encloses (`hide`). The hair's outline is drawn from the shell (hair outline colour, `skin` vertices in the skin's), and `HideUnder(fig, mask, hairStyle)` leaves those body triangles out of the body's outline only.

## Building a style

From its three (or four) sprites, no hand modelling. Surface nets on 6 px blocks; the game's average head (`HeadSprites`, measured from its art) is the "drawn-over head". Steps, each with why:

- **Line up** (`LinedUp`, `LineUp*`): many mods draw hair a few px off-centre the same way in front and back, so mirrored the back is off by twice that; each is moved half of it toward the head's middle, measured over rows where both hug the head. The side sprite is moved back 2–4 px (`SideBack`) where its hair stops short of a back the back sprite covers. Harness: `LINEUP` lines; `M3AT_NO_LINEUP`.
- **Let out** (`Hugging`, `LetOut`): in head rows at least `HugRows` of full width, an outer hair edge within `HugGap` px inside the head's outline (and `HugShare` of the row) is moved `HugPast` past it (front and back sprites both edges, side at the back only), as hair drawn for a smaller head left skin strips at the back corners. The atlas uses the let-out sprites. `HUG`; `M3AT_NO_HUG`, `M3AT_HUG_GAP`.
- **Hull**: the hair's visual hull (side sprite with front and back together) joined to the drawn-over head's, each slice cut to a superellipse. (Filling the head into each outline left bald temples.)
  - From `UnderChin` (0.4 head heights) above the chin down, in front of the head's middle only where the front sprite has hair (else a beard-like band joined the locks).
  - Behind the middle only where the back sprite has hair, narrowing past its ends from the front's width to the back's (`Tapered`); that flank is the side sprite's alone (`flank`). `M3AT_NO_TAPER`.
  - `Carry`: hair a sprite shows over the head is carried out to just past the head's surface on that side, where within `FringeGap` px inside the head's hull and the surface faces within `FringeTurn` (front) / `SideTurn` (sides, back) of its outermost: hair drawn tight to a smaller head lay inside the hull and the whole fringe was dropped. What the front sprite carried (`fore`) is on its word alone: in `Textured` the side sprites get no say over its forward triangles (else holes through the fringe). `M3AT_NO_FRINGE`, `M3AT_FRINGE_GAP`/`_TURN`, `M3AT_SIDE_TURN`, `M3AT_NO_FORE`.
  - `CurtainOn`: a lock the front sprite shows beside the face, past the carrying, is hung as a sheet in front of the cheek (`CurtainSlope` voxels back per column out; side faces only where the side sprite has hair). `M3AT_NO_CURTAIN`, `M3AT_CURTAIN_SLOPE`.
  - The face is hollowed `FaceHollow` deep behind its front, so a lock beside the face has an inside face. A lock's back, toward the head, comes only from where the side sprite shows it clear of the head.
  - Own west sprite (`Sprites.west`, `TakeWest`, `WestDiffers`): a fourth view (index 3, read unmirrored, as hair mods draw it facing east); slices take the east outline before the middle column and the west from it on (`Slice`'s `deepWest`); `Side` gives each place its side's view (`ViewOf`, `SeenBy`, `OtherView`). Without one `px[3]` is `px[1]`. `WEST` line; `hair_sprites.py` writes `_west.rgba`.
- **Rows**: mapped in two pieces with the sprites' eye line (`EyeRow`, Facial Animation's) pinned to the chibi's (`eyes`) and each side stretched (`HeightOf`/`RowOf`, `EyePin`, `EyeMatch`; `M3AT_EYE_MATCH`), as one scale left every fringe high (the chibi's eyes are lower).
- **Mesh, smooth, keep clear of the head**, then `StretchForward` (fading out below the chin), as hair pulled onto the broad flat face followed its corners round the side: the fringe stretched `FaceStretch` forward; the sides slid forward round the head keeping their gap (`SlideAlong`, `SlideShare`, stopping where the head faces forward, `FaceTurn`), blended by how sideways the head faces (`SideFrom`..`SideFull`); moves smoothed first (`MoveSmooth`), else spikes.
- **Texture and cut**: each triangle takes the sprite facing it most, kept only where every sprite that sees it (`Sees`) has hair and within `HairReach` voxels of the hair's own hull. A sprite's veto is strict over the drawn-over head but `VetoSlack` px past its outline elsewhere (`AlphaAt`, `Vetoes`); a triangle on clear art or outline ink (`InkEdges`) goes to the other view if more of it is on art (`OtherView`). Islands under `IslandShare` of the kept area are dropped (`WithoutIslands`; `M3AT_NO_ISLANDS`).
- **Atlas**: fine strand streaks over each sprite (`StrandStreak`, `Streak`). The crown (`Crown` up), which no sprite sees, has its own panel seen from above (`Crowned`): each texel blends the sprites at its place, at the edge only the view its neighbours take (`ViewOf`), over `CrownBlend` the front and back by forwardness (`CrownMix`), grown `CrownGrow` texels; coloured from just under each sprite's top ink (`TopEdges`: first solid row lighter than `SkirtDark`, plus `Outline`).
- **Builder's walls** (under open edges, walked as loops): reach smoothed along the loop (`WallSmooth`), one colour per loop (`WallShade` up from its darkest), depth by how far below the scalp's edge the head is (`HairBuilder.Hairline`, `BelowHairline`, from `SkinnedFigure.Scalp`): full on the scalp, shortening over `HairlineEase` to `SkirtShort`, then `SkirtChin`, tapering over `FallFade` by `FallAway`, none where hair hangs free (below the chin, eased over `ChinBlend`; without a scalp a fixed `HairlineAt`). The file's `walls` line marks where they start. Mostly replaced in the game (below).

## Reading it in the game

- `SkinnedFigure.DrawHair` reads a style on first use (`LoadHair`): built hair and its shell (not a `M3AThair/` style) cut to `SettingsMod.HairDetail` of their triangles (`Source/Decimate.cs`: quadric edge collapses, each vertex keeping its texture place, normal and keys, open edges kept as lines, a seam copy only moved onto a neighbour's copy on the same sprite).
- Hairline walls (`Source/HairlineWalls.cs`, `SkinnedFigure.CutWithWalls`, shared with the harness; `HairlineWallsOn`): the hair cut without its walls (`WithoutWalls`), then one wall per style hung from the hairline (`HairlineWalled`). The line is the rig's `Hairline` curve (`skin.txt`'s `hairline` line, laid back on the skin, `DrawnLine`; see below), else the `Scalp` group's edge.
  - A column every `WallStep` (0.03 cells; finer was a quarter of the triangles, coarser lost walls) along it (`AlongLoop`), straight out along the skin and tilted up `WallTilt` until it meets the cut hair within `WallReach`; its top takes that hair triangle's texture place and keys, its foot the body's. Never looking down.
  - Where no hair is over the line, the foot walks back over the skin toward the crown in `WalkStride` steps (at most `WalkSteps`) until there is.
  - Neighbouring tops join within `WallJump`, or up to `WallJumpMost` if the line between stays within `WallOnHair` of the hair or `WallSagShare` of its length (`Joins`); else left open (a gap between locks). `M3AT_FIXED_SAG`.
  - One face each, facing away from the crown, lit as its columns face; corners shared unless texture places differ. Each column's texture place is snapped `WallInset` inside the art (`WallAtlas.Snap`, from the image's alpha, so `LoadHair` reads the image first), blended across a quad only within `WallBlendPixels` with solid art between (`SolidBetween`), else flat. `WallOneColour` uses the file's `wall_uv` instead.
  - A style covering under `HairlineCover` of the line within `CoverWalk` steps (a mohawk) keeps the builder's walls, cut by `SkinnedFigure.Decimated` (only the hair before the `walls` line) and re-hung from the cut edges (`Rehang`). `M3AT_EDGE_WALLS` keeps edge walls in the harness.
- Drawn with `MarusSmoothTex` (`MarusSmooth.frag.glsl` with `USE_TEXTURE`, alpha cut at 0.5; `MarusSmoothTex.vert.glsl` is a copy of IsoRim's `IsoRimTex.vert.glsl` plus the normal). Its night light pass is its own `MarusSmoothTexMark` (`IsoRimLitMarkTex`'s render state as a third part, set up unlit by `ConfigureFigureMaterial` then given the contract's model curve and own-cell light), as IsoRim's missed triangles and the hair changed colour at night. Outlines: `dev_notes/skinning.md`.
- Scalp: the rig's `Scalp` group (`skin.txt`'s `scalp` line, `SCALP_GROUP`; without one, head vertices above a fixed line raised `ScalpForeheadRaise` over the forehead, `ScalpEdgeDrop`) is drawn in the hair colour shaded `ScalpShade`, and the face patch leaves it out, so gaps show hair-coloured scalp.
- Hats cut or push the hair: `dev_notes/skinning.md`.

## The hairline curve

`anim_scripts/make_hairline.py` (Run Script in the open session, or `blender -b Models/pawnHumanRig.blend --python anim_scripts/make_hairline.py -- <out.blend>`) draws the rig's `Hairline` freehand from `LINE` (height share from the chin at angles round the head), laid on the `Head` by rays from its upright axis. `anim_scripts/hairline_to_mesh.py` (same ways) turns it into a mesh loop for X-mirror editing (`MIRROR_FROM` `'+x'`/`'-x'` rebuilds the other half). `export_frames.py` writes it (`hairline_points`).

## Tools outside the game

- `anim_scripts/hairbuild/run.sh <out folder> [--beard] [--detail share] <style> ...`: `anim_scripts/hair_sprites.py` finds the sprites as the game would (active mods' Textures, last loaded first, by `ModsConfig.xml`, then Assets.zip) and resamples them as IsoRim's `SpritePixels.Sample`; the harness (`HairBuild.csproj`, `Main.cs`, run from `~/.cache/m3at-hairbuild/run`) reads the chibi with `SkinnedFigure`'s reader, builds with `HairBuilder.Build` and cuts as `LoadHair` (`--detail` 0.3; uncut kept as `.full.txt`). Matches the game's cache to within a few pixels of the alpha cut.
- `./view_hair.sh [--cached] [--beard] [--detail share] [--full] [--keep folder] <style or style.txt> ...` (`anim_scripts/view_hair.py`): builds the styles (or takes them from the game's newest cache with `--cached`) and puts them on a throwaway copy of the rig with the pose preview: parented to `walk.HEAD` from rest, scaled by skin.txt's height over the rig's, tinted `COLOUR`, body keys driving the hair's, shell hidden. It opens at Rest Position so walls line up with the unparented `Hairline`.

## Race hair (`Source/AddonHair.cs`, part of `HairBuilder`; HAR by reflection)

- A race such as Kiiro gives pawns a blank hairstyle (`Kiiro_Null`) and draws hair as head add-ons (HAR's `AlienComp.addonGraphics`). For a race with hair add-ons (aligned with the head, `hair` colour channel, "hair" in the path; under `AddonLeast` of the head's area left out) `HairOf` (from `DrawDressed`) gives a stand-in `HairDef` (never in the DefDatabase), texPath `M3AT_HAR/<race>/<add-ons>`. `Read` takes sprites from `Combined`: per view the add-ons behind the body (cut by the race's head sprite, left out of the front view, where they made a collar), the pawn's hairstyle, then those in front, each graphic once, then mapped so the race's head box lands on the human head (`FitToHuman`, across and down apart). Not yet: an add-on's own draw size and offsets.
- A race's ordinary hairstyle is fitted the same way (`RaceFitted`, `M3AT_HAR/<race>/<head>/<style>`) when its head sprite's box is more than `RaceHeadSlack` off the human one in any view (`HeadLikeHuman`).

## Beards

- `SkinnedFigure.DrawBeard` (`WalkFigures.BeardOf`, after the hair in `DrawDressed`) gets them from a second `HairBuilder` (`beard` set, `SkinnedFigure.Beard`, `Styled` shared), into `beards/<style>.txt`/`.png` in the hair cache or a `M3AThair/` folder (`<style>`: texPath less `Things/Pawn/Humanlike/Beards/`). Ride the head bone, take the keys. `.none` for nothing at alpha 0.5.
- Differences from hair: the north sprite is ignored; fitted to `Male_Average_Normal` first; rows by eyes, mouth and chin (`HeightOf`/`RowOf`: `EyeRow` and `MouthRow` to Facial Animation's on the face patch, `FaceRows`, with `FaceLayers.FaceRaise`/`MouthRaise`, the head's last row to the chin, then the head's scale below so long ones keep length); widened from none at the mouth to `WidenEyes` (1.5) at the eyes and above (`Widen`, `AcrossAt`; 3D only), as FA's eyes reach nearly to the face's sides; each sprite projected only on triangles facing it (`Prisms` for `NearHair`, `BeardAlpha`; the front out to `BeardFront`, the side no further round than `FrontWidths`), as a side sprite's moustache is a blob; no crown; depth from none at the eyes to all low on the jaw (`DepthShare`, `BeardDeep`) plus `BeardPuff` on art, kept `ChestClear` off the chest; one wall colour; no shell (outlined by its own mesh), hides nothing. `LoadHair` keeps at least `BeardTriangles`.
- Left off for a baby, with no head, under what hides the face (`HidesFace`), or apparel `SkinnedFigure.HidesBeard` names (the game's `Beard` render skip flag, or covering `FullHead` or `Mouth`).
- Modes (`Settings.beardMode`: `Flat` default, `ThreeD`, `Off`; `WalkFigures.FlatBeards`/`Beards`). Flat: `SkinnedFigure.DrawFlatBeard` lays `FaceLayers.DrawFlat` over `Posed.face` at queue 2998 (over face layers and ridges, under wounds; `MarusFaceLayer` mode 1, hair-tinted), its texture from `HairBuilder.FlatFace` (512 px in the patch's UV layout, each texel mapped back to the sprite pixel the 3D beard would use; chin = the exporter's lowest head vertex; cleared where the ears are front-most, `ClearEars`, the triangles `No_Human_Ears` moves). Needs no FA, front only, cut off at the chin. The harness's `--beard` writes it as `<style>.flat.png`. Each texel reads the sprite sharper (`HairBuilder.SharpAt`, `FlatSharp`), as `FaceArt` sharpens the face art on screen but once at build: alpha as a cubic B-spline of the 256 px sprite, stretched about half its local peak so the edge is about a texel wide (`FlatEdge`, with `FlatSoft` 2 as the 128 px art is read at 256), colour the nearest pixel's so the strand streaks stay crisp; then `BleedColour` gives clear texels the art's colour nearby. Taking the nearest pixel, as before, drew the outline as a staircase of the art's pixels, which broke into dashes from a few cells off. Not mipmapped: in the game the mipmapped copy came out blurrier and blockier close up whatever `mipMapBias`, and no better further off (Nov 2026, A/B stills of nearest/sharp × mips at 1, 3 and 7 cells). The harness's `M3AT_FLAT_NEAREST` builds it as before, `M3AT_FLAT_SOFT` sets `FlatSoft`. The face shaders' `sharpFaceArt` hardly touches it: at 512 px its texels are below a screen pixel except in freecam or the first-person view.
- Checked on "Vanilla Hairs and Beards Retextured" sprites, as vanilla's are packed.
