# Postures and activities

What the skinned chibi does when. The clips themselves are made in `walk.py` (`dev_notes/clips.md`); how they are layered (`Over`, `Top`, `Nudge`, `Override`): `dev_notes/skinning.md`. Weapons and fighting: `dev_notes/weapons.md`. Everything here is the skinned figure only; most activities are recognised by the job's defName so other mods (CE, Romance on the Rim, ...) aren't referenced.

## What is drawn plainly or left to IsoRim

- IsoRim draws statues itself. People gone to bones are the chibi's skeleton (`dev_notes/body.md`), or IsoRim's with `skeletons` off. The old baked (non-skinned) frames do only standing and walking, leaving the rest to IsoRim.
- Drawn plainly (`WalkFigures.DrawPlain`: the idle, sit or lie at IsoRim's placing): what the rules don't cover, such as people floating upright in a vat, pawns not spawned that IsoRim still draws (`PlainOnly`), and pawns another mod places on a host (`Api.AttachedPlace`, by anchor and `AttachPose`: the sit's seat on it, the lie's back on it, a building's `SitNoLegs` sunk `HotTubSink`). Also any figure whose drawing threw (`DrawFailed`, logged once per place in the code, as IsoRim switches off a drawer that throws).
- Gibbet cages: corpses are drawn slumped where they stand (`WalkFigures.DrawCaged`: `walk.caged_pose`, the `caged` clip, `CAGED_` numbers; an old export mixes the idle `GibbetSlump` of the way to the ragdoll pose), no taller than IsoRim's shrunk figure. `gibbet` autotest.
- Growth vats and biosculpter pods: a person on the couch is kept inside the glass (`WalkFigures.UnderGlass`: the glass as an elliptic tube per def, `Glasses`, measured from IsoRim's glass models; let down only as far as its top needs, at most `GlassMostDown`, and slid toward its feet if its head passes the glass's end), with no baby romper and no clothes unless `vatClothes` (off; `WalkFigures.VatClothes`; `InVat` in `WornGarments` and `BeardOf`). `vat` autotest.

## Dubs Bad Hygiene (`Source/Hygiene.cs`)

The IsoRim pack for DBH (`james.isorimwaylith.dbhmodels`, Workshop 3827392706) places pawns on toilets, in baths, hot tubs and pools by attaching them.
- Washing (DBH's `WashingJobDef` with its `Washing` hediff) is drawn with nothing on, as DBH's sprite (`SleepWearOf`).
- Lying in a bath or hot tub (`InTub`, the anchor being the water's surface): sat back `SoakRecline` more, slid `SoakAlong` toward the feet, let down `SoakDepth` under the water, the head out.
- A swimmer attached in a pool (`PoolOf`: a swimming job, a `SitNoLegs` attachment on a building whose class is named a pool) is drawn through `DrawSkinned` with its own stride, heading and pace (`PoolStroke`, as IsoRim steps none for an attached figure). On the move it swims (the `swim` clip, weighed by `PoolStroke`'s pace, `ease.poolGait`), the neck `PoolFaceClear` over the water (its anchor plus `PoolWaterOverAnchor`); still it crouches in to the shoulders (`PoolCrouch`: `SkinnedFigure.Crouch` by the drop from its shoulders, `PoolShoulder`, to the water, past the crouch's full depth if need be, up to `CrouchMost`; eased out as it sets off, by `ease.poolGait`; `LetDown` never bends a knee back, as past the full crouch the hip-to-ankle line tips forward and a thigh still pointing down read as pointing back, folding the legs round behind). Anyone else in the pool stands, as below. Any pawn in a raised pool (that or `InRaisedPool`) wades on its floor (`wade` whole, `PoolFloor`, the pack's 0.091 cells; wall 0.8, water 0.7, about chest deep on the chibi), without sinking into the water terrain DBH lays under it (floated with the neck at the water line, the body went through the floor). Drawn with nothing on while in (`InPool` in `SleepWearOf`).
- Vaulting the wall: pools IsoRim draws as a model (`RaisedPool`: a building whose class is named a pool, `Api.ModelStandsIn`) are `PassThroughOnly` at no path cost, so pawns cut across them. Anyone walked over the wall vaults it (`PoolVault`, after `PoolClimb`; on its feet, moving, not mid-climb): `pool_vault` (`walk.vault_at`, `VAULT`: crouch, hands on the rim, a tucked hop and landing, its ends blended from ground to pool floor) played by where it is across the stretch from `VAULT_REACH` behind the wall's middle to as far past (`VaultReach`, `WallMiddle` inside the edge), facing the way it goes, so it never falls behind.
- Climbing (`PoolClimb`, from `DrawFigure` first; `Hygiene.ClimbSwimmers` off, as it was slow beside the game's walk over the wall, but `PoolClimb` still tracks crossings for `inPool`): `pool_climb` (`walk.climb_at`, `CLIMB` keyed as `DRESS` by `walk.keyed_at`, hands on the rim by the `floor` landmark; `CLIMB_WALL`; `CLIMB_FLOOR`: springs up with a knee on the rim, kneels, sits on the edge and slides off) on its own clock from the crossing, forward out and backwards in, facing out, from `ClimbOut` outside the edge; in only once DBH has made it a swimmer (`Washing`), out on any crossing out while in; then eased to the game's place over `ClimbBackTicks` (`ClimbBack`). Tracked from the swim job's target pool (`Climbing`).
- Preview items: `Climbing out of a pool`, `Vaulting a pool wall`. Autotests `hygiene`, `hygiene_vault`.

## Postures

- Follow IsoRim's placing (`FigurePlacement`): `Situation` (`Seated`, `InBed`, `OnCouch`, `Crawling`, lying), `Position` (seat, bed or bunk slot, couch with the platform's sway, a corpse's shelf, plus `Lift`), `Yaw`, `ChestLift`; how far onto a seat, the game's own shift (`SeatAmount`).
- Lying and crawling clips are laid down by a whole-body matrix passed to `SkinnedFigure.End(p, whole)` (premultiplied into every bone), then rested on the lowest point (`Extent`); in a bed on the placing's height, on top of the blanket even where the game shows only the head (IsoRim's bedding is too thin to hide the body).
- Seated: `sit` blended in by `seatAmount`, resting `SeatOf` (lowest vertex no further forward than the hips) on the seat.
- In a bed each lying clip is moved along it so its body's middle, laid and reclined, is where `sleep`'s is (`SkinnedFigure.LaidMiddleOf`, `BedMiddleClip`).
- Corpses: `lie` frame 0 (or `dead`) and the rotten colour (`SkinOf`).
- Lying on its back, the chibi's big head reaches further back than its back, so the trunk is bent forward over the hips (`SkinnedFigure.Recline`, `Bend`, in `Chain`, thighs turned back) by `ReclineOf` (per shape and clip: the least bend, at most `ReclineMost`, bringing the trunk down to the head; none for one already down on its side), less as a fall eases in.
- Lying on the ground in water (downed, dead, fallen, a ragdoll) it floats: `floor` lowered `FloatSink` (0.5) of the body's thickness as it lies (`Extent`'s `bodyTop` less `lowest`), at most the water's depth (`Ease.depth`).

### Lying loops (`LieClip`)

- Corpse `dead`. In a bed: `sleep` asleep (downed or not), `lie` awake. Downed and unconscious (`!Awake()`: anaesthetic, catatonia, psychic shock) `sleep` anywhere (a variant only off a couch). Downed awake: `downed` on a couch, `downed_side` on the ground, but on its back once someone kneels to tend or feed it there (`Ease.onBack`, set in `WorkOf`, kept until it stands; `sleep` asleep, `downed` awake), as curled on its side the doctor knelt over its head; a ragdoll is eased out into that over `TendRollSeconds` (`dollOut`). Else `lie`.
- Resting awake in a lie-down job (`JobDriver_LayDown`: LayDown, LayDownAwake, LayDownResting) is drawn as asleep, the eyes left to FA (closed only for LayDown). Asleep, one of `sleep`'s variants by the job's `loadID` and the pawn's id, on the back while tended (`OnBack`).
- A baby (always `Downed`, `alwaysDowned`): `sleep` asleep or resting, else `baby` (waving and kicking), unless in pain shock.
- A change of loop blends over `LieTurnSeconds` (`LieTurnOf`).

### Falling, getting up, getting into bed

- Falling: a pawn drawn on its feet (`upFrame`: standing, not seated, swimming, in the air or carried) next drawn lying on the ground, downed or dead, falls (`FallOf`: `knockdown` or `die` variants, `FallHurry` times faster, game time, blended into the loop from `FallOut`), its hips moved from over its place to the loop's, turned by `FallFacing` (`SkinnedFigure.FacingFrom`) so it starts facing the way it stood (`upYaw`), and lies on that yaw (`fellYaw`, corpses too). With ragdolls on, it falls limp instead (below).
- Getting up: a pawn drawn lying on its back (not crawling or dead) next drawn standing plays `getup` once (`RiseOf`, `RiseHurry` times its length, game time, as the game stands it up at once), whole in place of the rest (`land` scaled down by it), eased to standing from `RiseOut`; hips from where they lay (`lieAlong`) back to its place, turning from the lying yaw (`lieYaw`), resting on its lowest point off the bed (`lieFloor`). From a ragdoll, eased out of its pose over `RiseFromDoll`.
- Into bed: the getup backwards (`BedDownOf`, `BedDownHurry`, `Ease.bedded`): a pawn on its feet within two frames (`upFrame`, `upAt`) now lying in a bed, not downed, carried or dead, is drawn on the standing path from where it stood (`bedFromYaw`) to the lying branch's place, yaw and bed top, up onto the bed by the getup's middle; then the lying loop (and blanket). `bedin` autotest.

### Ragdolls (`Source/Ragdoll.cs`, part of `SkinnedFigure`; `ragdolls`, on)

- A pawn that falls where it stood falls limp. Drawing only, unless flung: the hips are held near its cell (`HipsFree`, `HipsHeld`).
- Twenty joints (`JointBones`: hips, belly, neck, head middle, each limb's four), each with a weight (`JointMass`) and size (`Radii`, per shape), held by the bones between (`Links`, pelvis and chest braced), Verlet-stepped on game time (`StepSeconds`: `Time.deltaTime` × game speed; interpolated from `before`).
- Tone: springs between joints a bone apart at the ragdoll pose's lengths (`ToneLinks`, `DownedTone`, `DeadTone`); a pull toward a place worked out from the joints themselves put energy in and walked the body. Limits (`Drives`: cones about the ragdoll pose; elbows and knees as hinges limited in bend and twist from the pose's plane, `ElbowTwist`/`KneeTwist`, and the shoulder's and hip's roll, else knees rolled out flat) slow near the edge (`SoftMargin`, `SoftDamp`) and stop there (`Stop`: the movement into the limit taken out and moved with where it was a step ago). Limbs kept out of the trunk and each other the same way (`Apart`).
- Ground at 0 with friction (`Friction`, `StaticSlip`); walls and furniture nearby as boxes (`Surroundings`: impassable buildings, else `fillPercent` from `BlockFill` up to `Api.SurfaceHeight`; the map edge a wall).
- The fall: joints held to the canned fall (`Pin`, `HoldTo`), let go from the upper body down, then the legs (`UpperLetGo`, `LegsLetGo`), sooner the harder the blow (`dollHit`) and the more it goes against the canned fall (`dollHeld`, `SkinnedFigure.FallAgrees`). The blow (`Blown`, a postfix beside `Struck` that records even a killing blow, `blows`; from `dinfo.Angle` and `Amount`, `PushLeast`..`PushMost`, `PushBlast`) throws the trunk, the shoulders more than the hips and the feet back, once the upper body is let go (`pending`). A body lying already is thrown again (`PushLying`). Slowed from `CalmFrom` to `CalmTo` after the last throw; at rest when nothing moves faster than `SettleSpeed` for `SettleSteps` (at most `LongestSteps`).
- Bone turns rebuilt from the joints (`Ragdoll.Frames`, `Turns`: pelvis and chest from three joints, head from neck and chest, a bent limb from its bend's plane, else the least turn from the group above, hands and feet the least turn from the lower bone; on top of the ragdoll pose's turns) put in place of the pose by `SkinnedFigure.Override` between `Chain` and `Finish`, the breathing (`lie`'s, `Nudge`) on top while alive. Drawn at its own place and yaw (`Origin`, `Yaw`), rested on its lowest point once at rest.
- Dropped when the body leaves the ground or its cell (`Cell`) or crawls. One at rest is saved with the map (`Source/RagdollRests.cs`, a `MapComponent`: joints, cell, yaw, place by pawn id), so it lies as it fell after loading (`SavedDoll`).
- With ragdolls off a fall plays its own clip.

### Flings (`Source/Flings.cs`, a `MapComponent`; `flingBodies`, `flingWeapons`, on, ragdolls on too)

The first things this mod moves in the game rather than only draws, so they run on game ticks with no real time, drawing or random numbers, the same for every player, and are saved.
- Bodies: a blow leaving a humanlike downed or dead (`Blown`) throws it if a blast from `FlingBlastFrom` damage, a shot (`isRanged`) from `FlingShotFrom`, or any other blow with a way (`dinfo.Angle`) from `FlingMeleeFrom` (`FlingSpeedOf`: the damage plus that of the same kind within `FlingSameWay` of the same way in the last `FlingGatherTicks`, `GatheredDamage`, so a shotgun's pellets count as one; `FlingBlastLeast`..`Most`, `FlingShotLeast`..`Most`, `FlingMeleeLeast`..`Most`, by body size within `FlingHeaviest`..`FlingLightest`, up at `FlingUp`; times `flingPower`). In flight a body is one point (`Flings.FlyBody`: `Gravity`; a wall cell, deep water, a closed door or the map edge stops it `BodyRadius` off, `WallBounce`, `BodyBounce`, `BodySlide`); the pawn or corpse's `Position` follows cell by cell (`TickBody`, `Notify_Teleported`, its job kept), not in a bed.
- The ragdoll follows on the drawing side: `WalkFigures.Flung` moves its `Cell` (its `Surroundings` come again), the hips held to the point (`Follow`, `Ragdoll.Following`), then to the middle of the landing cell (`FlingSettle`), `Origin` and `Yaw` kept; left behind off screen by `FlingLost` it is dropped; a pawn already lying gets one; a new one takes the flight's speed (`Push`'s `up`) and from `FlingLetGo` isn't held to the canned fall; a throw slower than the body's flight is ignored (`Flings.Throw`).
- Weapons: a prefix and postfix on `Pawn.DropAndForbidEverything` take the `Primary` a pawn dropped going down (`Flings.TakeWeapon`), a tick later so the killing blow is known (`BlowOf`); it flies (`Launch`, by a hash of its id and the tick; `Fly`, `ItemBounce`, `ItemKeeps`, `ItemStops`), staying spawned on its cell but hidden (`Flings.Hidden`: a first prefix on `SectionLayer_ThingsGeneral.TakePrintFrom` and on `Thing.DynamicDrawPhaseAt`), the component saving only a reference (a save loaded without the mod has it lying there); picked up meanwhile, the flight ends; `GenPlace.TryPlaceThing` moves it where it lands. Drawn in `MapComponentUpdate` by `WalkFigures.DrawFlying` (`DrawSlab`) from the hand as last drawn (`HeldLast`, `Ease.held*`), tumbling, laid flat as it lands.
- `Flings.Hide` also hides apparel being put on (`dev_notes/apparel.md`).

## On the move

- Gait: `dev_notes/skinning.md` and the root CLAUDE.md (stride by ground covered, walk-to-run by speed).
- Limping (`LimpOf`, every `LimpCheck` ticks; `injuryAnims`): compares the legs (`MovingLimbCore` parts with their `MovingLimbSegment` feet, `PawnCapacityUtility.CalculatePartEfficiency`, missing 0, capped at 1; sides by `untranslatedCustomLabel`) and blends `limp_<side>` in place of the gait from `LimpFrom` to `LimpFull` of a difference. Exactly one leg gone (not a prosthetic; a missing foot still limps): `hop_<side>` fully, even with injury animations off (`favouring`, `hopGround`).
- Balance on one leg: whatever else it does on its feet, the hips are moved over the other foot (`SkinnedFigure.Balance`, `OverFoot`, in `Chain`: `BalanceShare` sideways, that foot left where the clip put it and the leg re-bent, the hips let down if it can't reach; `BalanceEase`; not seated, lying, crawling, swimming, in the air, getting up, meditating or at `research_floor`; a clip already there, the hop, left alone).
- Water (`WaterOf`): the depth at its cell (none on a bridge; `Marsh` for water IsoRim's `Ground.IsWater` draws as ground; else `Shallow`, or `ChestDeep` for passable water named deep), eased on game time. `wade` takes the stride's place up to `Shallow`; the figure sinks by it (IsoRim's water sheet at `WaterLine` is opaque), the neck kept `HeadClear` out.
- Swimming (`pawn.Swimming`: GoSwimming on a water cell): `swim` on the move, laid down like the crawl but raised `SwimLift`, `tread` when still (over `SwimTurn` game seconds, `StrokeOf`), the neck joint (`NeckBone`) at `WaterLine`; drafted and carry overlays and the bend drop out.
- Jumping: a pawn in a `PawnFlyer` (jump packs, jump abilities, the stun throw) plays `jump` by how far through the flight it is (`FlightOf`: `ticksFlying / ticksFlightTime` by reflection, by time), not wrapping, sprung from its crouch over the first `TakeOff`; IsoRim's placing gives the height (`Lift`). On the ground `CrouchOf` blends `land` in place of the standing pose (still only) over the warm-up of a `Verb_Jump` or `Verb_CastAbilityJump` (`Stance_Warmup`) and on landing (over `LandDown` of `LAND_SECONDS`, eased out).
- Turning on the spot: standing, the figure is drawn at its feet's yaw (`StepOf`, `Ease.footYaw`) and the trunk turned to the tracker's yaw (aim, foe, work) by `SkinnedFigure.Twist` (over `spine.001`..`spine.003` about the vertical, in `Place`; arms, head and weapon go with it). The feet stay until the twist passes `StepSlack` or the trunk has held its way `StepSettle`, then step round at `StepSpeed` (`StepTurn` degrees a step, `StepShare` of the legs) to within `StepDone`; past `TwistMost` they swing with it. Moving, they come round to the heading. Aiming: `dev_notes/weapons.md`.
- Head turns: `SkinnedFigure.Look` (neck and head, `LookBones`/`LookShares`, about the vertical in `Place`, after the twist; at most `LookMost`, eased at `LookSpeed`, `Ease.look`), set by `LessonLookOf` (teaching, glances, reading together, listening) and `chatFace`. `SkinnedFigure.Nod` tips it (kisses, weddings). `SkinnedFigure.Lean` leans sideways (shared reading).

## Hits, casting, stagger and stun

- Casting (`CastOf`): while a `Verb_CastAbility` (not a jump) warms up, `cast`, or `cast_touch` for `Verb_CastAbilityTouch`, eased by `CastEase`, facing the target (`FaceOf`, shared with `StrikeOf`).
- Flinch: a hit (Harmony postfixes on `Pawn_DrawTracker.Notify_DamageApplied` and `Notify_DamageDeflected`, `Struck`; `hits` by pawn) plays by `FlinchOf` over `FlinchTicks` with `SkinnedFigure.Nudge` (each bone's turn from the clip's first frame, slerped by weight, applied after `Over` and `Top`, so on any pose, lying too). The four ways are weighted by `dinfo.Angle` against the yaw (none: from the front), from `FlinchLeast` to full at `FlinchFull` damage; deflected `FlinchDeflected` of that; a missed melee attack (`MeleePostfix` on `Verb_MeleeAttack.TryCastShot`) `FlinchMissed` of the least.
- Stagger: `reel` (or `reel~2`) while `stances.stagger.Staggered` with no hit behind it, once through by `StaggerTicksLeft` against the most seen (`ReelOf`; lengthened part way, the rest over the new time, held no further than `ReelHold`), whole standing still, from the waist up moving (`ReelEase`). Off for now (`StaggerReels` false): the game staggers every pawn hit for `HitStagger` ticks, which shows as the flinch (`StaggeredByHit`).
- Stun: `daze` while `stances.stunner.Stunned` (`DazeEase`), in place of the idle, hunker and drafted stance standing still.
- These three are laid on with `SkinnedFigure.Top` (over what `Over` blended, which averages by weight), the daze over the other two.
- Variants (`SkinnedFigure.Variant(clip, roll)`): a one-shot event keeps the variant it started with, hashed with the pawn's id (`Gen.HashCombineInt`): a bend (`bentRoll`), a landing (`landedRoll`), a getup (`risenRoll`), a jump (the flyer's id), a flinch (the hit's tick), a melee blow (`LastShotTick`), a fall (the tick it fell, `fallClip`: any of `walk.FALL_CLIPS`), a bout of work (`workRoll`; the variant's own `tool` line, `toolKind`).

## Work (`WorkOf`)

- Only once at the work (`WorkReach`), not moving, drafted or not, with IsoRim's work animations on (some activities below go whatever IsoRim's setting). The figure faces the work (Track's facing) and `work_<kind>` is added in place of the idle, eased by `WorkEase`, on the clock (`LoopPhase`). Standing only, but the `SeatedWork` kinds (`work_research`, `work_bench_sit`, `work_chair`, `work_hammer_bench_sit`, `meditate_sit`, `stir_campfire_sit`, the `_sit` instruments) are laid on the sit with `Over`. Each falls back to the old tool kind on an export without its clip. The hurt hold gives way to a tool.
- Tool: `SkinnedFigure.ToolOf` (the `tool` line) drawn past `ToolShown` with `Api.DrawModel`, as `Scale(size) * BoneOf(hand.R) * place`; this mod's own tools (`Models/tool_*.obj`) are looked for before IsoRim's (`WalkFigures.ToolModel`).
- Kinds (as IsoRim's `Work.cs`, plus jobs of the same motion: `RepairMech`, `DeconstructForBlueprint`, `RemoveFoundation`, `ClearPollution`, `ClearSnow`, `UseCommsConsole`, `AnalyzeItem`, `Hack`):
