[h1]M3AT: Maru's 3D Animated Toons[/h1]

[i]Animated 3D pawns for IsoRim.[/i]

M3AT is an add-on for [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3819577715]IsoRim[/url]. IsoRim shows your colony in 3D. M3AT replaces its people with fully animated 3D toons: they walk, run, sit, sleep, fight, work, eat, read, play music, carry each other about, limp when hurt and fall when they're shot.

[b]This is an early version.[/b] A lot already works, but you will find rough edges. Bug reports are very welcome (see below).

[hr][/hr]

[h2]Features[/h2]

[h3]Animation[/h3]
[list]
[*]Smooth walking and running that keeps pace with how fast the pawn is really moving, so feet don't slide
[*]Sitting, lying in bed, sleeping, crawling, wading and swimming
[*]Work animations: mining, chopping, building, crafting at a bench, planting, harvesting, fishing, research, tending patients, taming, firefighting and more
[*]Eating at a table or on their feet, reading books, playing the piano, harp and drum, teaching, horseshoes and hoopstone
[*]Carrying items, babies and people (wounded colonists are carried in the arms or over the shoulder)
[*]Chatting at parties, rubbing their hands in the cold, getting up off the floor, jumping with jump packs
[/list]

[h3]Combat[/h3]
[list]
[*]Each weapon type has its own drafted stance, and weapons are held properly in the hands using IsoRim's 3D weapon models or slabs
[*]Aiming, recoil, bow drawing, melee swings, punches and grenade throws
[*]Weapons not in use are worn on the body (back, hips, thighs) and drawn and put away with an animation
[*]Flinching when hit, staggering, being stunned, muzzle flashes and a red flash on a hit
[*]Ragdolls: downed and killed pawns fall limp where they stood
[*]Optional: blows and explosions can throw bodies and dropped weapons across the map (off by default, as this changes where things end up)
[/list]

[h3]Looks[/h3]
[list]
[*]3D clothing, armour and headgear for every piece of apparel in the base game, all DLCs and Combat Extended, coloured by what it's made of
[*]Hair and beards built in 3D from your hair textures, including hair mods you have installed. Nothing has to be made by hand for each style
[*]Faces animated by [NL] Facial Animation: blinking and expressions
[*]Body types, plus optional varied builds and heights so not every colonist looks the same
[*]Children grow gradually from babies to adults, with changing proportions
[*]Visible wounds, bandages and scars, and missing limbs
[*]Pawns limp on an injured leg, hop when a leg is gone, and hold a wounded arm, chest or head
[*]Genes: Modeled and animated cat, pig and floppy ears, tails, horns, fur, facial ridges, pig noses and more
[*]Pregnancy shows
[/list]

[h3]And also[/h3]
[list]
[*][b]Freecam[/b] (F6): fly a free camera through your colony with the mouse and keyboard, and still select pawns and give orders
[*]Packs, belts and gear from IsoRim are shown on the body
[*]Plenty of options: pawn and head size, beards, outlines, shoes, ears under hats, and turning any feature off
[*]Runs on the graphics card to improve performance
[/list]

[h2]Other mods[/h2]
Most mods work without anything special. Here's what to expect from content M3AT doesn't know about:
[list]
[*][b]Hair and beard mods[/b]: work fully. Every style is built in 3D from its textures automatically
[*][b]Weapon mods[/b]: weapons are held as a flat 3D cut-out of their picture (or IsoRim's 3D model, if it has one) and use the general stance and animations for their type: rifle, pistol, bow or melee
[*][b]Clothing mods[/b]: shown as the most similar garment M3AT has, in the right colour. Their hats and masks aren't shown yet
[*][b]Alien race mods[/b]: their pawns are shown as ordinary human pawns
[/list]

[h2]Known limitations[/h2]
[list]
[*]Statues, people in growth vats and bodies in gibbet cages are currently drawn by IsoRim as usual
[*]Some animations are still quite basic and will be improved
[/list]

[h2]Reporting a bug[/h2]
Something looks wrong? Select the pawn it's happening to, open the mod's options and press [b]Copy bug report[/b]. This copies the details I need (your settings, mod list, any errors and what that pawn is doing) to your clipboard, ready to paste in a comment, a discussion or the [b]#bug-reports[/b] channel on the Discord. Nothing is sent anywhere by itself. A screenshot helps a lot too.
